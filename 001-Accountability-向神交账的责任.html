ACCOUNTABILITY TO GOD ｜ 向神交账的责任

标题 / Title

ENACCOUNTABILITY TO GOD; Responsibility, Individual and Personal; Judgment according to Life-Work.

语法纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语）
标题、词条、纲要常用『名词短语＋分号/逗号并列』的无谓语句式：ACCOUNTABILITY TO GOD; Responsibility, Individual and Personal; Judgment according to Life-Work. 三个并列的名词短语从不同角度点出主题，没有谓语动词。理解方法：把每个分号段当作一个独立的名词短语，找出中心名词（Accountability / Responsibility / Judgment），其余成分都是修饰语。本书每个词条的标题和小节标题都采用这种文体。
本句：三个名词短语以分号并列，全句无谓语动词：Accountability to God／Responsibility...／Judgment...，从三个角度点出本词条主题。
语法形容词后置（名词, 形容词 and 形容词）
标题和正式文体中，形容词可以放在名词之后（常用逗号隔开）以突出中心词：Responsibility, Individual and Personal = Individual and Personal Responsibility（个人的、切身的责任）；Angels, Holy and Fallen = 圣洁的与堕落的天使。经文中也常见：things eternal（永恒之事）。翻译时把后置形容词移回名词前即可。
本句：Responsibility, Individual and Personal = Individual and Personal Responsibility：形容词后置，突出中心词 Responsibility。
语法Judgment according to Life-Work：according to 引导的介词短语作后置定语，修饰 Judgment——『照一生行为（所作之工）的审判』。类似结构：Judgment according to his deeds。

中向神交账的责任；个人性、切身的责任；照一生行为受审判。

正文对照 / Text

ENThe sense of accountability, the inward conscious conviction of personal responsibility, is an inherent product of the conscience or moral judgment, that exists and spontaneously acts in every man by nature.

语法同位语结构
两个名词短语并列，后者对前者作解释说明，中间用逗号隔开：The sense of accountability, the inward conscious conviction of personal responsibility, is...（交账的意识，即内心对个人责任的自觉确信，是……）。长句中先识别出同位语，把它当作插入的解释成分，主干就清晰了。19 世纪论说文大量使用这种结构。
本句：the inward conscious conviction of personal responsibility 是主语 The sense of accountability 的同位语；that exists and spontaneously acts... 是修饰 the conscience or moral judgment 的定语从句。全句主干：The sense is an inherent product。

中交账的意识，即内心对个人责任的自觉确信，是良心（道德判断力）与生俱来的产物；这种良心天然存在于每个人里面，并自发地运行。

ENThe recognition by the moral judgment of right and wrong, good and evil, is necessarily accompanied by the moral feeling consequent upon the willing or doing the right or the wrong.

语法the + 动名词的名词化结构
定冠词 the + 动名词把动作整体名词化，是 19 世纪书面语常见结构：the willing or doing the right or the wrong = 『意愿或行出正事或恶事（这件事）』。动名词后可直接带宾语（doing the right），翻译时补出『……这件事/这一行为』更通顺。
本句：the willing or doing the right or the wrong：定冠词 + 动名词的名词化结构，动名词后直接带宾语，意为『意愿或行出正与恶（这件事）』。

中道德判断力对是非、善恶的辨识，必然伴随着一种道德感受——这感受随着人意愿或行出正事或恶事而产生。

ENAnd, like the other self-recognized fact of dependence, which is produced and set in every man's conviction by known and felt helplessness, this conviction of responsibility implies of necessity a Being to whom the account must be rendered, or a rightful and supreme Moral Ruler and Judge.

语法介词前置的关系从句 to whom / of which
正式书面语把介词提到关系代词之前：a Being to whom the account must be rendered = a Being whom the account must be rendered to（一位必须向其交账的存在者）。理解时可先把介词放回从句末尾再翻译。类似：the comfort wherewith we are comforted（我们所受的安慰）。
本句：a Being to whom the account must be rendered：介词 to 前置于关系代词 whom，= a Being whom the account must be rendered to（必须向其交账的那位）。

中正如人因深知并切身感到自己的无助，而在心中确认“依赖”这一自明的事实一样，这种责任感也必然指向一位受账的存在者——一位名正言顺、至高无上的道德主宰与审判者。

ENTo this character the fact of dependence intimates the further relation of Creator, Upholder, and Provider. So much of God is assuredly disclosed by conscience and by actual living.

语法To this character 为状语前置（正常语序：the fact of dependence intimates ... to this character），此处前置但不倒装，用于承接上文。So much of God 作主语：『关于神的这么多内容』，of 表『关于……的部分』。

中在此之上，“依赖”这一事实进一步暗示出祂作为创造者、扶持者、供应者的关系。关于神的这些方面，确实已借着良心和实际的生活显明出来。

经文见证 / Scripture Testimony

ENRom. 14:10, 12. We shall all stand before the judgment-seat of God. Each one of us shall give account of himself to God. XI. 268.

BSBRomans 14:10, 12 10 Why, then, do you judge your brother? Or why do you belittle your brother? For we will all stand before God’s judgment seat. 12 So then, each of us will give an account of himself to God.

语法shall 表应许、命定的将来
在钦定本英语中，shall 不只是构成将来时：用于第二、三人称时带有『必然、命定、应许』的意味（God shall wipe away... 神必擦去……），比 will 更强调出于权柄或誓约的确定性。经文中大量的 shall 都应读出『必要、必然』的语气。第二人称形式为 shalt（thou shalt）。
本句：We shall all stand / shall give account：shall 表命定、必然的将来（必要站在……必要说明），语气强于 will。

中罗马书 14:10, 12 —— 我们都要站在神的台前……我们各人必要将自己的事在神面前说明。

EN1 Pet. 4:5. Shall give account to Him that is ready to judge the quick and dead.

BSB1 Peter 4:5 But they will have to give an account to Him who is ready to judge the living and the dead.

语法him that / them that = he who / those who
古英语中常用 that 引导指人的定语从句，且先行词直接用人称代词：Him that is ready to judge = 现代英语 Him who is ready to judge（那位预备好施行审判者）；them that suffer = those who suffer（那些受苦的人）；he that believeth = he who believes（信的人）。
本句：Him that is ready to judge = 现代英语 Him who is ready to judge。另注意 the quick 是古义『活人』（quick = living），与 dead 相对。

中彼得前书 4:5 —— 他们必在那将要审判活人死人的主面前交账。

ENMatt. 12:36. Every idle word … shall give account in the day of judgment.

BSBMatthew 12:36 But I tell you that men will give an account on the day of judgment for every careless word they have spoken.

中马太福音 12:36 —— 凡人所说的闲话，当审判的日子，必要句句供出来。

ENHeb. 13:17. As they that shall give account.

BSBHebrews 13:17 Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they watch over your souls as those who must give an account. To this end, allow them to lead with joy and not with grief, for that would be of no advantage to you.

语法参：him that / them that = he who / those who → 001 they that shall give account = those who shall give account（将来要交账的人）。

中希伯来书 13:17 —— 好像那将来交账的人。

ENEccles. 3:15. God requireth (seeketh again) the past. VI. 462.

BSBEcclesiastes 3:15 What exists has already been, and what will be has already been, for God will call to account what has passed.

语法古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est
-eth 是古英语第三人称单数现在时词尾，相当于现代英语的 -s：requireth = requires，seeketh = seeks，loveth = loves。-est 是与 thou 搭配的第二人称单数词尾：thou scornest = you scorn，thou chastenest = you chasten。特殊形式：hath = has，doth = does，saith = says，art = are（与 thou 连用），shalt = shall，wilt = will。
本句：requireth = requires，seeketh = seeks：-eth 为古英语第三人称单数词尾。

中传道书 3:15 —— 并且神使已过的事重新再来（原文直译：神必再寻回已过的事）。

ENEccles. 11:9. VI. 511. Eccles. 12:14. VI. 528.

BSBEcclesiastes 11:9 Rejoice, O young man, while you are young, and let your heart be glad in the days of your youth. Walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes, but know that for all these things God will bring you to judgment.

中另参：传道书 11:9；12:14。

ENMatt. 25:15. To every man according to his ability.

BSBMatthew 25:15 To one he gave five talents, to another two talents, and to another one talent—each according to his own ability. And he went on his journey.

中马太福音 25:15 —— 按着各人的才干给他们银子。

ENProv. 9:12. Wise for thyself; if thou scornest thou alone shalt bear it. VI. 293.

BSBProverbs 9:12 If you are wise, you are wise to your own advantage; but if you scoff, you alone will bear the consequences.

语法古英语第二人称代词 thou / thee / thy / ye
钦定本（KJV）时代的英语区分第二人称单复数：thou（你，主格）、thee（你，宾格）、thy/thine（你的）、thyself（你自己）；ye（你们，主格）、you（你们，宾格）。现代英语已统一为 you/your。阅读时把 thou/thee/ye 都理解为 you 即可，但注意单复数信息：thou 指一个人，ye 指多人。
本句：thou（你，主格）、thyself（你自己）；thou scornest 中 -est 是与 thou 搭配的动词词尾；shalt = shall 的第二人称形式。

中箴言 9:12 —— 你若有智慧，是与自己有益；你若亵慢，就必独自担当。

ENJob 34:11. The work of a man shall He render to him, and cause every man to find according to his way. VI. 179.

BSBJob 34:11 For according to a man’s deeds He repays him; according to a man’s ways He brings consequences.

语法倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装）
把宾语或状语提到句首以示强调时，主语和（助）动词常发生倒装：The work of a man shall He render to him = He shall render the work of a man to him（人所做的，他必报应他）；gave He the right = He gave the right；yet will I trust Him = yet I will trust Him；In faithfulness hast Thou afflicted me = Thou hast afflicted me in faithfulness。还原语序后再理解。
本句：The work of a man shall He render to him：宾语 The work of a man 前置引起主谓倒装，还原为 He shall render the work of a man to him。

中约伯记 34:11 —— 他必按人所做的报应人，使各人照所行的得报。

ENPs. 62:12.

BSBPsalm 62:12 and loving devotion to You, O Lord. For You will repay each man according to his deeds.

中诗篇 62:12 —— 主啊，慈爱也是属乎你，因为你照着各人所行的报应他。

ENRom. 2:5. Who will render to every man according to his deeds.

BSBRomans 2:6 God “will repay each one according to his deeds.”

中罗马书 2:5（-6）—— 他必照各人的行为报应各人。

EN2 Cor. 5:10. We must be made manifest before the judgment-seat of Christ, that every one may receive according to that he hath done. XI. 362.

BSB2 Corinthians 5:10 For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive his due for the things done in the body, whether good or bad.

语法古体助动词 hath / doth
hath = has（he hath done = he has done）；doth = does，常用于否定和强调：He doth not afflict willingly = He does not afflict willingly（他并不甘心使人受苦）。这些只是拼写古体，语法功能与现代 has/does 相同。
本句：he hath done = he has done：hath 是 has 的古体。according to that he hath done 中 that = what（按他所行的）。

中哥林多后书 5:10 —— 因为我们众人必要在基督台前显露出来，叫各人按着本身所行的，或善或恶受报。

ENRev. 2:23. I will give unto each one of you according to your works.

BSBRevelation 2:23 Then I will strike her children dead, and all the churches will know that I am the One who searches minds and hearts, and I will repay each of you according to your deeds.

中启示录 2:23 —— 我要照你们的行为报应你们各人。

ENRev. 22:12.

BSBRevelation 22:12 “Behold, I am coming soon, and My reward is with Me, to give to each one according to what he has done.

中启示录 22:12 —— 看哪，我必快来！赏罚在我，要照各人所行的报应他。

ENEzek. 18:20. The soul that sinneth, it shall die; the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him. IX. 81-86.

BSBEzekiel 18:20 The soul who sins is the one who will die. A son will not bear the iniquity of his father, and a father will not bear the iniquity of his son. The righteousness of the righteous man will fall upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked man will fall upon him.

语法参：古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est → 001 sinneth = sins。
语法参：shall 表应许、命定的将来 → 001 it shall die / shall be upon him：shall 表命定的必然。
语法the + 形容词表一类人
定冠词 the + 形容词（不加名词）泛指具有该特征的一类人：the righteous 义人，the wicked 恶人，the good 善人，the upright 正直人，the ransomed 蒙救赎的人。作主语时按复数处理：Why the good suffer and the bad prosper?（为何善人受苦而恶人亨通？）
本句：the righteous 义人 / the wicked 恶人：the + 形容词表一类人。

中以西结书 18:20 —— 惟有犯罪的，他必死亡。义人的善果必归自己，恶人的恶报也必归自己。

ENSee Condemnation; Day of the Lord; Conscience.

中另参主题词条：定罪（Condemnation）；主的日子（Day of the Lord）；良心（Conscience）。
