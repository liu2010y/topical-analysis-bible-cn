标题与定义 / Title & Definition

ENADOPTION. Admission, by gracious Reinstatement, to full rights and privileges of Childship and Home Fellowship with God.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 词典释义式的无谓语句：全句是一个名词短语，主干为 Admission to full rights and privileges（被接纳获得完整的权利与特权），by gracious Reinstatement 为插入的方式状语。

中得儿子的名分：借着恩典中的复位，被接纳进入与神之间儿女身份和家中团契的完整权利与特权。

正文对照 / Text

ENA Fruit and Proof of the Father's love. 1 John 3:1. A result of Christ's redeeming work. Gal. 4:4, 5. Childship ensures heirship with Christ to eternal glory. Rom. 8:17.

语法省略结构（省略主语、系动词 be 等）
书面语和诗体中常省略可从上下文补出的成分：And because sons, God sent forth... = because we are sons（因为你们既是儿子）；Many the afflictions of the righteous = Many are the afflictions...（义人的苦难是多的）；Blessed the man whom... = Blessed is the man（那人有福了）。理解时先补全再翻译。
本句：前两句为省略 It is 的名词短语句：A Fruit and Proof of the Father's love = (It is) a fruit and proof...（这是父爱的果实与明证）。纲要式文体常用。

中这是父之爱的果实与明证（约翰一书 3:1）；是基督救赎之工的成果（加拉太书 4:4-5）。儿女的身份确保与基督同作后嗣，承受永远的荣耀（罗马书 8:17）。

借圣灵的见证与引导 / By the Witness and Leading of the Holy Spirit

ENRom. 8:14-16. As many as are led by the Spirit of God, are the sons of God. Ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father. The Spirit beareth witness with our spirit that we are the children of God. If children, then heirs with Christ. XI. 236.

BSBRomans 8:14, 15, 16 14 For all who are led by the Spirit of God are sons of God. 15 For you did not receive a spirit of slavery that returns you to fear, but you received the Spirit of adoption to sonship, by whom we cry, “Abba! Father!” 16 The Spirit Himself testifies with our spirit that we are God’s children.

语法as many as 关联结构
as many as = all who / everyone who（凡……的人）：As many as are led by the Spirit of God, are the sons of God = 凡被神的灵引导的，都是神的儿子。As many as I love, I reprove = 凡我所疼爱的，我就责备。注意它作整个句子的主语或宾语。
本句：As many as are led by the Spirit = all who are led（凡被圣灵引导的人）。
语法复合关系副词 whereby / wherewith / wherein
where + 介词构成的复合关系词，等于『介词 + which』：whereby = by which（借此）、wherewith = with which（用以）、wherein = in which（在其中）、whereof = of which（关于它）。例：the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba = 借着这灵我们呼叫『阿爸』。
本句：whereby we cry = by which we cry（借着这灵我们呼叫）。
语法参：省略结构（省略主语、系动词 be 等） → 002 If children, then heirs：省略系动词，= If we are children, then we are heirs。

中罗马书 8:14-16 —— 因为凡被神的灵引导的，都是神的儿子。……你们所受的乃是儿子的心（原文直译：得儿子名分之灵），因此我们呼叫：“阿爸！父！”圣灵与我们的心同证我们是神的儿女；既是儿女，便是后嗣，……和基督同作后嗣。
