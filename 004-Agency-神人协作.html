标题 / Title

ENAGENCY, DIVINE AND HUMAN, in Providence and Redemption. Divine Willing and Working the Source and Cause. Human Willing and Doing a Condition and Means. Human Agency Demanded. Divine Agency Assures Results.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 四个纲要式短句，均无谓语或省略系动词：Divine Willing and Working (is) the Source and Cause…。
语法参：the + 动名词的名词化结构 → 001 Willing / Working / Doing：动名词名词化，指『意愿』『运行』『行动』这些行为本身。

中神人协作——在护理与救赎之工中。神的意愿与运行是源头与原因；人的意愿与行动是条件与途径。人的作为是被要求的本分；神的作为保证成果。

语法不用 do 的古体否定（Be not / Fret not）

四、论述摘录 / Extracts

EN[If the gospel be accepted, it must be by a process in which the will has a part, and to which the soul gives determinant force. It is true that the gospel offers to man reasonable grounds for decision, but it never crushes out his individuality. The prodigal remembers his Father's house, and is melted. The sinner looks to Him that "first loved him," but is neither captured by force nor imprisoned with violence. If he be saved, it will be by the exercise of that which is most distinctive of him as a man and an individual—the use of God's noblest gift to the spirit, the free human Will. — Interior.]

语法参：though / if 从句中的虚拟语气（动词用原形） → 003 If the gospel be accepted / If he be saved：if 从句用 be 原形，古典虚拟语气。
语法参：介词前置的关系从句 to whom / of which → 001 a process in which the will has a part, and to which the soul gives determinant force：两个介词前置的关系从句并列修饰 process。
语法参：同位语结构 → 001 the use of God's noblest gift to the spirit, the free human Will：the free human Will 是 gift 的同位语。

中【摘录】福音若被接受，必是经由一个过程：人的意志在其中有份，心灵为其注入决定性的力量。福音固然向人提供合理的抉择根据，却从不碾碎人的个体性。浪子想起父家，心就融化；罪人仰望那『先爱他』的主，却既非被强力掳获，也非被暴力囚禁。人若得救，必是借着运用他作为人、作为个体最独特的禀赋——神赐给人灵最尊贵的礼物：自由的人类意志。——《Interior》杂志

EN[When treating of the divine efficiency in redemption and of the divine origination of it, Paul speaks freely and unreservedly from the standpoint of God's good pleasure in the choice of men to salvation; when speaking of the human conditions of its appropriation, he speaks as unreservedly from that point of view, laying full, strong emphasis upon the capacity of men to receive or reject the offered grace, and upon their responsibility for their choice. … When the aim is to humble human pride and pretension before the sovereign might of God, he overleaps all human conditions, and without definition or qualification asserts the divine absoluteness, as though God dealt with men as the potter deals with passive clay. But when the aim is to silence human excuses for neglect and disobedience, he brings into full prominence the free wrong choices and conduct of men as the reason for God's rejection and condemnation. These two truths Paul asserts with equal energy and plainness. He adds no word of comment upon their relation to each other. He betrays no consciousness of any conflict or contradiction between them. — G. B. Stevens.]

语法When treating of… / when speaking of…：现在分词短语作时间状语，逻辑主语为句子主语 Paul（当他论到……时）。laying full, strong emphasis upon…：分词短语作伴随状语。
语法参：though / if 从句中的虚拟语气（动词用原形） → 003 as though God dealt with men…：as though + 过去式表虚拟（仿佛神真是这样待人——实际并非全貌）。
语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 These two truths Paul asserts…：宾语前置（= Paul asserts these two truths…），此处前置但不倒装，用于强调与承接。

中【摘录】保罗论到救赎中神的效力、救赎出于神的发起时，全然站在神乐意拣选人得救恩的立场上，畅所欲言、毫无保留；而论到人领受救恩的条件时，他同样毫无保留地站在另一立场，全力强调人有接受或拒绝所赐恩典的能力，以及人对自己抉择所负的责任。……当目的是要使人的骄傲与僭妄在神主权的大能面前降卑时，他越过一切属人的条件，不加界定、不设限制地宣告神的绝对性，仿佛神对待人就像陶匠对待无生命的泥土；但当目的是要堵住人为疏忽悖逆所找的借口时，他又把人自由做出的错误选择与行为摆在最显眼处，作为神弃绝定罪的理由。这两个真理，保罗以同等的力度和明晰来宣讲；对两者之间的关系，他不置一词；在他身上也看不出丝毫察觉二者冲突矛盾的意识。——G. B. 史蒂文斯（G. B. Stevens）
