标题 / Title

ENANGER OF MAN; Wrath; Vengeance. See Condemnation (Wrath of God).

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 词条标题：主题词＋分号并列近义词，并用 See 指出相关词条。

中人的怒气；忿怒；报复。（神的忿怒另参主题词条：定罪 Condemnation。）

一、怒气的恶果 / The Evil of Anger

ENProv. 27:4. Wrath is cruel, and anger is overwhelming. Prov. 29:22. An angry man stirreth up strife. Prov. 14:17. He that is soon angry dealeth foolishly.

BSBProverbs 27:4 Wrath is cruel and anger is like a flood, but who can withstand jealousy?

语法参：him that / them that = he who / those who → 001 He that is soon angry = he who is quickly angered（轻易发怒的人）。
语法参：古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est → 001 stirreth = stirs（挑动），dealeth = deals（行事）。

中箴言 27:4 —— 忿怒为残忍，怒气为狂澜。29:22 —— 好气的人挑启争端。14:17 —— 轻易发怒的，行事愚妄。

ENEph. 4:31. Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger be put away from you, with all malice. Col. 3:8. Ps. 37:8. Rom. 12:19. Eph. 6:4.

BSBEphesians 4:31 Get rid of all bitterness, rage and anger, outcry and slander, along with every form of malice.

语法参：Let + 第三人称祈使句 → 003 Let all bitterness … be put away：Let + 第三人称的被动祈使（愿这一切都被除掉）。

中以弗所书 4:31 —— 一切苦毒、恼恨、忿怒……并一切的恶毒，都当从你们中间除掉。另参：歌罗西书 3:8；诗篇 37:8；罗马书 12:19；以弗所书 6:4（不要惹儿女的气）。

二、制怒的智慧 / Wisdom in Restraining Anger

ENProv. 15:1. A soft answer turneth away wrath, but a grievous word stirreth up anger. VI. 330. Prov. 29:11. A fool uttereth all his anger, but a wise man keepeth it back and stilleth it. VI. 418.

BSBProverbs 15:1 A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.

语法参：古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est → 001 turneth / stirreth / uttereth / keepeth / stilleth：全部是 -eth 第三人称单数（= turns / stirs / utters / keeps / stills）。

中箴言 15:1 —— 回答柔和，使怒消退；言语暴戾，触动怒气。29:11 —— 愚妄人怒气全发，智慧人忍气含怒。

ENEccles. 7:9. Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry, for anger resteth in the bosom of fools. Eph. 4:26. Be ye angry, and sin not; let not the sun go down upon your wrath. James 1:19. Slow to wrath. Prov. 14:29. He that is slow to anger is of great understanding. VI. 326.

BSBEcclesiastes 7:9 Do not be quickly provoked in your spirit, for anger settles in the lap of a fool.

语法参：不用 do 的古体否定（Be not / Fret not） → 004 Be not hasty / sin not / let not the sun go down：三个古体否定祈使，均不用 do。
语法参：古英语第二人称代词 thou / thee / thy / ye → 001 Be ye angry：祈使句保留主语 ye（你们）。

中传道书 7:9 —— 你不要心里急躁恼怒，因为恼怒存在愚昧人的怀中。以弗所书 4:26 —— 生气却不要犯罪，不可含怒到日落。雅各书 1:19 —— 慢慢地动怒。箴言 14:29 —— 不轻易发怒的，大有聪明。

ENProv. 16:32. VI. 343, 4. Prov. 19:11. VI. 359. Prov. 20:22. VI. 366. Prov. 24:29. VI. 398. Prov. 25:21, 22, 28. VI. 402.

BSBProverbs 16:32 He who is slow to anger is better than a warrior, and he who controls his temper is greater than one who captures a city.

中另参：箴言 16:32（不轻易发怒的，胜过勇士；治服己心的，强如取城）；19:11（人有见识，就不轻易发怒）；20:22（不要说：我要以恶报恶）；24:29；25:21-22, 28。

三、伸冤在神 / Vengeance Belongs to God

ENDeut. 32:35. To Me belongeth vengeance, and recompense. Ps. 94:1. Rom. 12:19. Heb. 10:30. III. 439.

BSBDeuteronomy 32:35 Vengeance is Mine; I will repay. In due time their foot will slip; for their day of disaster is near, and their doom is coming quickly.”

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 To Me belongeth vengeance：状语前置引起倒装（= vengeance belongs to Me，伸冤在我）。

中申命记 32:35 —— 他们失脚的时候，伸冤报应在我。另参：诗篇 94:1（耶和华啊，你是伸冤的神）；罗马书 12:19（主说，伸冤在我，我必报应）；希伯来书 10:30。
