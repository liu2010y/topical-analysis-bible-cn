标题 / Title

ENANXIETY; Care; Fret; Complaining.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 词条标题：主题词＋分号并列近义词（挂虑、烦躁、怨言），标示词条覆盖范围。

中忧虑；挂虑；烦躁不平；发怨言。

一、不要忧虑烦躁 / Fret Not

ENPs. 37:1, 7, 8. Fret not thyself. IV. 271, 276, 278. Ps. 49 and 73. Lam. 3:39. Wherefore doth a living man complain? VIII. 611.

BSBPsalm 37:1 Of David. Do not fret over those who do evil; do not envy those who do wrong.

语法参：不用 do 的古体否定（Be not / Fret not） → 004 Fret not thyself = Do not fret：古体否定祈使；thyself 作反身宾语（不要使自己烦躁不平）。
语法Wherefore doth a living man complain?：wherefore =【古】why；doth = does。

中诗篇 37:1, 7, 8 —— 不要心怀不平。另参诗篇 49、73 篇。耶利米哀歌 3:39 —— 活人因自己的罪受罚，为何发怨言呢？

ENMatt. 6:34. Take no anxious thought (for the morrow). X. 162, 197. Phil. 4:6, 7. In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known unto God; and the peace of God shall guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus. XI. 462.

BSBPhilippians 4:6, 7 6 Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. 7 And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

语法参：不用 do 的古体否定（Be not / Fret not） → 004 In nothing be anxious：否定状语前置的古体祈使（= Do not be anxious about anything，应当一无挂虑）。
语法参：Let + 第三人称祈使句 → 003 let your requests be made known：Let + 被动祈使（将你们所要的告诉神）。

中马太福音 6:34 —— 不要为明天忧虑。腓立比书 4:6-7 —— 应当一无挂虑，只要凡事藉着祷告、祈求和感谢，将你们所要的告诉神。神所赐出人意外的平安，必在基督耶稣里保守你们的心怀意念。

二、把忧虑卸给神 / Cast It upon Him

EN1 Pet. 5:7. Casting all your anxiety upon Him, because He careth for you. XI. 668. John 14:1. Let not your heart be troubled; … believe also in Me. X. 480, 5, 7.

BSB1 Peter 5:7 Cast all your anxiety on Him, because He cares for you.

语法参：不用 do 的古体否定（Be not / Fret not） → 004 Let not your heart be troubled = Do not let your heart be troubled：古体否定祈使。
语法Casting all your anxiety upon Him：现在分词短语，承接上文『你们要谦卑』的祈使（藉着把忧虑卸给神来谦卑服在神大能的手下）。

中彼得前书 5:7 —— 你们要将一切的忧虑卸给神，因为他顾念你们。约翰福音 14:1 —— 你们心里不要忧愁；你们信神，也当信我。

ENPs. 112:7. He shall not be afraid of evil tidings: his heart is fixed, trusting in the Lord. V. 252, 3. Deut. 33:25. As thy days, so shall thy strength be. II. 735.

BSBPsalm 112:7 He does not fear bad news; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD.

语法参：as … so … 关联结构（正如……照样……） → 008 As thy days, so shall thy strength be：as…so… 关联结构＋倒装（你的日子如何，力量也必如何）。原书节引作 "As thy days, thy strength"。

中诗篇 112:7 —— 他必不怕凶恶的信息，他心坚定，倚靠耶和华。申命记 33:25 —— 你的日子如何，你的力量也必如何。

ENSee Despondency; Infirmity.

中另参主题词条：沮丧（Despondency）；软弱（Infirmity）。
