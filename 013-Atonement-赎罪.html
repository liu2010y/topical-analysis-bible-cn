标题与主旨 / Title & Central Statement

ENATONEMENT. God has made the Death of Christ the Procuring Cause of Human Salvation. This is the Central and Cardinal Fact of Divine Revelation. Its expression is concentrated in the heralding Baptist's sublime Announcement when, pointing to the approaching Christ, he exclaimed: Behold the Lamb of God, Who taketh away the sin of the World!

BSBJohn 1:29 The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him and said, “Look, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!

语法make + 宾语 + 名词补语：has made the Death of Christ the Procuring Cause…（使基督的死成为……的原因）；pointing to the approaching Christ：现在分词短语作伴随状语。

中赎罪：神已使基督的死成为人类得救的成就性原因。这是神圣启示的中心与首要事实。它的表达集中于施洗约翰那庄严的宣告——他指着迎面走来的基督，喊道：『看哪，神的羔羊，除去（或作背负）世人罪孽的！』（约翰福音 1:29）

主旨经文的总括陈述 / This Vital Fact Comprehensively Stated

ENIsa. 53:5, 6, 12. He was wounded for our transgressions. The Lord laid on Him the iniquity of us all. He poured out His soul unto death. He bare the sin of many.

BSBIsaiah 53:5, 6 5 But He was pierced for our transgressions, He was crushed for our iniquities; the punishment that brought us peace was upon Him, and by His stripes we are healed. 6 We all like sheep have gone astray, each one has turned to his own way; and the LORD has laid upon Him the iniquity of us all.

语法bare 是 bear 的古过去式（= bore，担当）；注意与形容词 bare『赤裸的』同形。

中以赛亚书 53:5, 6, 12 —— 哪知他为我们的过犯受害。耶和华使我们众人的罪孽都归在他身上。他将命倾倒，以致于死。他却担当多人的罪。

EN1 Pet. 2:24. Who His own self bare our sins in His body upon the tree, that we might live unto righteousness. XI. 656.

BSB1 Peter 2:24 He Himself bore our sins in His body on the tree, so that we might die to sin and live to righteousness. “By His stripes you are healed.”

语法His own self = himself：古体强调反身（亲身）；that we might live：that = so that，目的从句用 might。

中彼得前书 2:24 —— 他被挂在木头上，亲身担当了我们的罪，使我们既然在罪上死，就得以在义上活。

EN1 Pet. 3:18. Christ suffered for sins once, the righteous for the unrighteous, that He might bring us unto God; being put to death in the flesh, but quickened in the spirit.

BSB1 Peter 3:18 For Christ also suffered for sins once for all, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring you to God. He was put to death in the body but made alive in the Spirit,

语法参：the + 形容词表一类人 → 001 the righteous for the unrighteous：义者代替不义者（the + 形容词表一类人）。
语法quickened：古义『使活过来』（quick = 活的），与现代义『加快』不同。

中彼得前书 3:18 —— 因基督也曾一次为罪受苦（有古卷作受死），就是义的代替不义的，为要引我们到神面前。按着肉体说，他被治死；按着灵性说，他复活了。

ENPhil. 2:8. He humbled Himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross. XI. 452. Heb. 12:2. Who for the joy set before Him endured the cross.

BSBPhilippians 2:8 And being found in appearance as a man, He humbled Himself and became obedient to death—even death on a cross.

中腓立比书 2:8 —— 就自己卑微，存心顺服，以至于死，且死在十字架上。希伯来书 12:2 —— 他因那摆在前面的喜乐，就轻看羞辱，忍受了十字架的苦难。

EN2 Cor. 5:21. Him Who knew no sin, He made to be sin on our behalf; that we might become the righteousness of God in Him. XI. 366.

BSB2 Corinthians 5:21 God made Him who knew no sin to be sin on our behalf, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Him Who knew no sin, He made to be sin：宾语（Him Who knew no sin）前置以示强调，= He made Him who knew no sin to be sin。

中哥林多后书 5:21 —— 神使那无罪的（原文作不知罪的），替我们成为罪，好叫我们在他里面成为神的义。

一、新旧约用词考释 / I. Meaning of Terms Employed

EN1. Atonement. (In O. T. exclusively.) Heb. Kippurim, Coverings; Kaphar, to Cover. Lev. 23:27, 28. The idea of atonement is the foundation-thought in all the sacrifices and in all the services, both in the ritual of the altar and of the Holy-place. One day of the year, and that the most sacred of all, the great fact of atonement was presented alone and in the most solemn manner before the minds of the people. — J. M. Gibson. It was in all its services and ceremonies the fullest representation, the most perfect shadow, of the great work of Redemption. — G. Bush. II. 414-419.

BSBLeviticus 23:27 “The tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement. You shall hold a sacred assembly and humble yourselves, and present a food offering to the LORD.

语法and that the most sacred of all：that 复指前文 One day，『而且是万日中最神圣的那一日』——古典文体的强调插入。

中1. 赎罪（Atonement）。（旧约专用词。）希伯来文 Kippurim『遮盖』（复数）；动词 Kaphar『遮盖』。利未记 23:27-28（赎罪日的条例）。赎罪的观念是一切祭祀、一切事奉的根基思想，无论祭坛的礼仪还是圣所的礼仪都是如此。一年中有一日——且是万日中最神圣的一日——赎罪这一大事实被单独地、以最庄严的方式呈现在民众心目之前。——J. M. 吉布森（Gibson）。赎罪日在其全部事奉与礼仪中，乃是救赎大工最完满的表象、最完美的影儿。——G. 布什（Bush）

EN2. Reconciliation; To Reconcile, Make Reconciliation. Heb. Kaphar; Gr. Katallasso, Katallage—signifying To Cover, Make Atonement; to Change thoroughly; to Appease, Propitiate. Dan. 9:24. To make reconciliation for iniquity, and bring in everlasting righteousness. IX. 299. Rom. 5:10, 11. Reconciled to God by the death of His Son; through Whom we have now received the (atonement) reconciliation. XI. 218. 2 Cor. 5:18, 19. Reconciled us to Himself through Christ; God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto Himself, not reckoning to them their trespasses. XI. 365, 6. Eph. 2:16. That He might reconcile both unto God. Col. 1:20, 21. Through the blood of the cross, by Him to reconcile all things to Himself. You that were enemies, by His death hath He reconciled. XI. 470. Heb. 2:17. Make reconciliation for sins. Heb. 9:12. Through His own blood entered in once for all into the Holy Place, having obtained eternal redemption. XI. 588.

BSBRomans 5:10 For if, when we were enemies of God, we were reconciled to Him through the death of His Son, how much more, having been reconciled, shall we be saved through His life!

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 You that were enemies, by His death hath He reconciled：宾语与状语前置引起倒装（= He hath reconciled you that were enemies by His death）。

中2. 复和（Reconciliation）。希伯来文 Kaphar；希腊文 Katallasso、Katallage——意为『遮盖、赎罪』；『彻底改变』；『平息、挽回』。但以理书 9:24 —— 赎尽罪孽，引进永义。罗马书 5:10-11 —— 我们……藉着神儿子的死，得与神和好……并且……以神为乐（原文直译：藉着他领受了复和）。哥林多后书 5:18-19 —— 一切都是出于神，他藉着基督使我们与他和好……这就是神在基督里叫世人与自己和好，不将他们的过犯归到他们身上。以弗所书 2:16 —— 使两下……与神和好了。歌罗西书 1:20-21 —— 藉着他在十字架上所流的血成就了和平，便藉着他叫万有……都与自己和好了；你们从前与神隔绝，……但如今他藉着基督的肉身受死，叫你们与自己和好。希伯来书 2:17 —— 为百姓的罪献上挽回祭。9:12 —— 用自己的血，只一次进入圣所，成了永远赎罪的事。【OCR 注：原书小节号误作 "3."，依次序改为 2.；Katailage 修复为 Katallage。】

EN3. Propitiation. Gr. Hilasmos—What Appeases, Propitiates. Rom. 3:25. A propitiation, through faith, in His blood. XI. 212, 3. 1 John 2:2. He is the propitiation for our sins. XI. 685. 1 John 4:10. Sent His Son to be the propitiation for our sins. XI. 696.

BSB1 John 4:10 And love consists in this: not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son as the atoning sacrifice for our sins.

中3. 挽回祭（Propitiation）。希腊文 Hilasmos——平息（怒气）、挽回之物。罗马书 3:25 —— 神设立耶稣作挽回祭，是凭着耶稣的血，藉着人的信。约翰一书 2:2 —— 他为我们的罪作了挽回祭。4:10 —— 差他的儿子为我们的罪作了挽回祭。

EN4. Mercy-Seat. Heb. Kapporeth (Kaphar), Place of Covering; Gr. Hilasterion, Place of Propitiation or Appeasement. Ex. 25:17. Make a mercy-seat of pure gold. (The word Kapporeth, or covering, used only in the spiritual sense of atonement.) II. 300, 1. Heb. 9:5. Cherubim of glory overshadowing the mercy-seat (propitiatory).

BSBExodus 25:17 And you are to construct a mercy seat of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.

中4. 施恩座（Mercy-Seat）。希伯来文 Kapporeth（出于 Kaphar），『遮盖之处』；希腊文 Hilasterion，『挽回、息怒之处』。出埃及记 25:17 —— 要用精金做施恩座。（Kapporeth『遮盖』一词只用于赎罪的属灵意义。）希伯来书 9:5 —— 柜上面有荣耀基路伯的影罩着施恩座。

EN5. Cross of Christ; Crucified. Gal. 6:14. God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ. XI. 412, 3. Phil. 2:8. Became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross. Col. 1:20. Peace through the blood of the cross. Heb. 12:2. Endured the cross. 1 Cor. 2:2. I determined not to know anything among you, save Jesus Christ, and Him crucified. XI. 284, 5.

BSB1 Corinthians 2:2 For I resolved to know nothing while I was with you except Jesus Christ and Him crucified.

语法save 作介词 =【古】except（只，除了）：save in the cross（只夸十字架）；save Jesus Christ（只知道耶稣基督）。God forbid that…：固定表达『断乎不可』，forbid 用虚拟原形。

中5. 基督的十字架；钉十字架。加拉太书 6:14 —— 但我断不以别的夸口，只夸我们主耶稣基督的十字架。腓立比书 2:8 —— 存心顺服，以至于死，且死在十字架上。歌罗西书 1:20 —— 藉着他在十字架上所流的血成就了和平。希伯来书 12:2 —— 忍受了十字架的苦难。哥林多前书 2:2 —— 因为我曾定了主意，在你们中间不知道别的，只知道耶稣基督并他钉十字架。

二、旧约献祭中以预表显明的赎罪 / II. Atonement as Disclosed in Type through Old Testament Sacrifice

ENThe only use and justification of Animal Sacrifice, by the Patriarchs from Adam to Jacob, and through the elaborate Sacrificial System of the Ceremonial Law of Sinai, was in the fact that it was a needed method of symbolizing to the Old Testament generations God's plan of Redemption by substitution of life for life. Nothing else could be devised so simple, clear and impressive, and so suited to man's relation to God and His Laws as a disobedient offender.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Nothing else could be devised so simple…：否定词开头＋补语后置的强调句式，= No other method so simple… could be devised。

中从亚当到雅各的列祖所献的动物祭，以及西奈山礼仪律法中繁复的献祭体系，其惟一的用途与存在理由就在于：对旧约世代的人而言，它是把神『以命换命、代替救赎』的计划象征出来的必需方式。再没有别的办法能设计得如此简明、清晰而深刻，又如此切合人作为悖逆的干犯者与神及其律法的关系。

ENThe ideas plainly presented to the offerer in the typical act of sacrifice were mainly three: 1. Substitution of an unoffending animal for the human offender in the matter of suffering for wrong-doing. 2. Nothing less than the life-blood of the substituted victim sufficed for the remission of deserved penalty to the human offender. (Lev. 17:11. "Blood maketh atonement by reason of the life.") 3. The offerer's act of laying his hand upon the substitute animal, in the exercise of faith and with words of penitent confession, intimated a transfer of guilt from the offerer to the victim, and, on account of such transfer, the offender's reliance for immunity from the penal effects of his own wrong-doing.

BSBLeviticus 17:11 For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it to you to make atonement for your souls upon the altar; for it is the blood that makes atonement for the soul.

语法Nothing less than…sufficed：『非……不足以』（惟有生命之血才够）；in the exercise of faith：介词短语表方式（在信心的运用中）。

中献祭这一预表性行动向献祭者明明呈现的观念主要有三：一、由一只无辜的牲畜代替有罪的人，承受行恶应得的苦楚；二、惟有被替代之牺牲的生命之血，才足以豁免人类干犯者当受的刑罚（利未记 17:11——『因血里有生命，所以能赎罪』）；三、献祭者凭信心、口称痛悔认罪，按手在替代牲畜之上——这一动作表明罪责从献祭者转移到牺牲身上；并且因着这一转移，干犯者可以倚靠它免受自己恶行的刑罚性后果。

ENThese ideas substantially convey and exhaust the typical teaching of Old Testament Sacrifice as regards the fact and meaning of Atonement. The full interpretation of that teaching we find partly in the Psalmists and Prophets, but chiefly in the words and sufferings of Christ and their full exposition by the inspired writers of the Gospels, the Acts, the Epistles, and the Revelation. Further, study the 9th and 10th chapters of Hebrews, of which the key thought is found in 9:22: All things are cleansed with blood, and apart from shedding of blood (life-blood) there is no remission. XI. 586-593. See Sacrifice.

BSBHebrews 9:22 According to the law, in fact, nearly everything must be purified with blood, and without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 The full interpretation of that teaching we find partly in…：宾语前置（= we find the full interpretation … partly in…）。
语法参：介词前置的关系从句 to whom / of which → 001 of which the key thought is found in 9:22：介词 of 前置的关系从句（这两章的钥节见于 9:22）。

中就赎罪的事实与意义而言，这三个观念大体上传达且穷尽了旧约献祭的预表性教导。对这教导的完整解释，我们一部分在诗人和先知那里找到，但主要是在基督的言语与受苦之中，以及福音书、使徒行传、书信和启示录受灵感的作者们对其所作的充分阐述之中。进一步可研读希伯来书第 9、10 两章，其钥节是 9:22——凡物差不多都是用血洁净的，若不流血，罪就不得赦免了。另参主题词条：献祭（Sacrifice）。

EN[The sacrifices of Israel were intended and adapted not only to meet the deep-felt want of human nature, common to them as to all other tribes, but also to point onward to Him in whose death a real want of mankind was met, in whose death a real sacrifice was offered, in whose death an angry God was not indeed propitiated, but in whose death the loving Father of our souls Himself provided the Lamb for the offering—without which, for reasons deeper than we can wholly fathom, it were impossible that sin should be remitted. There is no gospel worth calling so, worth the preaching, worth your believing, or that will ever move the world or purify society, except the gospel which begins with the fact of an atonement, and points to the cross as the altar on which the sacrifice for the sins of the world has died for us all. — Maclaren.]

语法参：介词前置的关系从句 to whom / of which → 001 Him in whose death…（四连排比）：介词 + whose 引导的关系从句连续四次，层层推进——19 世纪讲章文体的典型修辞。
语法参：though / if 从句中的虚拟语气（动词用原形） → 003 it were impossible that sin should be remitted：were 为虚拟（= it would be impossible）；that 从句用 should + 原形，正式书面语。

中【摘录】以色列的祭祀，其设立与设计不仅是为满足人性深处共同的需要——这需要以色列人与万族无异——更是为指向前方的那一位：在他的死里，人类真实的需要得了满足；在他的死里，献上了一个真实的祭；在他的死里，并非平息了一位怒气冲冲的神，而是我们灵魂的慈父亲自为燔祭预备了羔羊。若没有这祭——由于比我们所能完全测透的更深的缘由——罪得赦免便无可能。凡配称福音、配得传讲、配得你信、并且真能撼动世界洁净社会的福音，无一例外，都始于赎罪这一事实，并指向十字架——那为世人之罪所献的祭在其上替我们众人死了的祭坛。——麦克拉伦（Maclaren）

三、基督舍己之祭所成全的赎罪 / III. Atonement as Realized and Fulfilled by the Self-Sacrificial Offering of Jesus Christ

EN1. Distinct Intimations, References, and Declarations of the Old Testament. (An Outline for study.) First intimated in the primal root-promise of God to Adam: Gen. 3:15. Referred to in covenant promises to Abraham: chiefly Gen. 22:17, 18—In thy seed shall all nations of the earth be blessed (explained Gal. 3:7-9, 16, 29); and to David: 2 Sam. 7:12-16. See Covenants. Prophetic References and Declarations: Ps. 22:1, 7, 8, 16-18. IV. 169-175. Isa. 52:13-15; 53:1-12. VIII. 276-293. Dan. 9:24-26. IX. 299, 303. Zech. 11:12, 13. IX. 598. Zech. 13:1, 7. IX. 605-608. 1 Pet. 1:11. The Spirit of Christ in the prophets testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ. XI. 648.

BSBGenesis 3:15 And I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your seed and her seed. He will crush your head, and you will strike his heel.”

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 In thy seed shall all nations of the earth be blessed：状语前置引起倒装（= all nations shall be blessed in thy seed）。

中1. 旧约中清楚的预示、指涉与宣告。（研经纲要。）最早预示于神对亚当所发的原初根本应许：创世记 3:15（女人的后裔要伤你的头）。继而见于对亚伯拉罕的立约应许——主要是创世记 22:17-18『地上万国都必因你的后裔得福』（加拉太书 3:7-9, 16, 29 对此的解释）；以及对大卫的应许：撒母耳记下 7:12-16。另参主题词条：诸约（Covenants）。先知性的指涉与宣告：诗篇 22:1, 7-8, 16-18（我的神，我的神，为什么离弃我）；以赛亚书 52:13-15；53:1-12；但以理书 9:24-26；撒迦利亚书 11:12-13（三十块钱）；13:1, 7（刀剑哪，应当兴起攻击我的牧人）。彼得前书 1:11 —— 基督的灵……预先证明基督受苦难、后来得荣耀。【OCR 注：本段引用编号在扫描中散乱，已按经文体系重建。】

EN2. Plain and Multiform Assertions of the New Testament, under varied aspects and statements. (1) Our Reconciler with God. For texts see above, I. 2. Also Eph. 2:13, 14. In Christ Jesus ye that were afar off are made nigh in the blood of Christ. For He is our peace, Who hath made both one. XI. 422. (2) The Propitiation for our sins. For texts see above, I. 3. (3) Our Efficient, Sufficient and Accepted Sacrifice. 1 Cor. 5:7. Christ our Passover is sacrificed for us. XI. 295. Eph. 5:2. Christ gave Himself up for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God. XI. 436. Heb. 9:26. He appeared to put away sin by the sacrifice of Himself. XI. 588.

EN[Two great facts stand out in the Scriptural statements covering the death of Christ. The first is that He died for us, in our place, as covering us, and so receiving in His own person the penalty of the violated law of God. He is our substitute, not by exchange of place, but by covering: He occupies His own place, which place covers also our own. The second fact is that He who dies for us is none other than the instrumental cause of our being. The crucified is none other than He by whom the worlds were made. Not Adam, but Christ, is the Root of the race. The agent of God in creation is the agent of God in redemption. He is the representative and substitute of man in atoning action; and He is the creative source of our being. The representative function is rooted in the creative relation. There is no imputation of guilt. There is no transfer of penalty. There is no substitution of one form of punishment for another. Christ, in the agony of conscious abandonment by the Father, endures and exhausts that death which is the proper penalty of the violated law; and He endures it as covering our place. It has often been said that Christ must be man to represent man, and that He must be God to represent God. But the more Scriptural statement is that Christ must be God as well as man in order to represent man. God needs no representative; man does. And Christ, as God-Man, as the Eternal Word become Flesh, died for sinners, was their representative and substitute under the Law. — Behrends.]

语法none other than…：『不是别人／别物，正是……』；which place covers also our own：which + 名词的关系结构（而这位置……），正式文体；God needs no representative; man does：does 替代 needs one，省略式对比。

中【摘录】关于基督之死的圣经陈述中，有两大事实凸显出来。其一，他为我们死，站在我们的位置上，作我们的『遮盖』，从而亲身领受神律法被干犯的刑罚。他作我们的替代者，不是借着位置的互换，而是借着遮盖：他站在自己的位置上，而这位置也遮盖了我们的位置。其二，那为我们死的，正是我们存在的成因——被钉十字架的，不是别人，正是那位创造诸世界的。人类的根不是亚当，乃是基督；神在创造中的执行者，也就是神在救赎中的执行者。他在赎罪之工中是人的代表与替代者，同时又是我们存在的创造性源头——代表的职分正植根于创造的关系。这里没有罪责的归算，没有刑罚的转嫁，也没有以一种刑罚顶替另一种刑罚：基督在自觉被父离弃的极痛之中，忍受并耗尽了那作为律法被干犯之本刑的死；而他是站在遮盖我们位置的地位上忍受这死的。人们常说：基督必须是人，才能代表人；必须是神，才能代表神。但更合乎圣经的说法是：基督必须既是神又是人，才能代表人。神不需要代表；人才需要。而基督，作为神而人者、作为道成肉身的永恒之道，为罪人死，在律法之下作了他们的代表与替代者。——贝伦兹（Behrends）

EN[At last, on the cross, it is still the heavenly temper which appears, and which gives to that its immortal significance. It is the temper of abhorrence of sin, yet of infinite longing for the sinner; the temper which honors and magnifies the absolute holiness that is God's eternal law for the universe, yet which reaches, even with agonized face and bleeding hands, after the meanest and vilest offender; the temper ready to bear even intolerable shames and pains in order to bring wandering spirits within the gates, yet which still makes repentance an absolute condition of its proposed blessings, and which will give unspeakable attestation to the authority of righteousness before any offer of forgiveness is made. It is not any sense of a commercial transaction there completed which gives to the cross its tender and sublime appeal to the world. It is not when viewed in legal or forensic relations, important as these are, that it most deeply stirs the heart. When this is seen, an appropriate response in us is inspired, the mission of Christ for us is answered, and a light flows back on all that had wonderfully gone before—to the advent itself, to the prophecies which had foretold His coming. — R. 中【摘录】最后，在十字架上显出来的，仍是那属天的性情，正是它赋予十字架不朽的意义。那是憎恶罪、却对罪人怀着无穷渴慕的性情；是尊崇并高举那作为宇宙永恒法则的绝对圣洁、却又带着痛楚的面容和流血的双手去够着最卑微最败坏之罪人的性情；是甘愿承受难以忍受的羞辱与苦痛、为把漂流的灵魂带进门内，却仍以悔改为其所应许之福分绝对条件、并且在发出任何赦免之先必先向公义的权威作出无可言喻之见证的性情。使十字架对世界发出温柔而崇高呼召的，并不是什么在那里完成了的商业交易的观念；也不是从法律或司法关系去看它的时候——尽管这些关系确实重要——它最深地打动人心；而在于它所作的启示——独一无二、至高无上、面向普世、面向万代——启示出天上对罪之罪责及其无可测度之恶果的判断，启示出神无瑕的纯洁，启示出那甘愿舍弃宝座与荣耀去拯救失丧者的舍己之爱。人一看见这个，天上诸世界最超越的方面便向我们敞开解明；人一看见这个，里面合宜的回应便被激发，基督为我们所负的使命便得了应答，一道光便回照在此前一切奇妙的预备之上——回照到降临本身，回照到预言他来临的众预言。——R. S. 斯托尔斯（Storrs）
