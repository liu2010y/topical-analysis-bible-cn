标题 / Title

ENBACKSLIDERS, BACKSLIDINGS.

中退后背道的人；背道（复数：屡次的背道）。

一、背道者的光景与神的医治 / The Backslider and God's Healing

ENProv. 14:14. The backslider in heart shall be filled with his own ways. VI. 323.

BSBProverbs 14:14 The backslider in heart receives the fill of his own ways, but a good man is rewarded for his ways.

中箴言 14:14 —— 心中背道的，必满得自己的结果。

ENJer. 2:19. VIII. 405. Jer. 3:22; 4:3. VIII. 415, 6. Jer. 14:7. VIII. 464.

BSBJeremiah 3:22 “Return, O faithless children, and I will heal your faithlessness.” “Here we are. We come to You, for You are the LORD our God.

中另参：耶利米书 2:19（你自己的恶必惩治你，你背道的事必责备你）；3:22（你们这背道的儿女啊，回来吧！我要医治你们背道的病）；4:3；14:7（我们的罪孽……我们本是多次背道，得罪了你）。

ENHosea 14:4. I will heal their backsliding; I will love them freely. IX. 378, 9.

BSBHosea 14:4 I will heal their apostasy; I will freely love them, for My anger has turned away from them.

中何西阿书 14:4 —— 我必医治他们背道的病，甘心爱他们。

二、责备与劝勉之言 / Words of Rebuke and Counsel

ENRev. 2:5, 14-16, 20; 3:1, 2, 15-18.

BSBRevelation 2:5 Therefore, keep in mind how far you have fallen. Repent and perform the deeds you did at first. But if you do not repent, I will come to you and remove your lampstand from its place.

中启示录 2:5（回想你是从哪里坠落的，并要悔改，行起初所行的事）；2:14-16, 20；3:1-2（按名你是活的，其实是死的……要警醒）；3:15-18（你既如温水，也不冷也不热……）。

ENJer. 4:3. Break up your fallow ground. VIII. 416. Hosea 10:12. IX. 365.

BSBJeremiah 4:3 For this is what the LORD says to the men of Judah and Jerusalem: “Break up your unplowed ground, and do not sow among the thorns.

中耶利米书 4:3 —— 要开垦你们的荒地，不要撒种在荆棘中。何西阿书 10:12 —— 开垦你们的荒地，因为现今正是寻求耶和华的时候。【OCR 注：原书 "Rosea" 为 Hosea 之误。】
