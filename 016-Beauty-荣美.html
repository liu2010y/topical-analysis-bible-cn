标题 / Title

ENBEAUTY; Beautiful.

中荣美；美好。

一、耶和华的荣美 / Of Jehovah

ENPs. 27:4. To behold the beauty of the Lord. IV. 204. Ps. 90:17. Let the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us. V. 114-117. Isa. 33:17. The King in His beauty. VIII. 165, 166. Zech. 9:17. How great is His beauty! IX. 592.

BSBPsalm 27:4 One thing I have asked of the LORD; this is what I desire: to dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to gaze on the beauty of the LORD and seek Him in His temple.

语法参：祈愿虚拟语气（The Lord bless thee） → 017 Let the beauty … be upon us：Let 式祈愿（愿荣美归于我们）。
语法How great is His beauty!：how + 形容词的感叹句，主谓倒装。

中诗篇 27:4 —— 瞻仰他的荣美。90:17 —— 愿主我们神的荣美归于我们身上。以赛亚书 33:17 —— 你的眼必见王的荣美。撒迦利亚书 9:17 —— 他的恩慈何等大！他的荣美何其盛！

二、圣洁的妆饰 / Of Holiness

EN2 Chron. 20:21. Praise the beauty of holiness. VII. 295. Ps. 110:3. In the beauty of holiness. V. 234-237. Ps. 29:2. Worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness. IV. 220-223. Ps. 96:9. V. 152.

BSBPsalm 29:2 Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness.

中历代志下 20:21 —— 赞美耶和华……穿上圣洁的礼服（直译：赞美圣洁的妆饰）。诗篇 110:3 —— 以圣洁的妆饰为衣。29:2 —— 以圣洁的妆饰（原文或作以圣洁为妆饰）敬拜耶和华。另参 96:9。

三、锡安圣所的华美 / Of the Sanctuary in Zion

ENPs. 50:2. Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined. IV. 357. Ps. 96:6. Strength and beauty are in His Sanctuary. V. 150, 151. Isa. 60:13. To beautify the place of My Sanctuary. VIII. 349.

BSBPsalm 50:2 From Zion, perfect in beauty, God shines forth.

语法参：同位语结构 → 001 Zion, the perfection of beauty：the perfection of beauty 是 Zion 的同位语（全美的锡安）。

中诗篇 50:2 —— 从全美的锡安中，神已经发光了。96:6 —— 有能力与华美在他圣所。以赛亚书 60:13 —— 修饰我圣所之地（使我圣所之地得荣耀）。

四、报佳音者脚踪的佳美 / Of the Advancing Steps of Christ's Heralds

ENIsa. 52:7. How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings of good! VIII. 272.

BSBIsaiah 52:7 How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of those who bring good news, who proclaim peace, who bring good tidings, who proclaim salvation, who say to Zion, “Your God reigns!”

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 How beautiful … are the feet：感叹句中表语前置＋主谓倒装（= the feet … are how beautiful）。
语法参：him that / them that = he who / those who → 001 him that bringeth good tidings = him who brings good news（那报好信息的人）。

中以赛亚书 52:7 —— 那报佳音、传平安、报好信、传救恩的……这人的脚登山何等佳美！

五、神所造万物的美好 / Of God's Natural Creation

ENEccles. 3:11. He hath made everything beautiful in its time. VI. 456-458, 465.

BSBEcclesiastes 3:11 He has made everything beautiful in its time. He has also set eternity in the hearts of men, yet they cannot fathom the work that God has done from beginning to end.

语法参：宾语补足语结构 count it all joy → 003 made everything beautiful：make + 宾语 + 形容词补语（使万物成为美好）。

中传道书 3:11 —— 神造万物，各按其时成为美好。
