二、导言要点：结构与主题的合一 / Points Introductory: Structure and Unity

ENStructure and Elements; Writers and Writings. I. 9. III. 361. Unity of Teaching and Theme. I. 13-15, 51, 53. II. 11-14, 132-139. Christ the Centre of Unity. I. 51. Redemption the Central Theme. VII. 19. Rev. 19:10. XI. 763, 766, 786.

BSBRevelation 19:10 So I fell at his feet to worship him. But he told me, “Do not do that! I am a fellow servant with you and your brothers who rely on the testimony of Jesus. Worship God! For the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.”

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 纲要式条目：Christ (is) the Centre of Unity / Redemption (is) the Central Theme，均省略系动词。

中结构与要素；作者与著作。教训与主题的合一。基督是合一的中心；救赎是中心主题（启示录 19:10——预言中的灵意乃是为耶稣作见证）。

EN[The nature and method of salvation is the one all-important subject of Revelation. In the Old Testament it is the heart of the whole sacrificial and priestly system and ritual, on which the religion of Judaism rested. In the New Testament three of the principal epistles of Paul are devoted to the doctrine of salvation, guarding against the three errors into which Jew, Greek and Roman—the world-races of that age and the representative and typical men of all time—were peculiarly liable to fall. In the Epistle to the Galatians it is shown, for the Jew, the representative of religious forms and ceremonies, that man is not to be saved by the observance of the ceremonial law, but by faith in Christ and "circumcision of the heart." In the Epistles to the Corinthians it is made clear, to the Greek, the representative of reason and philosophy, that salvation is not to be obtained by human wisdom, but by faith in "Jesus Christ and Him crucified," "who of God is made unto us wisdom, and righteousness, and sanctification, and redemption." In the Epistle to the Romans it is demonstrated, for the Roman, the representative of activity and works and law, that salvation cannot be secured by any human acts or works, but that, "being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ." Of these three epistles, that to the Romans manifestly approaches nearest to being of universal application—partaking of the character of a systematic treatise, and in its sweep taking in the whole range of law, human and divine. The Epistle to the Romans may be taken as God's presentation, according to Paul, of the preacher's message in the business of saving mankind. — D. S. Gregory.]

语法参：介词前置的关系从句 to whom / of which → 001 the three errors into which Jew, Greek and Roman … were … liable to fall：介词 into 前置于 which（容易陷入的三种错谬）。
语法参：同位语结构 → 001 the Jew, the representative of religious forms and ceremonies：三个民族各带一个同位语说明其代表性。

中【摘录】救恩的性质与方法，是启示独一无二、至关重要的主题。在旧约，它是整个献祭与祭司体系及礼仪的核心——犹太教即安放于其上。在新约，保罗的三卷主要书信专论救恩要道，分别防备犹太人、希腊人、罗马人——那个时代的世界性民族，也是万世之人的代表与典型——各自特别容易陷入的三种错谬。加拉太书向犹太人（宗教形式与礼仪的代表）表明：人得救不在乎谨守礼仪律法，而在乎信基督并『心里的割礼』。哥林多前后书向希腊人（理性与哲学的代表）阐明：救恩不能凭人的智慧获得，而是凭信『耶稣基督并他钉十字架』——他『成为我们的智慧、公义、圣洁、救赎』。罗马书向罗马人（行动、事功与法律的代表）论证：救恩绝不能靠任何属人的行为或功德取得，而是『我们既因信称义，就藉着我们的主耶稣基督得与神相和』。这三卷书信中，罗马书显然最接近普世的适用——带着系统论著的性质，其纵览囊括属人与属神之法的全部范围。罗马书可视为神藉保罗对传道者信息的呈示，用于拯救人类的大业。——D. S. 格雷戈里（Gregory）

EN[The Bible itself, interlocked as it is in all its parts, receives its final demonstration as the one Book of God for the world, from the discovery of the Christ pervading it all—in history, prophecy, song and symbol, in narrative, argument, foreshadowing of the future. It is the book in which He appears, through which He speaks, and whose apparently fragmentary or incidental pages take a lustre from Him, like that of the common frayed threads of His Galilean raiment, when on the mount He appeared in His glory, and when not only "His face did shine as the sun," but even "His raiment was white and glistening." — R. S. Storrs.]

语法interlocked as it is in all its parts：『形容词/分词 + as + 主语 + be』的让步/原因插入结构（它本就各部环环相扣）；His face did shine：did + 原形的古体强调式（= shone）。

中【摘录】圣经本身——它的各部分本就环环相扣——之所以最终被证明为神赐给世界的独一之书，乃在于人发现基督贯穿其全部：在历史、预言、诗歌与表记里，在叙事、论证、对未来的预影里。它是他在其中显现的书，是他藉以说话的书；它那些看似零碎或附带的书页，都从他得着光彩——正如他那加利利衣袍上寻常的磨损线缕，当他在山上荣耀显现时，不但『脸面明亮如日头』，连『衣裳也洁白放光』。——R. S. 斯托尔斯（Storrs）

四、圣经与社会学 / Sociology and the Bible

EN[Sociology is the science of social well-being; it concerns the relations of men to each other in society. Society is a divine organism in which God lives, and through which He is carrying out His will. This organic conception of society is the result of Bible teaching; the Gospel gives the strongest impulse in social movements. The Bible is a sociological book. The Old Testament is a history and text-book of national life. The Psalms, the most fervid voice of the individual soul, rarely fail in some way to teach the social or national spirit. And the message of the Prophets—is it not to the nation, as the society that should embody God's will and be the means of God's blessing to the world? "Patriotism, strong as a man's passion and tender as a woman's love, is the key-note of every chapter of Jeremiah's prophecies." (Stalker.)

语法is it not to the nation…?：否定疑问的修辞问句（岂不是……吗）；strong as a man's passion and tender as a woman's love：形容词 + as 的两个比喻状语对仗。

中【摘录】社会学是关于社会福祉的科学，研究人在社会中彼此的关系。社会是一个神圣的有机体：神住在其中，并藉着它施行自己的旨意。这种对社会的有机理解正是圣经教导的结果；福音给社会运动提供最强大的推动力。圣经本是一部社会学之书：旧约是一部民族生活的历史与教科书；诗篇虽是个人灵魂最炽热的声音，却几乎无一不以某种方式教导社会与民族的精神；而众先知的信息——岂不正是对那作为『当体现神旨意、作神赐福世界之媒介』的社会——民族——说的吗？『爱国之情，烈如男子的激情，柔如妇人的爱，是耶利米预言每一章的主调。』（斯托克 Stalker 语）

ENThe New Testament is not less sociological. The Kingdom is the great word of Christ, and it is essentially social. The Sermon on the Mount, the Magna Charta of the new society, touches all the relations of man. Social duties arise from the Christian conception of manhood: "We are members one of another; wherefore, putting away falsehood, speak ye truth, each with his neighbor." Eph. 4:25. The second commandment in Christ's summary of the law is as important as the first: "Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself." Matt. 22:39. And Paul: "For the whole law is fulfilled in one word, even in this: Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself." Gal. 5:14. The Lord's Prayer is sociological. The test of the final judgment is sociological. Christ worked for the whole man, and His promise is of a redeemed humanity. — A. S. Hoyt.] See Practical Christian Sociology, by W. F. Crafts.

BSBEphesians 4:25 Therefore each of you must put off falsehood and speak truthfully to his neighbor, for we are all members of one another.

语法参：古英语第二人称代词 thou / thee / thy / ye → 001 speak ye truth / Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself：ye 作祈使主语；thou shalt + thy + thyself 一句集齐古体第二人称全套。

中新约的社会学色彩毫不逊色。『国度』是基督的大词，而国度本质上是社会性的。登山宝训——新社会的大宪章——触及人的一切关系。社会责任出自基督教对人之为人的理解：『我们是互相为肢体……所以你们要弃绝谎言，各人与邻舍说实话』（以弗所书 4:25）。基督总括律法时，第二条诫命与第一条同等重要：『要爱人如己』（马太福音 22:39）。保罗更说：『因为全律法都包在“爱人如己”这一句话之内了』（加拉太书 5:14）。主祷文是社会性的；末日审判的检验标准是社会性的。基督为整全的人工作，他所应许的是蒙救赎的人类全体。——A. S. 霍伊特（Hoyt）。另可读克拉夫茨（W. F. Crafts）《实用基督教社会学》。

五、地理与风土的见证 / Testimony of Geography and Exploration

ENTestimony of Geography, Exploration, and Social Customs. III. 21. See Archeology. [The Bible is the record of God's revelation of Himself to man in the way of grace. This revelation was made in connection with and through the history of a particular people, the children of Israel. The history of that people and God's revelation are closely intertwined. Now as a divine revelation implies a history, a history in turn implies a locality. It is Palestine. We go to that locality and study it and interrogate it, and by it prove the history, and by the history prove the revelation. If the locality, as pictured in the Book, be false, everything else is false; if the locality be true, everything else is true. The land existed for the covenant people of God, and the covenant people of God existed for the world; i.e., they existed to give the world the Book and the Christ of the Book. As an argument I point out certain remarkable correspondences between the land and the Book—correspondences which indicate the presence of the hand of God, and which illustrate an accuracy in the Book that can come only from the guidance and superintendence of God. These correspondences make God the God of the Book, and the Book the Book of God.

语法参：though / if 从句中的虚拟语气（动词用原形） → 003 If the locality … be false：if 从句用 be 原形，虚拟语气。

中地理、考察与风土人情的见证（另参主题词条：考古学 Archeology）。【摘录】圣经是神在恩典之路上向人启示自己的记录。这启示是联结着、并借着一个特定民族——以色列子民——的历史作成的。这民族的历史与神的启示紧密交织。既然神圣的启示预设一段历史，历史又反过来预设一处地域——这地域就是巴勒斯坦。我们前往那地，研究它、究问它：以地域证历史，再以历史证启示。倘若书中所描绘的地域是假的，其余一切皆假；倘若地域是真的，其余一切皆真。这地为神的约民存在，而神的约民为世界存在——也就是说，他们存在的目的，是把这书和书中的基督给予世界。作为论证，我要指出这地与这书之间若干显著的对应——这些对应显明神之手的同在，也彰显书中那种惟有出于神的引导与督理才可能有的精确。这些对应使神成为这书的神，使这书成为神的书。

EN1. The land and the Book correspond in that they are both universal. In Palestine the geologist finds all the rock formations of the earth. Between the tepid waters of the Salt Sea and the perpetual snows of Mount Hermon—which never lifts its white cap from its brow—you have packed all zones and climates, from the frigid belt to the tropical equator, and also all the flora and fauna of the earth. Palestine is the world in a nutshell. The Bible is a world-book made in a world-land. As the Jew is the miracle of history, even so the cosmopolitan land of the Jew is the miracle of geography. Sacred geography, you see, has an apologetic value: the land and the people and the Book constitute a trinity of truth, and the testimony of one is strengthened by the testimony of the others. Another element in the universality is its location amid other lands: with Egypt to its south, Babylonia and Assyria to its east, and Greece and Rome to its west, it was the very centre of civilization. A centre of the world's life only can grow a world's book of life.

语法参：as … so … 关联结构（正如……照样……） → 008 As the Jew is the miracle of history, even so the cosmopolitan land … is the miracle of geography：as… even so… 关联结构。
语法in a nutshell：习语『浓缩为一言/一处』（果壳里的世界）。

中一、这地与这书相应，因为二者都是普世性的。在巴勒斯坦，地质学家能找到地球上所有的岩层构造。从盐海（死海）的温水到黑门山从不摘下白帽的常年积雪之间，浓缩了从寒带到热带赤道的所有气候带，也汇集了地上全部的动植物群。巴勒斯坦是浓缩于果壳中的世界；圣经是在一块『世界之地』上写成的『世界之书』。犹太人既是历史的奇迹，犹太人那四通八达的土地照样是地理的奇迹。可见，圣地地理自有其护教价值：这地、这民、这书构成真理的三重见证，其中任何一方的见证都因另两方的见证而加强。其普世性的另一要素是它居于列邦之中的位置：南邻埃及，东接巴比伦与亚述，西望希腊罗马——它正是文明的中心。惟有世界生活的中心，才能长出世界的生命之书。

EN2. There is a remarkable correspondence between the statements of the Book and the physical features of the land. The Book weaves the physical features of the land into its statements—fearlessly, knowing that it can be refuted if its references are false. No book in all the literature of the world has as honest a face as the Bible. Its narratives are accompanied with all the minute circumstances of time and place and situation and distance; yet in no single instance has geographical incorrectness been detected. Dean Stanley says: "It is impossible not to be struck by the constant agreement between the recorded history and the natural geography of both the Old and the New Testament, and many of these coincidences are manifestly undesigned."

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 in no single instance has geographical incorrectness been detected：否定状语前置引起倒装（从无一例查出地理错误）。

中二、书中的陈述与这地的自然地貌之间有惊人的对应。这书无所畏惧地把土地的自然地貌编织进自己的叙述——明知一旦所引不实即可被驳倒。全世界的文学中，没有一部书的面孔像圣经这样诚实：它的叙事带着时间、地点、情境、距离的一切细节；然而至今没有查出过一处地理错误。斯坦利院长（Dean Stanley）说：『新旧约所载的历史与自然地理之间恒常的一致，令人不能不惊叹；而这些吻合之中，有许多显然是无意为之的。』

EN3. There is a confirmatory correspondence between the land and the prophecies of the Book which predict desolation for the land—which predicted desolation marks the features of the land at this very hour. Moses records this prophecy concerning the land in the event of the people proving faithless: "Your land shall be desolate and your cities waste." Looking out over the land, Dean Stanley says, "Palestine is a land of ruins." Jesus predicted the destruction of Jerusalem, and in the twenty-fourth chapter of Matthew describes minutely the horrors of its fall. History matches these one by one. Palestine is the Bible in geography. God be praised for the Holy Land, with its Jordan and Bethlehem and Hermon and Zion and Calvary and Olivet: for these are all indestructible pages of the divine and indestructible Book—witnesses that God has spoken to men, and lived among men, and wrought out salvation for men. — D. Gregg. Read "The Testimony of the Land to the Book," by David Gregg, D.D.]
