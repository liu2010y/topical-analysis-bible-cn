正文对照 / Text

ENBLESSINGS AND CURSES of the Law of Moses. Lev. 26:3-13. Deut. 27:15-26; 28:1-14. II. 678, 682. Also Responsive Threatenings. IX. 440, 472. See Woes; Condemnation.

BSBDeuteronomy 28:1, 2 1 “Now if you faithfully obey the voice of the LORD your God and are careful to follow all His commandments I am giving you today, the LORD your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth. 2 And all these blessings will come upon you and overtake you, if you will obey the voice of the LORD your God:

语法Responsive Threatenings：responsive 指申命记 27 章『利未人宣告、百姓齐声应答“阿们”』的应答式仪文；threatening 作名词『警告性的宣告』。

中摩西律法的祝福与咒诅。利未记 26:3-13（你们若遵行我的律例……我要赐福）；申命记 27:15-26（以巴路山上十二样咒诅，百姓应声说阿们）；28:1-14（你若留意听从耶和华你神的话……这以下的福必追随你，临到你身上）。另有应答式的警告（Responsive Threatenings）。另参主题词条：祸哉（Woes）；定罪（Condemnation）。【OCR 注：原书 "Also Re-" 与 "sponsive Threatenings" 为跨栏断词，已重建。】
