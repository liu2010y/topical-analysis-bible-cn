标题 / Title

ENCALL OF GOD TO MAN. Personal and Urgent; Ever Seeking to win Back to Obedience, Childship, and Heart-Fellowship.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 标题＋两个形容词与一个分词短语的纲要式说明（呼召是亲自的、恳切的、始终寻求挽回的）。

中神对人的呼召——亲自而恳切；始终寻求把人赢回到顺服、儿女身份与心灵相交之中。

一、始于人堕落之时 / Commenced at the Fall

ENGod sought Adam. Gen. 3:9. The Lord God called unto the man (Where art thou?). I. 186. God sought Cain, the first-born man and offender. Gen. 4:6, 7. The Lord said to Cain, If thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted? I. 209.

BSBGenesis 3:9 But the LORD God called out to the man, “Where are you?”

语法参：古英语第二人称代词 thou / thee / thy / ye → 001 Where art thou? / If thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted?：art = are、doest = do、shalt = shall 的第二人称形式；疑问句直接倒装、不借助 do。

中神寻找亚当。创世记 3:9 —— 耶和华神呼唤那人，对他说：『你在哪里？』神寻找该隐——头生的人、也是犯罪的人。创世记 4:6-7 —— 耶和华对该隐说：你若行得好，岂不蒙悦纳？

ENGod warns the evil generation of Noah, and announces a period of patient waiting. Gen. 6:3. The Lord said, My Spirit shall not strive with man for ever; yet shall his days be an hundred and twenty years. I. 228-231. The Covenant of God's forbearance announced to Noah. Gen. 9:8-17. The Covenant of Grace made with Abraham. Gen. 15:9, 10; 17:4-7. Gen. 22:16-18. In thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed. Renewed to Isaac, Gen. 26:3, 4; to Jacob, Gen. 28:13, 14.

BSBGenesis 6:3 So the LORD said, “My Spirit will not contend with man forever, for he is mortal; his days shall be 120 years.”

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 yet shall his days be an hundred and twenty years：yet 前置引起倒装；an hundred 为古拼写（= a hundred）。

中神警告挪亚时代邪恶的世代，并宣告一段忍耐等候的时期。创世记 6:3 —— 耶和华说：人既属乎血气，我的灵就不永远住在他里面（原书直译：我的灵不永远与人相争）；然而他的日子还可到一百二十年。神向挪亚宣告宽容之约（创世记 9:8-17，虹为记号）。与亚伯拉罕立恩典之约（创世记 15:9-10；17:4-7）。22:16-18 —— 地上万国都必因你的后裔得福。此约向以撒重申（26:3-4），向雅各重申（28:13-14）。

五、结语：神永远是先爱、先寻找、先呼召的那一位 / God Ever First to Love, to Seek, to Call

ENAll these words of direct pleading show that God has ever been first to love (Jer. 31:3. I have loved thee with an everlasting love), first to seek, and first to call to the salvation promised at the Fall, through His own sacrifice. He gives His heart to every sinning child, before He pleads, "My son, give Me thy heart!" He expresses His good will to men in the abundance of natural and providential gifts, gratifying every sense and supplying every need. Matt. 5:45. Ps. 145:16.

BSBJeremiah 31:3 The LORD appeared to us in the past, saying: “I have loved you with an everlasting love; therefore I have drawn you with loving devotion.

语法first to love … first to seek, and first to call：三重排比（不定式作后置定语）；gratifying every sense and supplying every need：两个现在分词短语作伴随状语。

中这一切直接恳劝的话表明：神永远是先爱的那一位（耶利米书 31:3 —— 我以永远的爱爱你），先寻找的那一位，也是先呼召人来领受那在堕落之时便已应许、借他自己牺牲而成就之救恩的那一位。在他恳求『我儿，要将你的心归我』之先，他已经把自己的心给了每一个犯罪的儿女。他借着自然与护理之恩的丰盛厚赐——满足每一种感官、供应每一样需要——向人表明他的美意（马太福音 5:45——他叫日头照好人也照歹人；诗篇 145:16——你张手，使有生气的都随愿饱足）。

ENHis very law uttered from Sinai is prefaced upon an assurance of past goodness and mercy, and followed by many pledges (conditioned only on obedience) of all possible mercy and blessing. Thus the Commandments (all instinct with promise) impressively voice His call to men. And the chief central symbol of the Ceremonial Institutes, the Altar for Sacrifice—signifying His approach to man, not man's approach to Him—gives the most signal and sublime emphasis to His Call. II. 362. The altar an Oracle of Grace. And the whole Bible is filled with the spirit of this vital truth and fact. It finds utterance in manifold forms—in History, Psalm and Proverb, Prophecy and Epistle. Examples: Ps. 34:8. Prov. 23:26. 2 Cor. 6:1, 2. XI. 368, 9.

BSB2 Corinthians 6:1, 2 1 As God’s fellow workers, then, we urge you not to receive God’s grace in vain. 2 For He says: “In the time of favor I heard you, and in the day of salvation I helped you.” Behold, now is the time of favor; now is the day of salvation!

语法all instinct with promise：instinct 此处为形容词古义『充满，洋溢』（= filled with），与名词『本能』不同；His approach to man, not man's approach to Him：镜像对仗。
语法参：省略结构（省略主语、系动词 be 等） → 002 The altar an Oracle of Grace = The altar is an Oracle…：省略系动词的纲要句。

中连他在西奈山颁布的律法，也以过往恩慈怜悯的申明为前言，又以诸多应许（惟以顺服为条件）的怜悯与赐福为后随。这样，十诫（字字都洋溢着应许）有力地发出他对人的呼召。而礼仪制度的中心表记——献祭的坛——表明的是神向人走近、而非人向神走近，这就给他的呼召以最显著、最崇高的强调（祭坛乃是恩典的神谕所）。整部圣经都充满这一重大真理与事实的精神：它借历史、诗篇、箴言、预言、书信等多种形式发声。例证：诗篇 34:8（你们要尝尝主恩的滋味，便知道他是美善）；箴言 23:26（我儿，要将你的心归我）；哥林多后书 6:1-2（看哪，现在正是悦纳的时候）。【OCR 注：原书 "1 Cor. 6:1, 2" 依卷页号与文义推定为 2 Cor. 6:1-2。】
