三、讲论・对谈・简短话语 / III. Discourses, Colloquies, Brief Addresses and Sayings

ENMainly Subjective and Personal—a Progressive Series of Self-Disclosures. (Chronologically arranged.) Colloquy with Nathanael, John 1:47-51. With the Jews at Jerusalem, John 2:13-22. Discourse with Nicodemus, John 3:1-21—a clear and complete compendium of the Work of the Trinity in Redemption. Colloquy with the Woman of Samaria, John 4:7-26—God a Spirit, and worship of the heart; declares Himself Messiah. Discourse and Colloquy at Nazareth, Luke 4:17-27—announces His Messiahship. Colloquies with Jews at Jerusalem, John 5:17-47—proves His Divine Sonship and authority. Colloquy with Pharisees, Luke 6:1-11—expounds the true Law of the Sabbath.

BSBJohn 3:3 Jesus replied, “Truly, truly, I tell you, no one can see the kingdom of God unless he is born again.”

语法colloquy：对谈，问答（col- 共同 + loqu 说话，与 colloquial 同源）；compendium：纲要，概略（GRE 词）。

中主要是主观而位格性的——一个渐进的自我启示系列。（按年代排列。）与拿但业对谈（约 1:47-51——你们要看见天开了）；与耶路撒冷的犹太人（约 2:13-22——拆毁这殿，我三日内要再建立起来）；与尼哥底母的讲论（约 3:1-21——三一神救赎之工清晰完备的纲要：人若不重生，就不能见神的国）；与撒玛利亚妇人对谈（约 4:7-26——神是灵，拜他的必须用心灵和诚实拜他；明言自己是弥赛亚）；拿撒勒会堂的讲论（路 4:17-27——宣告自己的弥赛亚职分：主的灵在我身上）；与耶路撒冷犹太人的对谈（约 5:17-47——证明自己神儿子的身份与权柄）；与法利赛人论安息日的真义（路 6:1-11——人子是安息日的主）。

ENSermon on the Mount (Objective), Matt. chaps. 5-7. Shows the Kingdom of God as the aim and the substance of the Old Dispensation, and its consummation as the end and crown of the New; presents the point of connection and transition between Judaism and Christianity.

BSBMatthew 5:17 Do not think that I have come to abolish the Law or the Prophets. I have not come to abolish them, but to fulfill them.

语法Dispensation：神学用语『时代，经纶』（神在不同阶段治理的安排）；consummation：成全，完满终局。

中登山宝训（客观性的讲论），马太福音 5–7 章。显明神的国是旧约时代的目标与实质，而国度的成全是新约时代的终点与冠冕；提出犹太教与基督教之间衔接过渡的交点。

EN[In the Sermon on the Mount, which is the moral miracle of the gospels, we have simply, sublimely set before us the celestial conception of noble character, noble living, in man on the earth. It is spoken—the world itself has to recognize this—without the least effort, in words as simple as a child's, though more majestic than those of kings. It is radically diverse, not only from the prevalent customs of mankind, but from the choicest maxims of enlightened human prudence; so that the highest and purest human ethics only climb toward and imperfectly attain the heights on which it moves without effort. It has neither been reached, nor is it fortified, by large discussion, subtle analysis, the marshaling of rare philosophical precepts. It is only announced, as too evident in itself to need support, for spirits attuned to celestial instruction. It is plainly the customary thought of the worlds from which child and angels had come, put into forms of human speech, and sent abroad to shed its moral splendor on mankind. So the very speech of the earth is glorified by it; the life of the world takes higher light and nobler impulse from the summit-thought of spheres beyond sight, concerning the beauty of duty, the ultimate achievement of moral greatness, the blessedness of self-sacrifice. Not otherwise can this great discourse for the world, from the Horns of Hattin, be rightly understood. — R. S. Storrs.]

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Not otherwise can this great discourse … be rightly understood：否定状语前置引起倒装（惟有如此才能正解）。
语法It has neither been reached, nor is it fortified, by…：neither…nor… 连接两个谓语，nor 后倒装；too evident in itself to need support：too…to… 结构（自明得无需支撑）。

中【摘录】登山宝训是福音书中的道德奇迹。在其中，那关于人在地上高贵品格、高贵生活的属天构想，被简朴而崇高地陈明在我们面前。它的宣讲——连世界自己也不得不承认——毫不费力，用词简单如孩童的话，却比君王的言语更威严。它与人类通行的习俗根本不同，甚至与开明的人类智虑最精粹的格言也根本不同；以致最高最纯的人类伦理，也只能朝着它毫不费力地行走于其上的高度攀爬，且攀之不及。它既不是靠宏论、精析、罗列稀世哲学箴规达到的，也不靠这些来设防；它只是被宣告出来——对于调准了心弦聆听属天教诲的灵魂，它自明得无需任何支撑。它分明就是孩童与天使所来自的诸世界里的寻常心思，被放进人类语言的形式，播散出来，把道德的光辉洒向人类。于是地上的言语因它得了荣耀；世界的生活从那目不能及之诸天的峰顶思想，领受更高的光和更尊贵的动力——关乎本分之美、道德伟大的终极成就、舍己的福分。若不这样理解，这篇从哈廷双角山发出、面向全世界的大讲论，便无法得到正解。——R. S. 斯托尔斯（Storrs）
