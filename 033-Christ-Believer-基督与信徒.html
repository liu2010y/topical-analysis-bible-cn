导言 / Introduction

ENThe sublimest conceptions presented to human thought respect: 1. The Being, Character, and Moral Working of the Self-existent, Self-sufficient God; 2. The immortal nature and destiny of created Man; and 3. The Relation of God and Man, in this world and the world to come. In the Bible, our only source of knowledge of these high themes, enough is revealed, in the simple form of fact, for all our spiritual needs. Especially, patent upon all its pages, lies the supreme fact of a gracious Redemption for enslaved and sinful man, provided and proffered through the voluntary self-sacrifice of an Incarnate God.

语法patent upon all its pages, lies the supreme fact…：表语 patent 前置＋谓语 lies 提前的倒装（昭然于每一页之上的，是……）；patent 此处义『显明的，昭然的』，非『专利』。

中呈于人类思想面前最崇高的观念，关乎三件事：一、自有永有、自足无缺之神的存在、品格与道德作为；二、受造之人不朽的本性与归宿；三、神与人在今世和来世的关系。圣经是我们认识这些崇高主题的惟一源头；其中以朴素的事实形态所启示的，已足敷我们一切属灵的需要。尤其昭然于全书每一页之上的，是那至高的事实：为被奴役的罪人预备并奉上的恩典救赎——借着一位道成肉身之神甘愿的舍己。

ENAnd equally patent and radiant throughout these pages we read the glad fact that, with every penitent and believing soul who humbly and obediently accepts the proffered grace of Christ, there is established by the Divine Love a new, abiding and transforming relation with God in Christ. The fruit of this relation appears in the soul's increasing likeness in character, its growing oneness with Christ in thought, desire, sympathy, aim, and affection. This relation of close dependence and fellowship is indeed vital to us, since it takes hold upon every fibre of our spiritual being, directs and governs our present life, moulds and matures our character for an ultimate perfected likeness to, and an open fellowship with, God. Isa. 57:15. I dwell with him that is of a contrite and humble spirit. VIII. 323, 324. 2 Pet. 1:4. Partakers of the Divine nature. XI. 671.

BSBIsaiah 57:15 For thus says the One who is high and lifted up, who inhabits eternity, whose name is Holy: “I dwell in a high and holy place, and with the oppressed and humble in spirit, to restore the spirit of the lowly and revive the heart of the contrite.

语法参：him that / them that = he who / those who → 001 him that is of a contrite and humble spirit：那心灵痛悔谦卑的人。
语法likeness to, and … fellowship with, God：双介词共宾结构（参 030 词条同注）——like 之于 to、fellowship 之于 with，共享宾语 God。

中同样昭然发光于这些书页之间的，是那令人欢喜的事实：凡谦卑顺服地接受基督所奉恩典的痛悔相信的灵魂，神圣之爱都在其身上建立起一种在基督里与神崭新、恒久且更新人的关系。这关系的果实显于灵魂品格上日增的肖似，显于其在思想、愿望、同情、目标与爱慕上与基督日深的合一。这种紧密倚赖与相交的关系对我们确是性命攸关：它抓住我们属灵存在的每一根纤维，指引管治我们今下的生活，模塑成熟我们的品格，以致最终完全像神、与神坦然相交。以赛亚书 57:15 —— 我住在至高至圣的所在，也与心灵痛悔谦卑的人同居。彼得后书 1:4 —— 就得与神的性情有份。

一、这关系显于基督救赎主的全部职分工作 / I. Disclosed in His Office-Work as Redeemer

ENThe Relation of Christ to the Believer is disclosed in His entire Office-Work as Redeemer. See GOD (Office Work of the Son in Redemption). The corresponding Relation of the Believer to Christ is equally shown in the responsive feeling and action demanded of the believer—the required response of faith, repentance, surrender, obedience, love, and consecration; and this is confirmed by the actual effects wrought within the true believer's soul as the result of this penitent trust and self-devotion. For full textual treatment, see CHRISTIAN LIFE (Christian Experience).

中基督对信徒的关系，显明于他作救赎主的全部职分工作（参主题词条：神——子在救赎中的职分工作）。信徒对基督相应的关系，则同样显明于向信徒所要求的回应性情感与行动——即所要求的信心、悔改、降服、顺从、爱与奉献的回应；而这又被真信徒灵魂里因这痛悔的信靠与献身实际产生的果效所印证。详尽经文见主题词条：基督徒生活（基督徒经历）。

三、主临别讲论中的奇妙话语：葡萄树与枝子 / III. The Vine and Its Branches

ENThese Figures find their fullest emphasis, and our precious theme its deepest interpretation, in Our Lord's Marvelous Words in His Last Discourse—the Figure of the Vine and its Branches. John 15:1-11. Abide in Me, and I in you. I am the Vine, ye are the branches. He that abideth in Me, and I in him, the same beareth much fruit; for apart (severed) from Me ye can do nothing. If ye abide in Me, and My words abide in you, ask whatsoever ye will, and it shall be done unto you. Even as the Father hath loved Me, I also have loved you; abide ye in My love. If ye keep My commandments, ye shall abide in My love. These things have I spoken unto you, that My joy may be in you. X. 489-491. John 14:20, 23. Ye shall know that I am in My Father, and ye in Me, and I in you. My Father will love him, and We will come unto him, and make Our abode with him.

BSBJohn 15:4, 5 4 Remain in Me, and I will remain in you. Just as no branch can bear fruit by itself unless it remains in the vine, neither can you bear fruit unless you remain in Me. 5 I am the vine and you are the branches. The one who remains in Me, and I in him, will bear much fruit. For apart from Me you can do nothing.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 These things have I spoken unto you：宾语前置引起倒装（这些事我已对你们说了）。
语法ask whatsoever ye will：will 作实义动词『愿意』（凡你们所愿意的）；the same beareth much fruit：the same 古用法复指前述之人（这人就多结果子）。

中以上诸比方最充分的强调、我们这宝贵主题最深刻的解释，见于我们的主在临别讲论中的奇妙话语——葡萄树与枝子的比方。约翰福音 15:1-11 —— 你们要常在我里面，我也常在你们里面。我是葡萄树，你们是枝子。常在我里面的，我也常在他里面，这人就多结果子；因为离了我，你们就不能做什么。你们若常在我里面，我的话也常在你们里面，凡你们所愿意的，祈求，就给你们成就。我爱你们，正如父爱我一样，你们要常在我的爱里。你们若遵守我的命令，就常在我的爱里。这些事我已经对你们说了，是要叫我的喜乐存在你们心里。14:20, 23 —— 到那日，你们就知道我在父里面，你们在我里面，我也在你们里面。……我父也必爱他，并且我们要到他那里去，与他同住。

ENThese are wonder-words, fraught with a depth, breadth, and intensity of meaning that may well surcharge the heart of every thoughtful, responsive believer with immeasurable thankfulness and joy. Clearly and unqualifiedly Christ declares that He comes into the believer's life and takes the believer into His life, and this abidingly, both here and in the eternal hereafter. He affirms that He will establish between Himself and the believer an indissoluble union or oneness of spiritual life. This union consists, not in an organic blending of the two natures, Divine and human, but in such an identity and mutual responsiveness of thought, desire, affection and aim, touching God and godlike living, as constitute a real oneness of the Divine and human spirit. This is the cardinal fact, the blessed truth, that underlies and vitalizes all other facts and truths of the believer's spiritual experience.

语法not in …, but in such … as constitute …：not…but… 对比中嵌 such…as… 关系结构（不在于混融，而在于那构成真合一的同一与呼应）；fraught with：充满（常带庄重色彩）。

中这些是充满奇妙的话语，其意义之深、之广、之烈，足以使每一个肯思想、肯回应的信徒心中涨满无可计量的感恩与喜乐。基督清楚而毫无保留地宣告：他进入信徒的生命，也把信徒接入他的生命——并且是恒久如此，今世与永世皆然。他断言要在自己与信徒之间建立一种不可解除的联合，即属灵生命的合一。这联合不在于神人二性有机地混融，而在于在关乎神、关乎像神而活的事上，思想、愿望、爱慕与目标如此地同一、如此地彼此呼应，以致构成神的灵与人的灵真实的合一。这就是那根本的事实、蒙福的真理——信徒属灵经历的其他一切事实与真理，都以它为底基、因它而有生气。

ENAbiding in Him is imposed upon us as a commanded duty. Yet, as Andrew Murray writes: "It is not a work that we have to do, but a consenting to let Him do all for us, in us, and through us. It is a work that He does, under the mighty impulse of His redeeming love. Our part is simply to yield, to trust, and to wait for what He has engaged to perform." Of the Master's figure he says: "All the vine possesses belongs to the branches. All Christ's fullness is for the believer. And all that the branch possesses belongs to the vine. The branch exists to bear fruit in honor of the vine from which it derives its life. So every power of the believer, every thought and feeling, belong to Jesus, that from Him and for Him he may bring forth fruit to the glory of His name."

语法what He has engaged to perform：engage 此处义『承诺，担保』（他所承诺成就的）；that from Him and for Him he may bring forth fruit：目的从句中双介词短语前置。

中『常在他里面』是加给我们的一道命令、一项本分。然而正如慕安德烈（Andrew Murray）所写：『这不是一件要我们去做的工，而是一种应允——让他为我们、在我们里面、借着我们成就一切。这是他在其救赎之爱的大能推动下所做的工；我们的本分只是降服、信靠，并等候他所承诺要成就的。』论到夫子的比方，他说：『葡萄树所有的一切都属于枝子——基督一切的丰盛都是为着信徒的；而枝子所有的一切也都属于葡萄树——枝子存在，就是为着荣耀那赐它生命的树而结果子。照样，信徒的每一分力量、每一个意念与情感都属于耶稣，好叫他从他、也为他结出果子，使他的名得荣耀。』

EN[The greatest hindrance to the believer's entire surrender is the old self. Only as the natural man is crucified with Christ is the new self, the spiritual man, set free for a complete surrender to God, offered "a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God" (Rom. 12:1). And though the old self will continue to exert its hindering force as long as the believer lives, still, under the mastering might of "the Spirit of life in Christ," it can be so kept in its crucifixion place, and under its sentence of death, that it shall never again obtain dominion over him. Sadly true it is that, from unwatchfulness, and from weakness engendered by past habitual self-indulgence, the believer, under an occasional sore temptation, "overtaken in a trespass" (Gal. 6:1), is subjected to the temporary control of the old nature. But so soon as the new nature penitently retraces its steps, its power is re-established over the soul through the inworking of the restored Christ-life. In view of these vital facts the believer is called, not only to unsleeping trust and consecration, but to ceaseless vigilance, prayer, and resistant struggle at every point of exposure to old weaknesses and temptations. Above all should his supreme desire and petition be that Christ should fill the place, and exert the control, of the expelled self within his whole spiritual being—that "Christ may dwell in his heart by faith," and may be "formed within him, the hope of glory": that Christ may be his life. — B.] See Flesh and Spirit.

中【作者按语】信徒完全降服最大的拦阻是旧我。惟有属血气的人与基督同钉十字架，那新我——属灵的人——才得释放，可以完全降服于神，献上『当作活祭，是圣洁的，是神所喜悦的』（罗马书 12:1）。旧我固然要在信徒有生之年不断施加拦阻之力，然而在『赐生命圣灵』（罗 8:2）辖制之能底下，它可以被牢牢按在受钉之处、按在死刑判决之下，永不再辖制信徒。可悲的是，确有这样的事实：由于失了警醒、由于往日惯性的放纵所生的软弱，信徒会在偶发的猛烈试探之下『被过犯所胜』（加拉太书 6:1），一时落入旧性的辖制。但新性只要痛悔回转，其权能便借着复归之基督生命在里面的运行重新确立于灵魂之上。鉴于这些切身的事实，信徒蒙召不仅要有不眠的信靠与奉献，还要有不歇的警醒、祷告，并在每一个暴露于旧软弱旧试探的破口上作抵抗的争战。而至高无上的心愿与祈求应当是：让基督来填满被逐出的旧我之位、执掌其权，充满他整个属灵的存在——使『基督因信住在他心里』，并『成形在他里面，成了有荣耀的盼望』：使基督作他的生命。——巴特勒（B.）。另参主题词条：肉体与圣灵（Flesh and Spirit）。
