正文对照 / Text

ENCIVIL GOVERNMENT. Rom. 13:1-6. The powers that be are ordained of God. … He beareth not the sword in vain: for he is a minister of God, an avenger for wrath to him that doeth evil. 1 Pet. 2:13, 17. Be subject to every ordinance of man for the Lord's sake. … Honor all men. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honor the king. Titus 3:1. Christ's teaching: X. 425 (Render unto Caesar the things that are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's). Prayer for all in authority: 1 Tim. 2:1, 2. For kings and all that are in high place; that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness. See Law of Sinai (Civil Law).

BSBRomans 13:1 Everyone must submit himself to the governing authorities, for there is no authority except that which is from God. The authorities that exist have been appointed by God.

语法The powers that be：习语化的古体（现存的当权者）——that be 为古虚拟形式，此短语沿用至今；beareth not the sword in vain：古体否定（不是空空地佩剑）。
语法参：him that / them that = he who / those who → 001 an avenger … to him that doeth evil：那作恶的人（that 指人）。

中世上的政权。罗马书 13:1-6 —— 在上有权柄的，人人当顺服他，因为没有权柄不是出于神的，凡掌权的都是神所命的。……他不是空空地佩剑，他是神的用人，是伸冤的，刑罚那作恶的。彼得前书 2:13, 17 —— 你们为主的缘故，要顺服人的一切制度。……务要尊敬众人，亲爱教中的弟兄，敬畏神，尊敬君王。提多书 3:1（提醒众人叫他们顺服作官的、掌权的）。基督的教导：凯撒的物当归给凯撒，神的物当归给神（太 22:21）。为一切在位的祷告：提摩太前书 2:1-2 —— 为君王和一切在位的，也该如此，使我们可以敬虔端正、平安无事地度日。另参主题词条：西奈律法（民事律）。
