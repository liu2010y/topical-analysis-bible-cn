标题 / Title

ENCOMFORT; CONSOLATION. From God. From His Word. From Godly Men.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 标题＋三个 From 短语的纲目（安慰的三重来源）。

中安慰；慰藉。出于神；出于圣道；出于敬虔的人——三个源头。

一、出于神 / From God

ENIsa. 40:1. Comfort ye, comfort ye My people, saith your God. Isa. 51:12. I, even I, am He that comforteth you. Isa. 61:2. To comfort all that mourn. Isa. 66:13. As one whom his mother comforteth, so will I comfort you. John 14:18. I will not leave you comfortless. Acts 9:31. Rom. 15:5. The God of patience and of comfort. 2 Cor. 1:5, 7. Our comfort aboundeth through Christ. 2 Thes. 2:16, 17. Our Lord Jesus Christ Himself, and God our Father … comfort your hearts.

BSBIsaiah 66:13 As a mother comforts her son, so will I comfort you, and you will be consoled over Jerusalem.”

语法参：as … so … 关联结构（正如……照样……） → 008 As one whom his mother comforteth, so will I comfort you：as…so… 关联＋so 后倒装（母亲怎样安慰，我也照样安慰）。
语法参：古英语第二人称代词 thou / thee / thy / ye → 001 Comfort ye, comfort ye My people：祈使句带 ye 且叠用（你们要安慰，安慰）——以赛亚书下半卷的开卷名句。
语法参：祈愿虚拟语气（The Lord bless thee） → 017 God our Father … comfort your hearts：comfort 用原形，祈愿语气（愿神安慰你们的心）。

中以赛亚书 40:1 —— 你们的神说：你们要安慰，安慰我的百姓。51:12 —— 惟有我，是安慰你们的。61:2 —— 安慰一切悲哀的人。66:13 —— 母亲怎样安慰儿子，我就照样安慰你们。约翰福音 14:18 —— 我不撇下你们为孤儿（英译：不撇下你们无人安慰）。使徒行传 9:31；罗马书 15:5 —— 但愿赐忍耐安慰的神……。哥林多后书 1:5, 7 —— 我们既多受基督的苦楚，就靠基督多得安慰。帖撒罗尼迦后书 2:16-17 —— 但愿我们主耶稣基督和那爱我们……的父神，安慰你们的心。【OCR 注：原书 "Acts 0:31" 依文义推定为 9:31。】

二、出于圣道 / From His Word

ENPs. 119:50. This is my comfort in my affliction; for Thy word hath quickened me. Rom. 15:4. That we through patience and comfort of the Scriptures might have hope. 1 Thes. 4:18. Comfort one another with these words.

BSBPsalm 119:50 This is my comfort in affliction, that Your promise has given me life.

中诗篇 119:50 —— 这话将我救活了，我在患难中，因此得安慰。罗马书 15:4 —— 叫我们因圣经所生的忍耐和安慰，可以得着盼望。帖撒罗尼迦前书 4:18 —— 所以，你们当用这些话彼此劝慰。

三、出于敬虔的人 / From Godly Men

EN2 Cor. 1:3, 4. The God of all comfort, Who comforteth us in all our affliction, that we may be able to comfort them that are in any affliction, through the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God. 2 Cor. 7:6, 7, 13. God, that comforteth the lowly, comforted us by the coming of Titus. Eph. 6:22. Col. 4:11. 1 Thes. 3:2. See Affliction.

BSB2 Corinthians 1:3, 4 3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassion and the God of all comfort, 4 who comforts us in all our troubles, so that we can comfort those in any trouble with the comfort we ourselves have received from God.

语法参：复合关系副词 whereby / wherewith / wherein → 002 the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God：wherewith = with which（我们自己所受的那安慰）；comforted of God 中 of 引施动者。
语法参：the + 形容词表一类人 → 001 God, that comforteth the lowly：the lowly——卑微丧气的人。

中哥林多后书 1:3-4 —— 赐各样安慰的神。我们在一切患难中，他就安慰我们，叫我们能用神所赐的安慰去安慰那遭各样患难的人。7:6-7, 13 —— 但那安慰丧气之人的神，藉着提多来安慰了我们。以弗所书 6:22；歌罗西书 4:11（他们也成了我的安慰）；帖撒罗尼迦前书 3:2。另参主题词条：苦难（Affliction，003）。
