二、J. Orr 摘录：确定的报应与明智的存疑 / Extract: Certain Retribution and a Wise Agnosticism

EN[Isaiah says (3:11), "Woe unto the wicked! it shall be ill with him: for the reward of his hands shall be given him." This is the great and fundamental principle of certain retribution for sin—a principle we cannot hold too clearly or too strongly. Whatever tends to tamper with this principle, or to weaken its hold upon the conscience, is alien to the true Christian view. By unalterable laws impressed upon the nature of man and on the universe, righteousness is life, and sin is inevitable misery and death. Omnipotence itself could not reverse this law—that so long as a sinner continues in his sin, he must suffer. On the other hand, where this principle is firmly grasped, there ought to be much room for difference of views on points which, from the nature of the case, are obscure. In regard to those things on which Scripture gives no light and is silent, our wisdom is to imitate its caution, and refrain from dogmatism. In respect of so appalling a subject as the future fate of the lost, there is room for a wise Agnosticism: I prefer to say that, so far as my light goes, I see no end—and there to stop.

语法we cannot hold too clearly or too strongly：cannot … too … 结构（再……也不为过）；Omnipotence itself could not reverse this law：拟人化抽象名词作主语（连全能本身也不能……）。

中【摘录】以赛亚说（3:11）：『恶人有祸了，他必遭灾难，因为要照自己手所行的受报应。』这就是罪必受报这一伟大而根本的原则——我们对它的持守，再清楚、再坚定也不为过。凡倾向于篡改这原则、或削弱它对良心之约束力的，都与真正的基督教观格格不入。按着铭刻在人性与宇宙之上的不可更改之律：公义即生命，罪即无可避免的愁苦与死亡。全能者自己也不能推翻这条定律——罪人只要仍在罪中，就必受苦。另一方面，凡稳固持定这一原则之处，对于那些就事情本性而言幽暗难明的问题，就理应容许观点的分歧。凡圣经不予光照、保持沉默的事，我们的智慧就是效法它的谨慎、戒绝武断。对于失丧者将来的结局这样令人战栗的题目，是容得下一种明智的存疑的：我宁愿说——就我所得的光而言，我看不见尽头——并且到此为止。

ENConcerning the untold millions who have never heard of Christ at all, we feel instinctively that the last word has not been—cannot be—spoken by us here. It may be said, with much truth, that for those who have the light there is no excuse: salvation has been put within their reach, and they have deliberately rejected it. But even here, are there not elements we dare not overlook? Men are responsible for the use they make of light; but how much here also is not due to the individual will—crossed as it is by influences from heredity, from environment, from up-bringing, from the pressure of events! God alone can disentangle the threads of freedom in the web of character and action, and say how much is a man's individual responsibility in the result. It is certain, from Christ's own statement, that in the judgment of Omniscience all these things are taken into account; that even in the administration of punishment there are gradations of penalty, proportionate to men's knowledge and opportunities (Luke 12:47, 48); that, as Paul says, a distinction is made between those who have "sinned without law" and those who have "sinned under law." — J. Orr.] For a judicious treatment of the so-called "Future Probation," see The Christian View of God and the World, by Professor Orr—an admirable work throughout. See Day of the Lord; Wicked; Woes.

语法the last word has not been—cannot be—spoken：插入的情态递进（还没有说出——也说不出来）；crossed as it is by influences：『分词 + as + it is』的原因/让步插入（那意志本被……牵制着）；are there not elements we dare not overlook?：否定反问＋dare 作情态动词。

中至于那从未听过基督之名的亿万之众，我们本能地感到：最后的话，我们在此地还没有说出——也说不出来。或者有人说（此话大有道理）：对已经得了光的人，再无可推诿——救恩已放在他们伸手可及之处，他们却蓄意拒绝了。然而即便在这里，岂没有我们不敢忽略的因素吗？人固然要为自己怎样使用所得之光负责；但这里又有多少并非出于个人意志——那意志本被遗传、环境、教养、时势的压力纵横交错地牵制着！惟有神能从品格与行为的织网中解开自由的丝线，说出结果之中有多少是一个人自己的责任。从基督亲口的话可以确定：在全知者的审判里，这一切都被考虑在内；甚至在刑罚的施行上也有轻重的等差，与人的知识和机会成比例（路加福音 12:47-48 —— 多受责打与少受责打）；正如保罗所说，『没有律法犯了罪的』与『在律法以下犯了罪的』是有分别的。——J. 奥尔（Orr）。关于所谓『来世试炼期』的持平讨论，参奥尔教授《神与世界的基督教观》——通篇俱佳之作。另参主题词条：主的日子；恶人；祸哉。【OCR 注：原书 "Luke 27:47, 48" 依引文推定为 12:47-48；著者名原书误作 Joseph Orr，通行作 James Orr。】
