正文对照 / Text

ENCONSCIENCE. Rom. 2:15. Their conscience bearing witness therewith, their thoughts one with another accusing or else excusing them. Rom. 9:1. 1 Cor. 8:7. Their conscience, being weak, is defiled. Titus 1:15. 1 Tim. 4:2. Seared in their own conscience as with a hot iron. 2 Cor. 1:12. The testimony of our conscience. Acts 23:1. I have lived before God in all good conscience. Acts 24:16. A conscience void of offence toward God and men. 1 Tim. 1:5, 19. A good conscience (with a pure heart and faith unfeigned).

BSBRomans 2:15 So they show that the work of the law is written on their hearts, their consciences also bearing witness, and their thoughts either accusing or defending them

语法Seared as with a hot iron：sear『烙焦』——良心被烙惯而失去知觉的名喻；faith unfeigned：形容词后置（无伪的信心，unfeigned = 不假装的）。

中良心。罗马书 2:15 —— 他们是非之心（原文作良心）同作见证，并且他们的思念互相较量，或以为是，或以为非。9:1（我的良心被圣灵感动，给我作见证）。哥林多前书 8:7 —— 他们的良心既然软弱，也就污秽了。提多书 1:15；提摩太前书 4:2 —— 这等人的良心如同被热铁烙惯了一般。哥林多后书 1:12 —— 我们所夸的是自己的良心。使徒行传 23:1 —— 我在神面前行事为人都是凭着良心。24:16 —— 对神对人，常存无亏的良心。提摩太前书 1:5, 19 —— 命令的总归就是爱，这爱是从清洁的心和无亏的良心、无伪的信心生出来的。

ENAnother's conscience: 1 Cor. 8:7-12; 10:25-29. (Conscience, I say, not thine own, but the other's.) 1 Pet. 2:19. For conscience toward God endure griefs. 1 Pet. 3:16, 21. See Accountability. [Reason and conscience abide with the soul as indestructible elements of human nature—the one enforcing the sense of dependence upon God, the other compelling the consciousness of obligation to Him. — B.]

BSB1 Peter 3:16 keeping a clear conscience, so that those who slander you may be put to shame by your good behavior in Christ.

语法the one … the other …：分指前述二者（理性与良心）的对举句式；enforcing / compelling：两个现在分词的平行修饰。

中他人的良心：哥林多前书 8:7-12（伤了弟兄软弱的良心，就是得罪基督）；10:25-29 —— 我说的良心不是你的，乃是他的。彼得前书 2:19 —— 因为人若因着良心对得起神（原文作因着向神的良心），就忍受冤屈的苦楚，这是可喜爱的。3:16, 21。另参主题词条：向神交账的责任（001）。【作者按语】理性与良心作为人性中不可摧毁的要素与灵魂同在：一个强化人对神的依赖之感，一个催逼人对神的责任之识。——巴特勒（B.）
