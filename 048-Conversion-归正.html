标题 / Title

ENCONVERSION. Steps in the Process.

中归正（回转归向神）。其进程中的诸步骤。（与 034 三之一『悔改与归正』相呼应：归正是 metanoia 的外在实行——实际的转身。）

一、注意与省思，生出为罪的自觉 / Attention and Reflection, Producing Conviction of Sin

ENDeut. 30:1. (Thou shalt call them to mind among all the nations.) Ezek. 3:27. He that heareth, let him hear. Ps. 119:59. I thought on my ways. Luke 15:17, 18. And when he came to himself, he said … I will arise and go to my father. Acts 9:5, 6. Who art Thou, Lord? And he, trembling, said, Lord, what wilt Thou have me to do?

BSBLuke 15:17, 18 17 Finally he came to his senses and said, ‘How many of my father’s hired servants have plenty of food, but here I am, starving to death! 18 I will get up and go back to my father and say to him, “Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you.

语法when he came to himself：习语『醒悟过来』（回到自己——浪子比喻的转折点）；Who art Thou, Lord?：art 与 Thou 搭配的古体疑问。

中申命记 30:1 —— 你在诸国中心里追念（这一切咒诅与祝福）。以西结书 3:27 —— 听的可以听。诗篇 119:59 —— 我思想我所行的道。路加福音 15:17-18 —— 他醒悟过来，就说：……我要起来，到我父亲那里去。使徒行传 9:5-6 —— 主啊，你是谁？他就战兢地说：主啊，你要我做什么？（钦定本文句；和合本此语见 22:10。）

二、决断与立行 / Decision and Instant Action

ENPs. 119:59, 60. I thought on my ways, and turned my feet unto Thy testimonies: I made haste, and delayed not. Luke 15:20. And he arose, and came to his father. Acts 26:19. I was not disobedient unto the heavenly vision. Gal. 1:16. Immediately I conferred not with flesh and blood.

BSBPsalm 119:60 I hurried without hesitating to keep Your commandments.

语法参：不用 do 的古体否定（Be not / Fret not） → 004 delayed not / I conferred not with flesh and blood：两处古体否定（并不迟延／没有商量）——『立行』的句法标记。

中诗篇 119:59-60 —— 我思想我所行的道，就转步归向你的法度。我急忙遵守你的命令，并不迟延。路加福音 15:20 —— 于是起来，往他父亲那里去。使徒行传 26:19 —— 我故此没有违背那从天上来的异象。加拉太书 1:16 —— 我就没有与属血气的人商量。

三、认罪・舍己・信靠・爱与奉献 / Confession, Self-Surrender, Trust, Love and Consecration

ENLuke 15:21. Father, I have sinned against heaven, and in thy sight, and am no more worthy to be called thy son. (Read the testimonies of Paul, Peter and John.)

BSBLuke 15:21 The son declared, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you. I am no longer worthy to be called your son.’

中路加福音 15:21 —— 父亲，我得罪了天，又得罪了你，从今以后，我不配称为你的儿子。（可参读保罗、彼得、约翰的见证。）

四、凭据与实例 / Evidence; Recorded Conversions

ENEvidence: Ps. 34:8. O taste and see that the Lord is good. Ps. 66:16. Come and hear, all ye that fear God, and I will declare what He hath done for my soul. Acts 9:11. Behold, he prayeth. Some Recorded Conversions: Matthew, Luke 5:28, 29. Paul. The Ethiopian Treasurer, contrasted with Simon the Sorcerer, Acts 8:9-39. Lydia and the Jailer, Acts 16:14, 15, 27-34. See Christian Experience; Consideration. [The spirit of surrender and acceptance involved in the initial act of trust continues as the characteristic temper of the Christian man, and becomes a fixed mood of conscious dependence and receptiveness. — Stevens.]

BSBPsalm 34:8 Taste and see that the LORD is good; blessed is the man who takes refuge in Him!

语法参：呼格 O + 称呼语 → 003 O taste and see：O + 祈使的呼告（你们要尝尝主恩的滋味）。
语法参：古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est → 001 Behold, he prayeth：看哪，他正祷告——三个词写尽归正（prayeth = prays）。

中凭据：诗篇 34:8 —— 你们要尝尝主恩的滋味，便知道他是美善。66:16 —— 凡敬畏神的人，你们都来听，我要述说他为我所行的事。使徒行传 9:11 —— 他正祷告（扫罗归正的第一个凭据）。若干归正实例：马太（路加福音 5:28-29——撇下所有的，起来跟从了耶稣）；保罗；埃提阿伯的银库总管——与行邪术的西门恰成对照（使徒行传 8:9-39）；吕底亚与腓立比狱卒（16:14-15, 27-34）。另参主题词条：基督徒经历（034 三）；思想（047）。【摘录】起初信靠之举中所含降服与领受的灵，延续为基督徒品格性的性情，成为一种自觉依赖、自觉领受的恒定心境。——史蒂文斯（Stevens）
