EN[Covetousness, being a vice which has its seat in the affections, and which can work out its schemes through conventional forms, may very largely conceal its hideousness from public observation. But God, to whom the inner thought of a man is as visible as his outer act, regards it simply as a mode of that selfism which is the filthy fount of every human sin. Its offensiveness is less offensive to the public eye, but it has the same root as the worst crimes which men can commit. In its influence over one's spiritual interests it is perhaps more deadly than the lusts of the flesh, for these cannot wholly silence the voice of conscience; but conscience, with every other gift of noble origin, lies blasted in the soul which sells itself to Mammon. The covetous man's worship of gold makes him an "idolater." His trust in riches holds him back from that self-surrender to God which is the condition of admission into the kingdom of God. It requires almost a miracle of grace to dispose the covetous man to exchange his love of gold for the love of Christ.]

语法参：介词前置的关系从句 to whom / of which → 001 God, to whom the inner thought of a man is as visible as his outer act：介词 to 前置的关系从句（在神眼中内念如外行一样可见）。
语法lies blasted：lie + 过去分词作表语（枯萎凋残地躺着）；dispose sb to do：使人倾向于（使贪婪的人肯……）。

中【摘录】贪婪这一恶习，因其盘踞于情感之中，又能借体面的常规形式实现其图谋，故大体上能把它的丑陋遮掩于公众视线之外。但在神眼中——人的内心意念在他面前如同外在行为一样一览无余——它不过是那『自我主义』的一种样式，而自我主义正是人类一切罪恶的污秽之源。它的可憎在公众眼里不那么扎眼，但它与人所能犯的最恶罪行同出一根。就其对人属灵益处的影响而言，它也许比肉体的情欲更为致命——因为情欲尚不能完全压住良心的声音；而在把自己卖给玛门的灵魂里，良心连同一切出身高贵的禀赋，都已枯萎凋残。贪婪之人对金子的崇拜，使他成了『拜偶像的』。他对钱财的倚仗，拦阻他向神作那全然的降服——而这降服正是进入神国的条件。要使贪婪的人肯拿他对金子的爱换取对基督的爱，几乎需要一个恩典的神迹。
