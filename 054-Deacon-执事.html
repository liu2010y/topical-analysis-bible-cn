正文对照 / Text

ENDEACON. Acts 6:1-6. Seven deacons, set apart by prayer and the laying on of hands; some of their duties. 1 Tim. 3:8-13. Their character and qualifications. (Deacons in like manner must be grave, not double-tongued, not given to much wine, not greedy of filthy lucre; holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience. … For they that have served well as deacons gain to themselves a good standing, and great boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus.)

BSB1 Timothy 3:8, 9 8 Deacons likewise must be dignified, not double-tongued or given to much wine or greedy for money. 9 They must hold to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience.

语法deacon 源自希腊文 diakonos『仆役，服事者』；not double-tongued / not greedy of filthy lucre：否定形容词排比——double-tongued『一口两舌』、filthy lucre『不义之财』（lucre：钱财，贬义，GRE 词）。

中执事。使徒行传 6:1-6 —— 七位执事，借祷告与按手分别出来（管理饭食供给，使使徒专心以祈祷传道为事）。提摩太前书 3:8-13 —— 执事的品格与资格：作执事的也是如此，必须端庄，不一口两舌，不好喝酒，不贪不义之财，要存清洁的良心，固守真道的奥秘。……因为善作执事的，自己就得到美好的地步，并且在基督耶稣里的真道上大有胆量。
