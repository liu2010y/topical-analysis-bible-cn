正文对照 / Text

ENDESPONDENCY. "Cast Down." "Disquieted." Encouragement to be strong, of good courage, to trust, to hope in and wait on the Lord: Ps. 27:13, 14. Be strong, and let thine heart take courage; yea, wait thou on the Lord. Ps. 31:24. Deut. 31:6, 7. Josh. 1:6, 9, 18. 1 Kings 2:2. Ps. 119:25, 28.

BSBPsalm 27:14 Wait patiently for the LORD; be strong and courageous. Wait patiently for the LORD!

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 "Cast Down." "Disquieted."：并置引语式副题——摘取诗篇 42 篇的关键词作词条别名。
语法参：Let + 第三人称祈使句 → 003 let thine heart take courage：let 祈使（让你的心壮胆）。
语法wait thou on the Lord：带明示主语 thou 的祈使句，古体强调（你当等候耶和华）；yea：古副词『是的，再者』。

中灰心丧胆。『忧闷』『烦躁』。劝勉人刚强、壮胆、信靠、仰望并等候耶和华：诗篇 27:13-14 —— 要壮胆，坚固你的心（英译：要刚强，让你的心壮胆）；我再说，要等候耶和华。诗篇 31:24。申命记 31:6-7。约书亚记 1:6, 9, 18。列王纪上 2:2。诗篇 119:25, 28。

ENPs. 42:5, 11. Why art thou cast down, O my soul? Hope thou in God, for I shall yet praise Him. 2 Cor. 4:9. Cast down, but not destroyed. 2 Cor. 7:6. God, that comforteth those that are cast down. Ps. 56:3. What time I am afraid, I will put my trust in Thee. The special treatment of Elijah in his despondency (1 Kings 19). See Affliction; Anxiety; Infirmity; Faith; Hope; Waiting on God.

BSBPsalm 42:5 Why are you downcast, O my soul? Why the unease within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him for the salvation of His presence.

语法参：呼格 O + 称呼语 → 003 Why art thou cast down, O my soul?：呼格——诗人向自己的灵魂发问。
语法Hope thou in God：带主语 thou 的祈使句；What time I am afraid = whenever I am afraid（what time：古连词『每当』）。

中诗篇 42:5, 11 —— 我的心哪，你为何忧闷？应当仰望神，因我还要称赞他。哥林多后书 4:9 —— 打倒了，却不至死亡（英译：被击倒，却不被毁灭）。7:6 —— 那安慰丧气之人的神。诗篇 56:3 —— 我惧怕的时候要倚靠你。神对以利亚灰心求死时的特别看顾（列王纪上 19 章：使者供食、微小的声音）。另参主题词条：苦难（003）；忧虑（009）；软弱；信心；盼望；等候神。
