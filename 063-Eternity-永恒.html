定义与核心经文 / Meaning and Chief Texts

ENETERNITY. Heb. olam; Gr. aion. Everlasting; eternal. Eccles. 3:11. He hath set eternity in their heart. Isa. 57:15. The high and lofty One that inhabiteth eternity. 2 Cor. 4:18. The things which are not seen are eternal. 2 Cor. 5:1. A house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens. Heb. 7:16. The power of an endless life. 2 Cor. 4:17. An eternal weight of glory.

BSBEcclesiastes 3:11 He has made everything beautiful in its time. He has also set eternity in the hearts of men, yet they cannot fathom the work that God has done from beginning to end.

语法参：the + 形容词表一类人 → 001 the high and lofty One：the＋形容词＋One（那至高至上者）。
语法参：古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est → 001 inhabiteth：住在（永恒之中）。
语法参：形容词后置（名词, 形容词 and 形容词） → 001 a house … eternal in the heavens：形容词短语后置（在天上永存的房屋）。

中永恒。希伯来文 olam；希腊文 aion——『永远的，永恒的』。传道书 3:11 —— 又将永生（英译：永恒）安置在世人心里。以赛亚书 57:15 —— 那至高至上、永远长存（英译：住在永恒中）的。哥林多后书 4:18 —— 所不见的是永远的。5:1 —— 我们……必得神所造，不是人手所造，在天上永存的房屋。希伯来书 7:16 —— 无穷（原文作不能毁坏）之生命的大能。哥林多后书 4:17 —— 极重无比、永远的荣耀。

ENLife everlasting, eternal: Matt. 25:46. Mark 10:30. John 3:15; 6:47, 54; 10:28. (I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish.) John 17:2, 3. (This is life eternal, that they should know Thee, the only true God, and Him whom Thou didst send, even Jesus Christ.) Rom. 2:7; 6:23. 1 Tim. 6:12. 1 John 2:25; 5:12, 13, 20. Jude 21.

BSBJohn 10:28 I give them eternal life, and they will never perish. No one can snatch them out of My hand.

语法参：形容词后置（名词, 形容词 and 形容词） → 001 life eternal / life everlasting：形容词后置（永生），庄重语体。
语法This is life eternal, that they should know Thee：that 同位语从句说明 life eternal 的内容；even Jesus Christ：even 表同位强调（就是耶稣基督）。

中永生（everlasting / eternal life）：马太福音 25:46。马可福音 10:30。约翰福音 3:15；6:47, 54；10:28 ——（我又赐给他们永生，他们永不灭亡。）17:2-3 ——（认识你独一的真神，并且认识你所差来的耶稣基督，这就是永生。）罗马书 2:7；6:23。提摩太前书 6:12。约翰一书 2:25；5:12, 13, 20。犹大书 21。

『永远的』之诸般应用 / "Everlasting" and "Eternal" Further Applied

ENEverlasting and eternal further applied: Covenant, 2 Sam. 23:5; Heb. 13:20. Salvation, Isa. 45:17; Heb. 5:9; 2 Tim. 2:10; 1 Pet. 5:10. Redemption, Heb. 9:12. Inheritance, Heb. 9:15. Mercy, Ps. 100:5. Strength and Arms (of God), Isa. 26:4; Deut. 33:27. Righteousness, Ps. 119:142. Kingdom, Ps. 145:13. Gospel, Rev. 14:6. Consolation, 2 Thes. 2:16. Joy, Isa. 35:10. Light, Isa. 60:19. Love, Jer. 31:3. (I have loved thee with an everlasting love.) Punishment, Matt. 25:46. Destruction, 2 Thes. 1:9. Fire, Matt. 18:8; 25:41; Jude 7; Isa. 33:14. Also, Ps. 16:11. Pleasures for evermore. Ps. 133:3. Life for evermore. Luke 16:9. Everlasting habitations.

BSBJeremiah 31:3 The LORD appeared to us in the past, saying: “I have loved you with an everlasting love; therefore I have drawn you with loving devotion.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 Covenant, 2 Sam. 23:5; Salvation, …：词典式清单——名词＋出处并置，系动词全部省略；本块开列 everlasting / eternal 在圣经中修饰过的十七类事物。

中『永远的』一词的诸般应用：约——撒母耳记下 23:5；希伯来书 13:20。救恩——以赛亚书 45:17；希伯来书 5:9；提摩太后书 2:10；彼得前书 5:10。救赎——希伯来书 9:12。产业——9:15。慈爱——诗篇 100:5。能力与膀臂（神的）——以赛亚书 26:4；申命记 33:27（永生的神是你的居所，他永久的膀臂在你以下）。公义——诗篇 119:142。国度——145:13。福音——启示录 14:6。安慰——帖撒罗尼迦后书 2:16。喜乐——以赛亚书 35:10。光——60:19。爱——耶利米书 31:3 ——（我以永远的爱爱你。）刑罚——马太福音 25:46。沉沦——帖撒罗尼迦后书 1:9。火——马太福音 18:8；25:41；犹大书 7；以赛亚书 33:14。又：诗篇 16:11 —— 永远的福乐。133:3 —— 永远的生命。路加福音 16:9 —— 永存的帐幕。
