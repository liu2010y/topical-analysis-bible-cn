信心的定义 / Meaning and Definition

ENFAITH; Belief, Believe (pistis, pisteuo). Meaning: Trust, Confidence in, Leaning on. Trust, the Old Testament word; Faith or Belief, the word of the New Testament. Comprehensively, including intellectual assent to revealed truths respecting God and Redemption; submission of the will, or self-commitment and consecration; and confidence of the heart (Rom. 10:10), or loving trust in and leaning upon God and His words of promise. Briefly, Trust in a Redeeming God, and assured confidence in His Work for and in us.

BSBRomans 10:10 For with your heart you believe and are justified, and with your mouth you confess and are saved.

语法参：同位语结构 → 001 (pistis, pisteuo)：希腊原文同位注释——名词 pistis（信）、动词 pisteuo（相信）。
语法参：the + 动名词的名词化结构 → 001 Trust, Confidence in, Leaning on：名词与动名词并列作定义式表语（信靠、依赖、倚偎）。

中信心；信，相信（希腊文 pistis 名词、pisteuo 动词）。含义：信靠、依赖、倚偎。旧约的用词是『信靠』（Trust），新约的用词是『信』（Faith / Belief）。综合而言，信心包括：理智上认可神与救赎的启示真理；意志的降服，即自我交托与奉献；并心里的信赖（罗马书 10:10 —— 人心里相信，就可以称义），即爱慕地信靠、倚赖神和他应许的话。一言以蔽之：信靠那位施行救赎的神，并对他为我们、在我们里面所作之工笃信不疑。

信心与义、与称义 / With Righteousness; With Justification

ENRelations and connections of faith. With Righteousness (Gr. being right): The condition of one who stands accepted by the perfect Law, and is therefore approved of God. This condition or standing is attained either by exactly meeting the Law's demands, or by the reckoning of such perfect obedience on account of the sacrificial self-offering of the Incarnate Son of God, accepted and trusted in by the penitent transgressor. Thus the whole Scripture discriminates between "the righteousness which is of the Law" and "the righteousness which is of faith." Paul cites Moses as saying in reference to the former (Rom. 10:5), The man who doeth these things (who perfectly keeps God's commandments) shall live by them. So Christ, Matt. 19:17, If thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments. But Paul quotes the assertion of many who wrote before him (Rom. 3:9-20), There is none righteous; all are gone out of the way. And he concludes (vs. 20), Therefore by the deeds of the law shall no flesh be justified in His sight. Hence the great and central theme of the Scripture is "the righteousness which is of faith," and upon the pages of Old and New Testament, in manifold forms of announcement, appears the supreme and vital connection of Faith.

BSBRomans 10:5 For concerning the righteousness that is by the law, Moses writes: “The man who does these things will live by them.”
