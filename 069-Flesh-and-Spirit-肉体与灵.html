一、肉体 / I. Flesh

ENFLESH AND SPIRIT. I. Flesh: The entire unrenewed man, including mind, heart, will and body. Equivalent terms: Natural (animal, sensuous) man; Old man (in contrast with New or renewed man); Carnal mind, or mind of the flesh. Comprehensively, the man in whom the intellectual aims and pursuits, the emotional and passionate nature, the motives and choices of the will, and the bodily appetites, are all supremely directed and exercised in the exclusive interest and gratification of self, in utter disregard of God, and in disobedience of His Law of righteousness and love.

语法参：同位语结构 → 001 Natural man / Old man / Carnal mind：三个同义术语的同位罗列——保罗神学的『肉体』词群。
语法the man in whom … are all supremely directed …：in whom 引导的长定语从句，四个并列主语（理智／情感／意志／欲望）共享一个被动谓语——定义式长句。

中肉体与灵。一、肉体：指整个未经更新的人——包括心思、心灵、意志与身体。同义的称呼：属血气的（属动物性的、属感官的）人；旧人（与新人、更新之人相对）；属肉体的心，即体贴肉体的心思。综合而言：这样的人，其理智的目标与追求、情感与血气的天性、意志的动机与抉择、身体的欲望，统统只为自我的利益与满足而运转——全然不顾神，悖逆他公义与慈爱的律法。

EN[Mortifying, or making to die, the flesh: that is, determinedly setting all the energy of the soul into sharp and persistent resistance to the natural motions of self-will and selfish passion, excited and developed under the impulse of temptation; putting forth this determined resistance day by day, with the desire and purpose, in reliance upon the Spirit of God, of ultimately crushing the very life of the old self-will and the once dominant "fleshly" and worldly "lusts"—this, and nothing less than this, is our lifelong work and warfare; a toil and conflict lightened and sustained to the end by the assured inworking of God Himself, for present deliverance and ultimate victory. This is our "crucifixion with Christ." Thus, through the accompanying Divine renewing energy, we become "dead to sin" and alive to Christ, so that we can say, "I live, yet not I, but Christ liveth in me." In its full interpretation, "crucifixion with Christ" implies the adoption, in daily living, of the principles which governed His life of obedience, purity and beneficence. It means the entire surrender of self-will and self-guidance, the utter denial and subjugation of all self-aims and desires, of the mental passions and bodily appetites, involving a separation from the godless thoughts, ambitions, pleasures, and pursuits of the world, with the giving up of all self-interests which collide with the higher imperative interests of God's working for man. B.]

中〔治死肉体（使之死去），就是：下定决心，把灵魂全部的力量投入一场尖锐而持久的抵抗，抵抗自我意志与自私情欲在试探催动下激发滋长的天然冲动；天天拿出这份决然的抵抗，靠着神的灵，怀着一个愿望与目标——终要把旧的自我意志、把一度作王的『肉体的』『属世的』私欲的命根彻底碾碎。这个，一丝一毫不能少于这个，就是我们毕生的工作与争战；而这场劳苦争战，自始至终因着神亲自在里面动工的确据而得着减轻与扶持，既有今日的拯救，也有最终的得胜。这就是我们的『与基督同钉十字架』。这样，借着随之而来的神圣更新大能，我们向罪算自己是死的、向基督却是活的，于是能说：『现在活着的不再是我，乃是基督在我里面活着。』究其全义，『与基督同钉十字架』意味着在日常生活中采纳那曾支配他顺服、纯洁、行善一生的诸原则；意味着自我意志与自我引领的全然交出，一切自我目标与欲望、心智的激情与身体的嗜欲的彻底否认与降服；也就包含与世上不敬虔的思想、野心、宴乐、追逐分道扬镳，放弃一切与神为人动工的更高、更紧要的利益相冲突的自我利益。B.（编者巴特勒）〕

EN[The flesh may be present even in our efforts after sanctification. "Are ye so foolish? having begun in the Spirit, are ye now perfected in the flesh?" (Gal. 3:3)—it would appear that in Galatia there was a school of perfectionists, who were attempting to secure perfection in the energy of the self-life. True holiness, as true consecration, must be wholly of God, through Him, and to Him. "Of God are ye in Christ Jesus, who is made unto us . . . sanctification." The flesh may also be energetic in Christian work. How much of our Christian work has been along this line—suggested by the flesh, wrought out by the flesh or self-energy, and tending to the glorification of ourselves! And therefore much of it is wood, hay, and stubble, to be burnt up. How memorable is that incident in Hudson Taylor's life, in which God said to him: "I am going to evangelize inland China, and if you will walk with Me, I will do it through you!" This is the way in which we think when we have learned to consign the self-life to the death. F. B. Meyer.]

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 How memorable is that incident …：how＋表语前置的感叹倒装（何等令人难忘）。
语法Of God are ye in Christ Jesus：介词短语前置＋倒装（你们得在基督里本乎神）；consign the self-life to the death：consign to『交付于』——迈尔式的十架灵修语汇。

中〔连我们追求成圣的努力里，也可能掺着肉体。『你们既靠圣灵入门，如今还靠肉身成全吗？你们是这样的无知吗？』（加拉太书 3:3）——看来加拉太当年有一派完全主义者，想靠自我生命的力量去挣得完全。真圣洁如同真奉献，必须全然出于神、借着神、归于神：『你们得在基督耶稣里是本乎神，神又使他成为我们的……圣洁。』肉体在基督教事工里也可能干劲十足。我们有多少基督教工作正是走的这条路——由肉体起意，靠肉体（自我的力量）做成，归趋于荣耀我们自己！所以其中许多不过是草木禾秸，终必被烧掉。戴德生（Hudson Taylor）生平那件事何等令人难忘——神对他说：『我要亲自去传福音给中国内地；你若肯与我同行，我就借着你成就它！』当我们学会了把自我生命交付于死，我们的思路便是这样。迈尔（F. B. Meyer）〕
