正文对照 / Text

ENFLOOD. The Story and its Lessons (Gen. 6-9): see The Bible Work, Vol. I. The Extent of the Deluge: ibid.

BSBGenesis 7:17 For forty days the flood kept coming on the earth, and the waters rose and lifted the ark high above the earth.

语法The Story and its Lessons / Extent of the Deluge：名词标题式条目；deluge：大洪水的专称（the Deluge 特指挪亚洪水），比 flood 更书面。

中洪水。洪水的记事及其教训（创世记 6-9 章）：参作者注释书《The Bible Work》第一卷（原书注 I. 226-243）。洪水泛滥的范围问题：同上（I. 244-245，十九世纪注释家争论洪水是普世性的还是地域性的）。
