一、预知 / I. Foreknowledge

ENFOREKNOWLEDGE; FOREORDINATION; CALLING; ELECTION. These are indissolubly linked in the order of Divine procedure: Rom. 8:29, 30. Whom He foreknew, He also foreordained; . . . and whom He foreordained, He also called. I. Foreknow; Foreknowledge. Proginosko, Prognosis: to know first or beforehand. Only five texts: Rom. 8:29. Whom He did foreknow, He also foreordained. Rom. 11:2. God has not cast off His people, whom He did foreknow. Acts 2:23. Him, being delivered up by the determinate (ordained, horizo) counsel and foreknowledge of God. 1 Pet. 1:1, 2. Elect (eklectos) according to the foreknowledge of God. 1 Pet. 1:20. Christ, foreknown before the foundation of the world.

BSBRomans 8:29, 30 29 For those God foreknew, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, so that He would be the firstborn among many brothers. 30 And those He predestined, He also called; those He called, He also justified; those He justified, He also glorified.

语法参：同位语结构 → 001 Proginosko, Prognosis：希腊原文同位注释——pro（预先）＋ginosko（知道）；英文 prognosis（预后）即源于此。
语法Whom He foreknew, He also foreordained：关系句前置＋主句复指——罗马书八章『救恩金链』的链式句法，本词条骨架。

中预知；预定；呼召；拣选。在神作为的次序里，这四者密不可分地连成一环：罗马书 8:29-30 —— 因为他预先所知道的人，就预先定下……预先所定下的人又召他们来。一、预知（动词 Proginosko，名词 Prognosis：预先知道）。经文仅五处：罗马书 8:29 —— 预先所知道的人，就预先定下。11:2 —— 神并没有弃绝他预先所知道的百姓。使徒行传 2:23 —— 他既按着神的定旨（horizo：所定的）先见被交与人。彼得前书 1:1-2 —— 照父神的先见被拣选（eklectos）的人。1:20 —— 基督在创世以前是预先被神知道的。【OCR 注：原书 "1 Pet. 2:20" 依经文（创世以前预先被知道）推定为彼得前书 1:20。】

ENIn these only Scriptural references to God's foreknowledge, two (the first and fourth) intimate some connection between His foreknowledge and His subsequent acting—foreordination in one case and election in the other—in securing the believer's salvation. The second text refers to His foreknowledge of His ancient chosen people. The third and fifth connect His ordaining act with His foreknowledge in the delivering up of Christ to death for us. In all the explicit references of Scripture, the foreknowledge of God has to do exclusively with His merciful and gracious action in behalf of the saving of His own chosen people. The Scripture is utterly silent, here and in all other passages which refer to His unsearchable knowledge, as to the connection between His foreknowledge and foreordination. It is surely wisdom on our part to imitate its silence, in our satisfaction with the result of that connection to us as saved believers. Proof of the fact of God's foreknowledge of all events, thoughts and deeds in all duration, we find in His Eternal and Omniscient Being. In the glad conviction of the fact, we may find comfort, help, and rest.

语法It is surely wisdom on our part to imitate its silence：it 形式主语＋不定式真主语（效法圣经的缄默乃是智慧）——本词条的方法论宣言；Proof of the fact …, we find in …：宾语前置（这一事实的证据，我们在……里找到）。

中在圣经论神预知的仅有这五处经文中，有两处（第一与第四）提示他的预知与他随后为成全信徒救恩的作为（一处是预定，一处是拣选）之间存有某种关联。第二处说的是他对古时选民的预知。第三与第五处把他所定的旨意与他的预知相连——关乎基督为我们被交于死。纵观圣经一切明文，神的预知无一例外只关乎他为拯救自己选民所施怜悯恩慈的作为。至于预知与预定之间究竟如何关联，圣经在此处、在一切论及他测不透之知识的经文里，都全然缄默。我们的智慧之道，正是效法圣经的缄默——以这关联加给我们这些得救信徒的结果为满足。至于神预知万世一切事件、意念与作为这一事实的证据，就在他永恒全知的存有本身。在对这事实的欢然确信里，我们可以得安慰、得帮助、得安息。

二、预定 / II. Foreordination

ENII. Foreordination. Proorizo: to mark off, bound or limit beforehand. (The A. V. renders the word "predestinate"; the R. V., more judiciously, "foreordain," since the former word has with many minds a flavor of arbitrary or partial acting.) Here also the texts are very few, only four: Rom. 8:29, 30. Foreordained to be conformed to the image of His Son. Whom He did foreordain, He called. Eph. 1:5, 11. Foreordained us unto adoption as sons. Foreordained according to the purpose of Him; made a heritage in Christ.

BSBEphesians 1:5 He predestined us for adoption as His sons through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of His will,

语法参：同位语结构 → 001 Proorizo：pro（预先）＋horizo（划界，英文 horizon『地平线』同源）——『预定』一词的本义是预先划定界限。
语法more judiciously：比较级副词评点两个译本的取舍（修订本更审慎）；has with many minds a flavor of …：介词短语插入谓语中间的十九世纪语序。

中二、预定（Proorizo：预先划界、预先立定范围）。（钦定本将此词译作 "predestinate"『命定』；修订本更审慎地译作 "foreordain"『预先立定』——因为前一个词在许多人心里带着专断偏待的味道。）此处经文同样极少，仅四处：罗马书 8:29-30 —— 预先定下效法他儿子的模样。预先所定下的人，又召他们来。以弗所书 1:5, 11 —— 预定我们……得儿子的名分。照着他旨意所预定的，在基督里……得了基业（英译：成为他的产业）。

ENWith even greater emphasis it should be noted that these only direct Scriptural references to God's foreordination are connected exclusively with His saving processes, and immediately affect the spiritual transformation and adoption of the saved soul. While the Scripture is silent as to God's reasons, it plainly unfolds the gracious end or object of His foreordaining action. There is absolutely no clue to His motive or reason, except as we gather it from the end or its effect (holiness and sonship) referred to above, and from His revealed character. As disclosed, it is a gracious foreordination, and cannot be partial to any. Its operation must be impartial and universal, since no one is entitled by character or deed to more than any other, all alike being sinners and unworthy. It should be further noted that, while foreordination respects the gracious acts and gifts of God in man's behalf, we read of no foreordination to faith or repentance, or to any act required of man as a condition of salvation. Foreordination, therefore, cannot possibly infringe upon the free action with which man is solemnly invested by God. That God's foreordination includes the absolute and unlimited control over all events and over the actions of all spiritual creatures, appears conclusively in the fact that He is the Self-existent, Self-sufficient, Eternal and Omnipotent Upholder and Ruler of the Universe which He Himself has created. In this assurance also our comfort, help and rest may well be increased, and our satisfaction in God intensified. See Agency. Also Jer. 18:1-10.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 With even greater emphasis it should be noted that …：状语前置＋it 形式主语（更当着重指出）。
语法all alike being sinners and unworthy：独立主格结构作原因状语（人人同为罪人）；the free action with which man is solemnly invested：invest A with B 的被动＋介词提前（神郑重赋予人的自由行动）。

中更当着重指出的是：圣经论神预定的这仅有的几处明文，无一例外只与他拯救的进程相连，直接关乎得救灵魂的属灵改变与得儿子名分。圣经对神的理由缄默不言，却清楚展开了他预定作为的恩慈目的。关于他的动机与理由，除了从上述目的或果效（圣洁与儿子名分）并他已启示的品格中去推想，绝无任何线索。就其所显明的而论，这是恩慈的预定，不可能偏待任何人；其运作必是不偏不倚、普及众人的——因为论品格论行为，没有一人配得比别人更多，人人同为罪人、同样不配。还当指出：预定所关涉的是神为人所施的恩慈作为与恩赐；我们从未读到『预定人有信心』『预定人悔改』，或预定人去行任何被要求作为得救条件的行动。所以，预定绝不可能侵犯神郑重赋予人的自由行动。至于神的预定包含对一切事件、对一切属灵受造者行动的绝对无限的掌管，这从一个事实里确凿显明：他是自有永有、自足、永恒、全能的宇宙托住者与统治者——这宇宙原是他亲手所造。在这确据里，我们的安慰、帮助与安息更可加增，我们在神里面的满足也愈发深厚。参主题词条：神人协作（004）。另参耶利米书 18:1-10（窑匠与泥）。

三、呼召与拣选：原文与用法 / III–V. Kaleo and Eklego

ENIII. Call, Calling, Called. Gr. Kaleo, Klesis, Kletos: call or invite; calling or invitation; called or invited. IV. Choose, Gr. Eklego. Chosen, Eklectos. Elect, Eklectos. Election, Ekloge. Note that the original word is one and the same for all these renderings; its primary meaning is laid out or chosen. V. Scriptural uses of the words Kaleo, or Calling, and Eklego, or Choosing. Three texts only seem to discriminate by combining them: Matt. 22:14. Many are called, but few chosen. 2 Pet. 1:10. Make your calling and election sure. Rev. 17:14. Called and chosen and faithful. Careful study of the many passages containing one or other of these words shows an almost general interchangeableness in the use and in the application—practically in the intent or meaning—of the two words.

BSBMatthew 22:14 For many are called, but few are chosen.”

语法参：同位语结构 → 001 Kaleo, Klesis, Kletos / Eklego, Eklectos, Ekloge：希腊词族的同位罗列——ek（出）＋lego（选取）即『选出』；英文 eclectic（兼收并蓄的）同源。
语法参：省略结构（省略主语、系动词 be 等） → 002 Many are called, but few chosen：后半省略 are（被召的多，选上的少）。

中三、呼召（希腊文动词 Kaleo、名词 Klesis、形容词 Kletos：呼召或邀请；蒙召或蒙邀）。四、拣选（动词 Eklego；『被拣选的』与『选民』同为 Eklectos；『拣选』为 Ekloge）。请注意：这几种译法背后的原文实是同一个词，本义为『选出，拣出』。五、Kaleo（呼召）与 Eklego（拣选）二词在圣经中的用法。似乎只有三处经文把两词并举而加以分别：马太福音 22:14 —— 被召的人多，选上的人少。彼得后书 1:10 —— 使你们所蒙的恩召和拣选坚定不移。启示录 17:14 —— 蒙召、被选、有忠心的。细考含有这两个词的众多经文可见：二词在用法与应用上（实际上在意向或含义上）几乎可以通用。

四、三种呼召与拣选 / VI. Three Kinds of Calling and Election

ENVI. Threefold significance in the words Kaleo and Eklego, as interpreted by the entire text and context in each case. In plainer words, the Scriptures appear to intimate three kinds of calling and election. 1. A calling or choosing to place, function, and service: Heb. 11:8. Abraham, when he was called, obeyed to go out unto a place. Heb. 5:4. Called of God, as was Aaron. John 6:70. Have I not chosen you the twelve, and one of you is a devil? John 15:16. I chose you, and appointed you, that ye should go and bear fruit. Jacob, chosen before his birth as the progenitor of God's elect people: Rom. 9:11, 12. That the purpose of God according to election might stand, not of works, but of Him that calleth, it was said unto her (Rebecca), The elder shall serve the younger. So also Pharaoh was appointed to his place and service, Rom. 9:17. Hosea 11:1. When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called My son out of Egypt. Gal. 1:15-19. Paul's call to apostleship. Also Ex. 35:30, 31. Bezalel.

BSBRomans 9:11, 12 11 Yet before the twins were born or had done anything good or bad, in order that God’s plan of election might stand, 12 not by works but by Him who calls, she was told, “The older will serve the younger.”

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Have I not chosen you the twelve …?：否定疑问倒装（我不是拣选了你们十二个吗）。
语法Called of God, as was Aaron：of = by（蒙神所召）＋as 从句倒装（像亚伦一样）；The elder shall serve the younger：the＋比较级表两者中之一方。

中六、Kaleo 与 Eklego 二词按各处上下文解读出的三重含义。说得更直白些：圣经似乎提示了三种呼召与拣选。1. 蒙召／蒙选去担任某个位置、职能与服事：希伯来书 11:8 —— 亚伯拉罕因着信，蒙召的时候就遵命出去，往将来要得为业的地方去。5:4 —— 要蒙神所召，像亚伦一样。约翰福音 6:70 —— 我不是拣选了你们十二个门徒吗？但你们中间有一个是魔鬼。15:16 —— 是我拣选了你们，并且分派你们去结果子。雅各未出生即被选为神选民之族的先祖：罗马书 9:11-12 —— 神拣选人的旨意，不在乎人的行为，乃在乎召人的主……就对利百加说：将来大的要服事小的。法老也照样被派定其位置与用场——罗马书 9:17。何西阿书 11:1 —— 以色列年幼的时候我爱他，就从埃及召出我的儿子来。加拉太书 1:15-19 —— 保罗蒙召作使徒亦属此类。又出埃及记 35:30-31 —— 比撒列（蒙召作会幕的巧匠）。

EN2. A calling to salvation, or invitation to the unsaved: Matt. 9:13. I came not to call the righteous, but sinners. This one utterance of Christ, at least, is clear and conclusive upon this point; and it furnishes a large interpretation of His words in Matt. 22:14, Many are called, but few chosen. The substance of this call is variously and repeatedly expressed by Christ—in personal invitation, "Come unto Me," etc.; in the bidding of the Parables of the Great Supper, etc. The call is addressed to every one who wills to accept it. Matt. 16:24. If any man would come after Me. Rev. 22:17. He that is athirst, let him come; he that will, let him take the water of life freely.

BSBMatthew 9:13 But go and learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice.’ For I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners.”

语法参：不用 do 的古体否定（Be not / Fret not） → 004 I came not to call the righteous：不用 do 的否定（本不是召义人）。
语法参：Let + 第三人称祈使句 → 003 let him come / let him take：let 祈使（当来／可以取）。
语法参：him that / them that = he who / those who → 001 He that is athirst / he that will：口渴的人／愿意的人（will 作实义动词『愿意』）。

中2. 得救的呼召，即向未得救者发出的邀请：马太福音 9:13 —— 我来本不是召义人，乃是召罪人。基督这一句话，至少在这一点上清楚而具有定论性；它也为马太福音 22:14『被召的人多，选上的人少』提供了宽广的解释。这呼召的实质，基督曾以各样方式反复表达——在亲口的邀请里：『到我这里来』等等；在大筵席等比喻的召请里。这呼召向每一个愿意接受的人发出。马太福音 16:24 —— 若有人要跟从我。启示录 22:17 —— 口渴的人也当来；愿意的，都可以白白取生命的水喝。

EN3. An effectual call and choice of God, based upon His eternal gracious purpose, and carried into effect by His own Divine energy, through the enlightening, renewing, and sanctifying Spirit. All the texts of the New Testament touching this subject, except the few cited above, carry this profound and vital meaning. They intimate plainly an acting of God's Spirit within the soul—a call, based upon an eternal choice, that is effective in the result of bringing men to repentance and faith; an actual intervention of Divine energy in new-creating and sanctifying the spiritually dead and natively unholy soul. In reading the texts which follow, let it be definitely noted that they are addressed or refer distinctly to "saints," i.e., actually sanctified believers: Rom. 8:28, 30, 33. To them that love God all things work together for good, even to them that are called according to His purpose. Whom He foreordained, them He also called; and whom He called, them He also justified. Who shall lay anything to the charge of God's elect? 1 Cor. 1:2, 27, 28. God chose the foolish, the weak, and the base things of the world, that no flesh should glory before God. Gal. 1:15. Eph. 1:4-7. He chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blemish in Him: having in love foreordained us unto adoption as sons, through Jesus Christ, unto Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will, to the praise of the glory of His grace. Phil. 3:14. The prize of the high (upward) calling of God in Christ Jesus. 1 Thes. 1:4. Knowing, brethren beloved of God, your election. 1 Thes. 2:12. Walk worthily of God, who calleth you into His kingdom and glory. 1 Thes. 4:7. God called us not for uncleanness, but in sanctification. 1 Thes. 5:24. Faithful is He that calleth you, who will also do it. 2 Thes. 2:13, 14. God chose you from the beginning unto salvation, in sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth. 2 Tim. 1:9. Who saved us, and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before times eternal. Titus 1:1. According to the faith of God's elect. 1 Pet. 1:2. Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, in sanctification of the Spirit. 1 Pet. 2:9. Ye are an elect race, a people for God's own possession, that ye may show forth the excellencies of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light. 1 Pet. 5:10. The God of all grace, who called you unto His eternal glory in Christ, shall Himself perfect, stablish, strengthen you. 2 Pet. 1:10. Jude 1. To them that are called, beloved in God the Father, and kept for Jesus Christ. Rev. 17:14.

BSBEphesians 1:4, 5 4 For He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world to be holy and blameless in His presence. In love 5 He predestined us for adoption as His sons through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of His will,

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Faithful is He that calleth you：表语前置倒装（那召你们的本是信实的）；To them that love God all things work together：介词短语前置。

五、思考与结论 / VII. Considerations and Conclusions

ENVII. Considerations and Conclusions respecting this vital ground-truth of Divine Election. 1. Suggestive considerations: (1) The spiritual temper and tone of these disclosures are wholly alien to the thought of a God absorbed in the mere exercise of sovereignty, or satisfied with the mere indulgence of an arbitrary will. His whole willing and acting, as here represented, are pervaded with the thought of a deep, intense, loving sympathy for, and a yearning purpose to help, His self-blinded and imperiled creatures. It is the heart of God, far more than His will, that appears in His eternal purpose of grace, and in all the steps His electing love pursues. (2) God declares, through the message of Moses to Israel, that His choice or election is based upon His love: Deut. 7:7, 8. The Lord did choose you, because He loveth you. This message is repeated by the Prophets and Apostles, and even more distinctly and tenderly in the words and deeds of the Christ. And since we are assured that "God so loved the world that He gave His Son," and that "He by the grace of God tasted death for every man," is not the conclusion natural and reasonable, that in the desire and will of God His electing grace included in its purposed reach the entire race of sinners? (3) As election is of grace, and as grace, having an equal reach with promise, includes and enspheres the entire human race, it would seem that in the desire and plan and will of God there is no limitation of the sphere and reach of His gracious election. Acts 2:39. To you is the promise, and to all that are afar off, even as many as the Lord our God shall call unto Him. Rom. 5:20. Where sin abounded, grace did abound more exceedingly. Rom. 11:32. EN(4) Three utterances of Christ suffice to relieve the truth of the Divine Election from possible connection with the conception of a Divine "fate": "He that cometh unto Me I will in no wise cast out." Add to these the manifold declarations of God in both Testaments, concisely expressed 2 Pet. 3:9: "The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, but is longsuffering to you-ward, not wishing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance." These abounding expressions absolutely rule out any conception of Election that makes it proceed from the mere arbitrary decree or impassible purpose of God, irrespective of man's responsive willing and acting. They assuredly connect it, in a manner unknown to us, with the response of the individual man to the proffered grace of God. And in no inspired statement is found the least intimation that God has planned, purposed, or prepared for any human soul aught but salvation and everlasting life. Only the kingdom of light and blessedness, Christ declares (Matt. 25:34), is prepared for men; the realm of darkness and woe, to which the willfully disobedient are consigned, was prepared for the devil and his angels (v. 41). It would seem, then, that nothing but the resisting, rejecting will of the individual man has caused any actual limitation in the exercise of the Divine election to eternal life.

EN2. Conclusions of fact, confirmatory and helpful. Upon the face of these Scriptures the believer reads these simple confirmatory facts: (1) That the actual subjects of God's electing grace are those, and only those, who have themselves accepted divinely proffered mercy, and fulfilled the conditions of repentance, faith, love, and obedience upon which the proffer was based. (2) That all who have so accepted, repented, believed, loved, and obeyed, are Divinely elected unto holiness and salvation. These two facts intimate a vital, though not causal, connection between God's choice of the believer and the believer's faith and obedience toward God. (3) The further fact that all the passages which distinctly intimate an effectual calling or Divine election are addressed directly and exclusively to saints or believers—those who have already accepted, and are elect. The only possible aim in the statement of the doctrine must therefore be the spiritual instruction and help of those addressed. Not only the reason for so large a statement of the doctrine, but its only legitimate and healthful effect, is found in the inspiration, courage, strength, peace and rest which it brings into the heart and life of the weak and wavering but true believer. Such, in part, was its effect upon the penitent Peter, which drew from his loyal heart, in answer to the searching yet loving question of the Christ, the pathetic reply which was both confession of his own sin and a new confession of his Incarnate Lord: "Lord, Thou knowest all things; Thou knowest that I love Thee!"

中2. 事实性的结论——印证人心，有益人灵。就这些经文的字面，信徒可以读出几条朴素的印证事实：（1）神拣选之恩实际的对象，是且仅是那些自己接受了神所递的怜悯、并履行了这递恩所基于的悔改、信心、爱心、顺服等条件的人。（2）凡这样接受了、悔改了、信了、爱了、顺服了的人，都蒙神拣选归于圣洁与救恩。这两条事实提示：神对信徒的拣选，与信徒对神的信与顺服之间，存在一种攸关生命的联系——虽然不是因果性的联系。（3）还有一条事实：凡明确提示有效呼召、神圣拣选的经文，无一例外都是直接写给圣徒、写给信徒的——写给已经接受、已在选中的人。所以陈明这教义的唯一可能的目的，只能是对受书人属灵的教导与扶助。这教义之所以有如此宏大铺陈，其唯一正当而有益的果效，正在于它给软弱摇动、却真实信主之人的心灵与生活所注入的鼓舞、勇气、力量、平安与安息。它在悔改的彼得身上的果效即部分如此——基督那句既鉴察又慈爱的追问，从他忠诚的心里逼出那声动人的回答，既是认自己的罪，又是重新认他道成肉身的主：『主啊，你是无所不知的，你知道我爱你！』

ENIX. No directly alternative or converse statement to the doctrine of Election is discernible in the Scriptures. There is no corresponding positive statement touching those who are not included among the elect. No sentence or passage of the Bible can be reasonably construed into a positive declaration that God has decreed any to doom simply in the exercise of His own will, and irrespective of the character and conduct of the doomed. It is "ungodly men, turning the grace of God into lasciviousness, and denying our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ," who were of old set forth (ordained, not foreordained) to this condemnation (Jude 4). It is "such as disbelieve," who "stumble at the word, being disobedient," to whom Christ becomes "a stone of stumbling," "whereunto they were appointed" (1 Pet. 2:7, 8). It is the "wicked and slothful and unprofitable servant" who is "cast into the outer darkness" (Matt. 25:26, 30); "the cursed," who "go away into eternal punishment" (Matt. 25:41, 46). In other words, no doctrine of "reprobation," proceeding from the mere arbitrary will or decree of God, is explicitly stated, nor can it be exegetically or reasonably inferred from any passage in the Word of God. See Agency; Condemnation.

中九、圣经中辨认不出与拣选教义直接相对或相反的陈述。关于未列于选民之中的人，圣经并无对应的正面陈述。圣经没有一句话、一段经文，可以被合理地解释成这样一条正面宣告：神曾单凭行使自己的意志、全然不顾其人品格行为，就谕定任何人沉沦。从古被列定（原文是 set forth『标出』——是 ordained 而非 foreordained『预定』）受这刑罚的，是『不虔诚的，将我们神的恩变作放纵情欲的机会，并且不认独一的主宰我们主耶稣基督』的人（犹大书 4）。基督向之成为『绊脚的石头』、『他们既不顺从，就在道理上绊跌，他们这样绊跌也是预定的』——所指的是『那不信的人』（彼得前书 2:7-8）。被『丢在外面黑暗里』的，是那『又恶又懒』无用的仆人（马太福音 25:26, 30）；『往永刑里去』的，是『被咒诅的人』（25:41, 46）。换言之：一种出于神单纯专断意志或谕旨的『弃绝定罪论』（reprobation），圣经既无一处明文陈述，也无法从神话语的任何一段经文里按解经学、按情理推出。参主题词条：神人协作（004）；定罪（044）。
