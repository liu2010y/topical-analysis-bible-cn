正文对照 / Text

ENHOPE. Expectation; looking and waiting for. God its Source and Giver, by grace: Ps. 71:5. Thou art my hope, O Lord God. Ps. 39:7. My hope is in Thee. Ps. 146:5. Happy is he . . . whose hope is in the Lord his God. Ps. 147:11. Those that hope in His mercy. 1 Tim. 1:1. The Lord Jesus Christ, which is our hope. 2 Thes. 2:16. Good hope through grace. Ps. 119:114. I hope in Thy word. Allied with faith, love, patience, joy and peace: 1 Cor. 13:13. Now abideth faith, hope, love. Rom. 15:13. Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, in the power of the Holy Ghost. 1 Pet. 1:21. Your faith and hope might be in God. Rom. 5:4. Patience worketh experience (evidence by testing); and experience, hope. Rom. 15:4. That we through patience and comfort of the Scriptures might have hope. Hoping and waiting are interchangeably used in the Psalms: Ps. 130:5. I wait for the Lord . . . and in His word do I hope. Rom. 8:25. If we hope for that we see not, then do we with patience wait for it. The helmet of the believer's panoply: 1 Thes. 5:8. And for an helmet, the hope of salvation.

BSBRomans 15:13 Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you believe in Him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.

语法参：祈愿虚拟语气（The Lord bless thee） → 017 Now the God of hope fill you …：祈愿虚拟——动词原形 fill（但愿……充满你们）。
语法Patience worketh experience; and experience, hope：顶真＋后半省略动词（忍耐生老练，老练生盼望）；then do we with patience wait：do 强调式＋状语插入。

中盼望。期待；仰望等候。神本乎恩，是盼望的源头与赐予者：诗篇 71:5 —— 主耶和华啊，你是我所盼望的。39:7 —— 我的指望在乎你。146:5 —— 仰望耶和华他神的，这人便为有福。147:11 —— 盼望他慈爱的人。提摩太前书 1:1 —— 我们的盼望基督耶稣。帖撒罗尼迦后书 2:16 —— 因恩典……的盼望（英译：借恩典赐美好的盼望）。诗篇 119:114 —— 我甚仰望你的话语。与信、爱、忍耐、喜乐、平安联盟：哥林多前书 13:13 —— 如今常存的有信、有望、有爱。罗马书 15:13 —— 但愿使人有盼望的神，因信将诸般的喜乐平安充满你们的心，使你们借着圣灵的能力大有盼望。彼得前书 1:21 —— 叫你们的信心和盼望都在于神。罗马书 5:4 —— 忍耐生老练（受试验而得的凭据），老练生盼望。15:4 —— 叫我们因圣经所生的忍耐和安慰，可以得着盼望。诗篇中『盼望』与『等候』互用：诗篇 130:5 —— 我等候耶和华……我也仰望他的话。罗马书 8:25 —— 但我们若盼望那所不见的，就必忍耐等候。信徒全副军装中的头盔：帖撒罗尼迦前书 5:8 —— 把得救的盼望当作头盔戴上。

ENFruits or effects: Rom. 5:5. Hope maketh not ashamed. 1 John 3:3. Every one that hath this hope set on Him purifieth himself, even as He is pure. Heb. 6:19. Which hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and steadfast. Jer. 17:7. Blessed is the man . . . whose hope the Lord is. Rom. 12:12. Rejoicing in hope. Lam. 3:26. It is good that a man should hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord. Its sublime and blessed end: Rom. 8:24. We are saved by hope. Col. 1:27. Christ in you, the hope of glory. Col. 1:5. The hope which is laid up for you in the heavens. 1 Pet. 1:3, 4. Begotten us again unto a living hope, by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead, unto an inheritance . . . reserved in heaven. The believer encouraged and urged to hope: Ps. 42:5, 11. Hope thou in God. Zech. 9:12. Turn you to the stronghold, ye prisoners of hope. 1 Pet. 1:13. Set your hope perfectly on the grace that is to be brought unto you at the revelation of Jesus Christ. Heb. 6:11. Show diligence unto the fullness of hope, even to the end. 1 Pet. 3:15. Ready always to give answer to every man that asketh you a reason concerning the hope that is in you. See Waiting on God; Thirsting for God.

BSBHebrews 6:19 We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure. It enters the inner sanctuary behind the curtain,

语法参：him that / them that = he who / those who → 001 Every one that hath this hope / every man that asketh you：凡有这指望的／凡问你们的。
语法参：呼格 O + 称呼语 → 003 ye prisoners of hope：呼格——『被囚而有指望的人』（撒迦利亚名句）。
语法参：不用 do 的古体否定（Be not / Fret not） → 004 Hope maketh not ashamed：不用 do 的否定（盼望不至于羞耻）。

中盼望的果子与果效：罗马书 5:5 —— 盼望不至于羞耻。约翰一书 3:3 —— 凡向他有这指望的，就洁净自己，像他洁净一样。希伯来书 6:19 —— 我们有这指望，如同灵魂的锚，又坚固又牢靠。耶利米书 17:7 —— 倚靠耶和华、以耶和华为可靠的，那人有福了。罗马书 12:12 —— 在指望中要喜乐。耶利米哀歌 3:26 —— 人仰望耶和华，静默等候他的救恩，这原是好的。盼望崇高蒙福的归宿：罗马书 8:24 —— 我们得救是在乎盼望。歌罗西书 1:27 —— 基督在你们心里成了有荣耀的盼望。1:5 —— 是为那给你们存在天上的盼望。彼得前书 1:3-4 —— 借耶稣基督从死里复活，重生了我们，叫我们有活泼的盼望，可以得着……存留在天上的基业。劝勉催促信徒去盼望：诗篇 42:5, 11 —— 应当仰望神。撒迦利亚书 9:12 —— 你们被囚而有指望的人，都要转回保障。彼得前书 1:13 —— 专心盼望耶稣基督显现的时候所带来给你们的恩。希伯来书 6:11 —— 显出这样的殷勤，使你们有满足的指望，一直到底。彼得前书 3:15 —— 有人问你们心中盼望的缘由，就要常作准备……回答各人。参主题词条：等候神；渴慕神。
