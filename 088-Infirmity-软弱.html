正文对照 / Text

ENINFIRMITY, WEAKNESS, bodily or spiritual. Regarded and helped of God: Ps. 103:14. For He knoweth our frame; He remembereth that we are dust. Isa. 32:2. The shadow of a great rock in a weary land. Isa. 40:29. He giveth power to the faint. Isa. 50:4. That I should know how to sustain with words him that is weary. Lam. 3:22, 32. His compassions fail not. Matt. 8:17. Himself took our infirmities, and bare our sicknesses. Matt. 26:41. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak. Rom. 8:26. The Spirit also helpeth our infirmity. Heb. 4:15. Touched with the feeling of our infirmities. James 5:11. The Lord is very pitiful, and of tender mercy.

BSBPsalm 103:14 For He knows our frame; He is mindful that we are dust.

语法参：古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est → 001 knoweth / remembereth / giveth / helpeth：-eth 词尾群。
语法参：the + 形容词表一类人 → 001 the faint / the weary / the weak：the＋形容词表一类人——疲乏的、困倦的、软弱的。

中软弱，无力——身体的或灵性的。神眷顾并帮助软弱：诗篇 103:14 —— 因为他知道我们的本体，思念我们不过是尘土。以赛亚书 32:2 —— 像大磐石的影子在疲乏之地。40:29 —— 疲乏的，他赐能力。50:4 —— 使我知道怎样用言语扶助疲乏的人。耶利米哀歌 3:22, 32 —— 他的怜悯不至断绝。马太福音 8:17 —— 他代替我们的软弱，担当我们的疾病。26:41 —— 你们心灵固然愿意，肉体却软弱了。罗马书 8:26 —— 况且我们的软弱有圣灵帮助。希伯来书 4:15 —— 能体恤我们的软弱。雅各书 5:11 —— 主是满心怜悯，大有慈悲。

ENTo be regarded and helped by men: Isa. 35:3. Strengthen ye the weak hands, and confirm the feeble knees. Zech. 7:9. Show mercy and compassion every man to his brother. Acts 20:35. Ye ought to support the weak. Rom. 15:1. We then that are strong ought to bear the infirmities of the weak. 1 Thes. 5:14. Support the weak; be long-suffering toward all. Heb. 12:12. Lift up the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees. 1 Pet. 3:8. Be pitiful, be courteous. Gal. 6:1. If a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness. Illus. Luke 10:33. The Good Samaritan. 1 Cor. 9:22. To the weak became I as weak, that I might gain the weak. 2 Cor. 11:29. Who is weak, and I am not weak? See Anxiety; Despondency; Hope; Waiting on God.

BSBRomans 15:1 We who are strong ought to bear with the shortcomings of the weak and not to please ourselves.

语法参：though / if 从句中的虚拟语气（动词用原形） → 003 If a man be overtaken in a fault：条件句虚拟 be（若有人偶然被过犯所胜）。
语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 To the weak became I as weak：介词短语前置＋倒装（向软弱的人我就作软弱的人）。

中人也当眷顾并帮助软弱：以赛亚书 35:3 —— 你们要使软弱的手坚壮，无力的膝稳固。撒迦利亚书 7:9 —— 各人以慈爱怜悯弟兄。使徒行传 20:35 —— 又当扶助软弱的人。罗马书 15:1 —— 我们坚固的人应该担代不坚固人的软弱。帖撒罗尼迦前书 5:14 —— 扶助软弱的人，也要向众人忍耐。希伯来书 12:12 —— 所以，你们要把下垂的手、发酸的腿挺起来。彼得前书 3:8 —— 存慈怜谦卑的心。加拉太书 6:1 —— 若有人偶然被过犯所胜，你们属灵的人就当用温柔的心把他挽回过来。例证：路加福音 10:33 —— 好撒玛利亚人。哥林多前书 9:22 —— 向软弱的人，我就作软弱的人，为要得软弱的人。哥林多后书 11:29 —— 有谁软弱，我不软弱呢？参主题词条：忧虑（009）；灰心丧胆（057）；盼望（082）；等候神。【OCR 注：原书 "Rom. 5:1" 依经文（坚固的人应该担代）推定为罗马书 15:1。】
