一、今生临到邦国与个人的审判 / I. God's Judgments on Earth

ENJUDGMENT OF GOD. 1. Upon nations and individuals in this life. 2. The final and abiding judgment upon the race. I. God's judgments upon peoples and persons on earth—judgment His "strange work": Isa. 28:21. That He may do His work, His strange work; and bring to pass His act, His strange act. Ps. 9:16. The Lord is known by the judgment which He executeth. Ps. 99:4. Thou executest judgment and righteousness in Jacob. Ps. 103:6. The Lord executeth righteousness and judgment for all that are oppressed. Prov. 29:26. Every man's judgment cometh from the Lord. Jer. 23:5. A King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth. Rev. 16:7. True and righteous are Thy judgments. Dan. 7:9-14.

BSBIsaiah 28:21 For the LORD will rise up as at Mount Perazim. He will rouse Himself as in the Valley of Gibeon, to do His work, His strange work, and to perform His task, His disturbing task.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 True and righteous are Thy judgments：表语前置倒装。
语法参：古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est → 001 executeth / cometh / executest：施行／出于（-est 为第二人称词尾）。
语法His strange work / His strange act：strange 此处古义『非常的，异乎寻常的』——审判非神所乐为，故称『非常之工』。

中神的审判。1. 今生临到邦国与个人的审判。2. 临到全人类最终而恒久的审判。一、神在地上对万民与个人的审判——审判是他『非常的工』：以赛亚书 28:21 —— 他必兴起……好做成他的工，就是非常的工；成就他的事，就是奇异的事。诗篇 9:16 —— 耶和华已将自己显明了，他已施行审判。99:4 —— 你在雅各中施行公平和公义。103:6 —— 耶和华施行公义，为一切受屈的人伸冤。箴言 29:26 —— 定人事非乃在耶和华。耶利米书 23:5 —— 他必掌王权，行事有智慧，在地上施行公平和公义。启示录 16:7 —— 你的判断义哉，诚哉。但以理书 7:9-14。

ENAll His judgments executed solely in the interest of righteousness and mercy, of man's redemption: Isa. 26:9. For when Thy judgments are in the earth, the inhabitants of the world will learn righteousness. Isa. 30:18. Therefore will the Lord wait, that He may be gracious unto you; . . . for the Lord is a God of judgment. Ps. 101:1. I will sing of mercy and judgment. Ps. 89:14. Righteousness and judgment are the habitation of Thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before Thy face. Joel 2:13. Isa. 63:4. For the day of vengeance is in Mine heart, and the year of My redeemed is come. James 2:13. Mercy glorieth against judgment. Amos 4:12. Because I will do this unto thee, prepare to meet thy God, O Israel.

BSBIsaiah 26:9 My soul longs for You in the night; indeed, my spirit seeks You at dawn. For when Your judgments come upon the earth, the people of the world learn righteousness.

语法参：呼格 O + 称呼语 → 003 prepare to meet thy God, O Israel：呼格＋祈使——阿摩司的传世警句。
语法mercy and truth shall go before Thy face：拟人化（慈爱诚实如先导之使者行在前面）；glorieth against：向……夸胜（怜悯胜过审判）。

中他一切的审判都只为公义与怜悯、为人的救赎而施行：以赛亚书 26:9 —— 因为你在世上行审判的时候，地上的居民就学习公义。30:18 —— 耶和华必然等候，要施恩给你们……因为耶和华是公平的神。诗篇 101:1 —— 我要歌唱慈爱和公平。89:14 —— 公义和公平是你宝座的根基；慈爱和诚实行在你前面。约珥书 2:13。以赛亚书 63:4 —— 因为报仇之日在我心中，救赎我民之年已经来到。雅各书 2:13 —— 怜悯原是向审判夸胜。阿摩司书 4:12 —— 我必向你如此行；以色列啊，你当预备迎见你的神。

二、末后的审判：基督为审判者 / II. The Final Judgment; Christ the Judge

ENII. The final judgment; Christ the Judge: Ps. 9:8. He shall judge the world in righteousness. Acts 17:31. He hath appointed a day in the which He will judge the world in righteousness by that Man whom He hath ordained. Eccles. 3:17. God shall judge the righteous and the wicked. John 5:22. The Father . . . hath committed all judgment unto the Son. Description of the judgment: Matt. 25:31-46. (Note the determining principle of vs. 40, 45: "Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these My brethren, ye have done it unto Me"; "Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to Me.") Rom. 14:10. We shall all stand before the judgment-seat of Christ. 2 Cor. 5:10. That every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad. Heb. 9:27. It is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment. Rev. 20:11-15. I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened. See Condemnation.

BSB2 Corinthians 5:10 For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive his due for the things done in the body, whether good or bad.

语法Inasmuch as ye have done it … / Inasmuch as ye did it not …：inasmuch as『既然，就其……而论』的正反对句——绵羊山羊之别全押在这一个连词上。
语法参：介词前置的关系从句 to whom / of which → 001 a day in the which He will judge：in the which =【古】in which。

中二、末后的审判；基督为审判者：诗篇 9:8 —— 他要按公义审判世界。使徒行传 17:31 —— 因为他已经定了日子，要藉着他所设立的人按公义审判天下。传道书 3:17 —— 神必审判义人和恶人。约翰福音 5:22 —— 父……将审判的事全交与子。审判的描绘：马太福音 25:31-46。（请注意 40、45 节的判准原则：『这些事你们既做在我这弟兄中一个最小的身上，就是做在我身上了』；『这些事你们既不做在我这弟兄中一个最小的身上，就是不做在我身上了。』）罗马书 14:10 —— 我们都要站在神的台前（英译：基督的审判台前）。哥林多后书 5:10 —— 叫各人按着本身所行的，或善或恶受报。希伯来书 9:27 —— 按着定命，人人都有一死，死后且有审判。启示录 20:11-15 —— 我又看见死了的人，无论大小，都站在宝座前。案卷展开了。参主题词条：定罪（044）。
