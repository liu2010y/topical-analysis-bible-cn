正文对照 / Text

ENMESSIANIC REFERENCES AND PREDICTIONS. For a brief outline, see God (the Son: King, Messiah or Anointed of God; and the Old Testament references to Messiah and His rule); also Man (the Protevangelion, and the successive limitation of the promise—to the race of Shem, to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, to the tribe of Judah and the family of David—with the typical intimation of the method of deliverance in sacrifice, and the presentation of the Deliverer by Moses as Prophet, by David as King, and in the Epistle to the Hebrews as Priest, Himself both offerer and victim).

BSBLuke 24:27 And beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, He explained to them what was written in all the Scriptures about Himself.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 纯交叉索引条目——原书体例：大题已在别处展开时，此处只留指路牌；Messianic：弥赛亚的（Messiah 源自希伯来文 mashiach『受膏者』，希腊文对译即 Christos）。

中弥赛亚的经文与预言。简要纲目参见主题词条『神』（074，子神一节中『君王——弥赛亚，即神的受膏者』及旧约论弥赛亚及其治权的经文）；又参主题词条『人』（107，原始福音，以及应许逐步收窄的谱系——限于闪的族系，限于亚伯拉罕、以撒、雅各，最终限于犹大支派与大卫家；并以献祭预表拯救的方法，以及摩西把这位拯救者显为先知、大卫显为君王、希伯来书显为祭司——他自己既是献祭者又是祭牲）。
