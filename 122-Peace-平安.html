平安出于神，基督是平安 / Peace from God; Christ our Peace

ENPEACE. From God: Rom. 15:33. Now the God of peace be with you all. 2 Cor. 13:11. Heb. 13:20. Num. 6:26. The Lord lift up His countenance upon thee, and give thee peace. Ps. 29:11. The Lord will bless His people with peace. Ps. 85:8. He will speak peace unto His people. 2 Thes. 3:16. Now the Lord of peace Himself give you peace always by all means. Gal. 5:22. The fruit of the Spirit is . . . peace. Christ our Peace, and the Giver of peace: Luke 2:14. On earth peace, good will toward men. Isa. 53:5. The chastisement of our peace was upon Him. Micah 5:5. And this Man shall be the peace. Eph. 2:14. For He is our peace, who hath made both one. John 14:27. Peace I leave with you, My peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. John 16:33. These things I have spoken unto you, that in Me ye might have peace. [The last gift of Christ to His disciples before the Passion was the gift of His peace: not the peace of a still calm, untroubled by conflicts, but the peace which reigns supreme through the sorest trials, the sharpest agonies, the fiercest assaults—because it rests on the consciousness of an eternal sonship. We have no promise that we shall be free from suffering; it is enough that no suffering shall be fruitless which is seen in the issue of the Father's will. Bishop Westcott.]

BSBJohn 14:27 Peace I leave with you; My peace I give to you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled; do not be afraid.

语法参：祈愿虚拟语气（The Lord bless thee） → 017 Now the God of peace be with you / the Lord of peace Himself give you peace：祈愿虚拟两连（愿赐平安的神／主……）。
语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Peace I leave with you, My peace I give unto you：宾语前置两连（我留下平安给你们）——临别赠言的庄重语序。
语法the Passion：（大写特指）基督的受难；the issue of the Father's will：父旨意的结局（issue 古义『结局』）。

中平安。出于神：罗马书 15:33 —— 愿赐平安的神常和你们众人同在。哥林多后书 13:11。希伯来书 13:20。民数记 6:26 —— 愿耶和华向你仰脸，赐你平安。诗篇 29:11 —— 耶和华必赐平安的福给他的百姓。85:8 —— 因为他必应许将平安赐给他的百姓。帖撒罗尼迦后书 3:16 —— 愿赐平安的主随时随事亲自给你们平安。加拉太书 5:22 —— 圣灵所结的果子就是……和平。基督是我们的平安，也是平安的赐予者：路加福音 2:14 —— 在地上平安归与他所喜悦的人。以赛亚书 53:5 —— 因他受的刑罚我们得平安。弥迦书 5:5 —— 这位必作我们的平安。以弗所书 2:14 —— 因他使我们和睦（原文作因他是我们的和睦），将两下合而为一。约翰福音 14:27 —— 我留下平安给你们，我将我的平安赐给你们；我所赐的，不像世人所赐的。16:33 —— 我将这些事告诉你们，是要叫你们在我里面有平安。〔基督在受难之前赐给门徒最后的礼物，就是他平安的恩赐：那不是风平浪静、无争无扰的平安，而是在最痛的试炼、最尖锐的痛苦、最猛烈的攻击之中依然作王掌权的平安——因为它安放在『永远为子』这一自觉之上。我们并没有得着『必免于受苦』的应许；只要没有一样苦难是徒然的（凡在父旨意的结局中被看见的都不徒然），这就够了。韦斯科特主教（Bishop Westcott）〕

平安的条件与结局 / Conditions and Returns of Peace

ENGod's thoughts of peace: Jer. 29:11. I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the Lord, thoughts of peace, and not of evil. His covenant of peace: Isa. 54:10. Neither shall the covenant of My peace be removed. Ezek. 34:25. Conditions of peace—trust: Isa. 26:3. Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on Thee: because he trusteth in Thee. Rom. 15:13. Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing. Being justified: Rom. 5:1. Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Love of the Word: Ps. 119:165. Great peace have they which love Thy law: and nothing shall offend them. Following the paths of wisdom: Prov. 3:17. Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace. Rightness of life: Isa. 32:17. And the work of righteousness shall be peace. Isa. 48:18. O that thou hadst hearkened to My commandments! then had thy peace been as a river. James 3:18. The fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace. Spiritual-mindedness: Rom. 8:6. To be spiritually minded is life and peace. The Kingdom of God in the heart: Rom. 14:17. The kingdom of God is . . . righteousness, and peace, and joy in the Holy Ghost.

BSBIsaiah 26:3 You will keep in perfect peace the steadfast of mind, because he trusts in You.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Great peace have they which love Thy law / then had thy peace been as a river：宾语前置倒装＋省略 if 的虚拟倒装（你的平安就如河水）。
语法stayed on Thee：仗赖你（stay 古义『倚靠』）；O that thou hadst hearkened：O that＋过去完成虚拟的惋惜句（甚愿你素来听从）。

中神所怀的是赐平安的意念：耶利米书 29:11 —— 耶和华说：我知道我向你们所怀的意念，是赐平安的意念，不是降灾祸的意念。他平安的约：以赛亚书 54:10 —— 我的和平之约也不迁移。以西结书 34:25。平安的条件——信靠：以赛亚书 26:3 —— 坚心倚赖你的，你必保守他十分平安，因为他倚靠你。罗马书 15:13 —— 但愿使人有盼望的神，因信将诸般的喜乐平安充满你们的心。因信称义：罗马书 5:1 —— 我们既因信称义，就借着我们的主耶稣基督得与神相和。爱慕圣道：诗篇 119:165 —— 爱你律法的人有大平安，什么都不能使他们绊脚。行走智慧的路：箴言 3:17 —— 她的道是安乐，她的路全是平安。生活的正直：以赛亚书 32:17 —— 公义的果效必是平安。48:18 —— 甚愿你素来听从我的命令，你的平安就如河水。雅各书 3:18 —— 并且使人和睦的，是用和平所栽种的义果。心思属灵：罗马书 8:6 —— 体贴圣灵的乃是生命平安。神的国在心里：罗马书 14:17 —— 神的国……只在乎公义、和平并圣灵中的喜乐。

ENPresent and ultimate returns: Phil. 4:6, 7. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus. Ps. 37:37. Mark the perfect man, and behold the upright: for the end of that man is peace. Isa. 57:2. He shall enter into peace. To be sought, and how: Ps. 34:14. Seek peace, and pursue it. Job 22:21. Acquaint now thyself with Him, and be at peace: thereby good shall come unto thee. Zech. 8:19. Love the truth and peace. Col. 3:15. And let the peace of God rule in your hearts. Isa. 27:5. Let him take hold of My strength, that he may make peace with Me; and he shall make peace with Me. No peace to the wicked: Isa. 48:22; 57:21. There is no peace, saith my God, to the wicked.

BSBPhilippians 4:6, 7 6 Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. 7 And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

语法参：古英语动词词尾 -eth / -est → 001 passeth：出人意外的（原文『超过一切心思』）。
语法参：Let + 第三人称祈使句 → 003 let the peace of God rule / Let him take hold of My strength：let 祈使两连。

中现今与终极的回报：腓立比书 4:6-7 —— 神所赐出人意外的平安，必在基督耶稣里保守你们的心怀意念。诗篇 37:37 —— 你要细察那完全人，观看那正直人，因为和平人有好结局。以赛亚书 57:2 —— 他们得享（原文作进入）平安。当如何寻求：诗篇 34:14 —— 寻求和睦，一心追赶。约伯记 22:21 —— 你要认识神，就得平安，福气也必临到你。撒迦利亚书 8:19 —— 所以你们要喜爱诚实与和平。歌罗西书 3:15 —— 又要叫基督的平安在你们心里作主。以赛亚书 27:5 —— 不然，让他持住我的能力，使他与我和好，愿他与我和好。恶人毫无平安：以赛亚书 48:22；57:21 —— 我的神说：恶人必不得平安。
