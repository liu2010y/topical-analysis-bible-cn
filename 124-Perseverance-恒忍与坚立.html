ENThe New Testament exhortations to steadfastness and endurance to the end find abundant illustration in all the better men and women of Old Testament history—alike in the times of the Patriarchs, of the Exodus and Wanderings, of the Judges, Kings and Prophets. A main and vital purpose of the detailed personal histories is to inculcate the lesson needed by all in all time: that the only fulfilled and compensated life is that which holds on unwaveringly in the way of obedience and trust, of humility and truth, of submission to Divine ordering, and of unswerving fidelity in all appointed service, unto the end of the testing period. God's part and man's part in this momentous matter find still further exposition under Agency, Divine and Human. Both themes—Agency and Perseverance—are based upon the same facts of demanded human action and assured Divine co-operation. [We hold out because Christ holds us. It is not our grip on Him that counts, but His grip on us. "We stand by faith," but it is in Him: He is able "exceeding abundantly" to make us stand; and we go on in Him, and grow in Him. Christ also fights through His people, subdues kingdoms and works righteousness thus: their words, echo of His, sweep the world, causing empires of wrong to totter and fall. In Christ the Christian is always mightier than even his most extravagantly sanguine thought: his deeds beat his hopes, because, beyond his sight, Christ puts the pith of efficiency into those deeds. Always "above all" that the believer "asks or thinks" is Christ able.]

中新约中关于坚定不移、忍耐到底的劝勉，在旧约历史中一切较好的男女身上都得着丰富的例证——无论在列祖时代、出埃及与旷野飘流时代，还是士师、列王与先知时代。那些详尽的个人生平记事，其一个主要而攸关的目的，正是要把一条历世历代人人都需要的功课刻进人心：唯一得着成全、得着补偿的人生，是那种在顺服与信靠之路上、在谦卑与真诚之路上、在顺从神安排之路上、在一切所派服事上毫不动摇的忠信之路上，一直坚持到受试期终了的人生。在这攸关重大的事上，神的份与人的份如何，另见主题词条：神人协作（004）中更详的阐释。这两个主题——协作与恒忍——都建立在同一组事实之上：所要求的人的行动，与所保证的神的同工。〔我们能坚持住，是因为基督抓住我们。要紧的不是我们抓他的那一把力气，而是他抓我们的那一把力气。『我们凭信站立』，但那是在他里面站立：他能『充充足足地』使我们站住；我们也在他里面往前走、在他里面长大。基督也借着他的子民争战：他这样制伏敌国、施行公义；他们的话语——是他话语的回声——扫荡世界，使不义的帝国摇撼倾覆。在基督里，基督徒总是比他自己最夸张乐观的想象更有力：他的作为超过他的期望，因为在他视线之外，基督把成事的精髓放进了那些作为里。基督的能力，总是『超过』信徒所『求所想』的。〕
