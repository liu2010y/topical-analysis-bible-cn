祷告的本质与根基 / The Nature and Ground of Prayer

ENPRAYER. Call; cry; supplication. Comprehensively, communion with God. Essentially, prayer is the glad, grateful and heartfelt response of the believer to God's proffer to meet and commune with him. In this, as in all that transpires between God and man, it is God who takes the initiative, and invites the outpouring of all that is in the heart into His sympathizing ear. Two impressive texts assure us upon these points. (1) Ex. 25:22. And there I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee from above the mercy-seat, from between the two cherubim. This "figure of the true" (Heb. 9:24)—the blood-sprinkled mercy-seat, the "throne of grace" (Heb. 4:16), bespeaking atonement (covering) of sin—still applies to us with all its blessed meaning and results, while it finds full and abiding realization in the perfect communion of heaven. (Note: God comes to meet and commune with the believer on the mercy-seat; but the unbeliever goes to meet with God upon the judgment-seat—2 Cor. 5:10; Rev. 20:11, 12.) (2) Ps. 62:8. Trust in Him at all times; ye people, pour out your heart before Him: God is a refuge for us. In public or in private, the communing soul must feel, and be, alone with God: Matt. 6:6. Enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret.

BSBPsalm 62:8 Trust in Him at all times, O people; pour out your hearts before Him. God is our refuge. Selah

语法proffer：主动提议、递上（神先递出相会之约）；transpires：发生；bespeaking：述说、表明（bespeak）；a figure of the true：真体的影像（来 9:24）。
语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 it is God who takes the initiative：it is … who 强调（是神先采取主动）——本条祷告论的立足点。

中祷告。呼求；呼吁；恳求。总括而言，就是与神相交。就其本质，祷告是信徒对神『前来相会、彼此交谈』这一提议所作的欢欣、感恩、发自肺腑的回应。在这件事上，正如在神与人之间发生的一切事上一样，是神先采取主动，邀请人把心中的一切倾倒进他体恤的耳中。有两处感人的经文向我们保证这几点。（1）出埃及记 25:22 —— 我要在那里与你相会，又要从……施恩座上二基路伯中间，和你说我所要吩咐你……的一切事。这『真体的影像』（希伯来书 9:24）——那洒了血的施恩座、那『施恩的宝座』（希伯来书 4:16），述说着罪的赎（遮盖）——至今仍以其全部蒙福的含义与果效临到我们；而它在天上完全的相交中才得着完满恒久的实现。（按语：神是在施恩座上前来与信徒相会相交；而不信的人则要在审判台前去见神——哥林多后书 5:10；启示录 20:11-12。）（2）诗篇 62:8 —— 你们众民当时时倚靠他，在他面前倾心吐意；神是我们的避难所。无论在公开场合还是私下，与神相交的灵魂都必须感到、也确实是单独与神相对：马太福音 6:6 —— 你祷告的时候，要进你的内屋，关上门，祷告你在暗中的父。

ENThe name and mediation of Christ are the basis of access to God, and the ground of faith, boldness and hope in prayer: John 14:13, 14. Whatsoever ye shall ask in My name, that will I do. John 16:24, 26. Hitherto have ye asked nothing in My name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full. 1 Tim. 2:5. There is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus. Eph. 2:18. For through Him we both have access by one Spirit unto the Father. Rom. 8:34. Who also maketh intercession for us. Heb. 7:25. He ever liveth to make intercession for them. Eph. 3:12. In whom we have boldness and access with confidence by the faith of Him. Heb. 4:14-16. Seeing then that we have a great High Priest . . . Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast our profession. Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need. The Holy Spirit is the Inspirer and Helper in prayer: Jude 20. Praying in the Holy Ghost. Rom. 8:26. The Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought. Zech. 12:10. I will pour upon the house of David . . . the spirit of grace and of supplications. Eph. 6:18. Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit.

BSBHebrews 4:16 Let us then approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help us in our time of need.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Hitherto have ye asked nothing in My name：hitherto 前置＋倒装（向来你们没有奉我的名求什么）。
语法参：Let + 第三人称祈使句 → 003 let us hold fast … let us come boldly：let 祈使两连（希伯来书的劝勉句式）。

中基督的名与他的中保之职，是人得以亲近神的根基，也是祷告中信心、坦然与盼望的凭据：约翰福音 14:13-14 —— 你们奉我的名无论求什么，我必成就。16:24, 26 —— 向来你们没有奉我的名求什么，如今你们求，就必得着，叫你们的喜乐可以满足。提摩太前书 2:5 —— 因为只有一位神，在神和人中间，只有一位中保，乃是降世为人的基督耶稣。以弗所书 2:18 —— 因为我们两下借着他被一个圣灵所感，得以进到父面前。罗马书 8:34 —— 也替我们祈求。希伯来书 7:25 —— 因为他是长远活着，替他们祈求。以弗所书 3:12 —— 我们因信耶稣，就在他里面放胆无惧，笃信不疑地来到神面前。希伯来书 4:14-16 —— 我们既然有一位已经升入高天尊荣的大祭司，就是神的儿子耶稣，便当持定所承认的道。所以我们只管坦然无惧地来到施恩的宝座前，为要得怜恤，蒙恩惠，作随时的帮助。圣灵是祷告中的激发者与帮助者：犹大书 20 —— 在圣灵里祷告。罗马书 8:26 —— 况且我们的软弱有圣灵帮助，我们本不晓得当怎样祷告。撒迦利亚书 12:10 —— 我必将那施恩叫人恳求的灵，浇灌大卫家。以弗所书 6:18 —— 靠着圣灵，随时多方祷告祈求。

祷告的四大构成 / The Constituents of Prayer

ENConstituents or parts of prayer. (1) Praise—including ascriptions of honor, holiness, majesty, might, blessing and glory, and expressions of adoration, homage, reverence and devotion. All these are commanded and illustrated in the Psalms, in the recorded prayers of both Testaments, and in the celestial pictures of the Apocalypse. (2) Thanksgiving—repeatedly demanded and expressed by psalmists, prophets and leaders in the Old Testament, and commended in the New: Phil. 4:6. In every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. Col. 3:15. And be ye thankful. Eph. 5:20. Giving thanks always for all things unto God. Ps. 100:4. Be thankful unto Him, and bless His name. Ps. 50:14. Offer unto God thanksgiving. (3) Penitent confession: 1 John 1:9. If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins. Prov. 28:13. Whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy. Ps. 32:5. I said, I will confess my transgressions unto the Lord; and Thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin. Ps. 51. Isa. 6:5. Woe is me! for I am undone. (4) Supplication (asking, entreaty), including intercession—this is the chief constituent of prayer. In the Old Testament God says, Call upon Me: Ps. 50:15. Call upon Me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee. Ps. 105:4. Seek His face evermore. Isa. 65:24. Jer. 33:3. Call unto Me, and I will answer thee. Matt. 7:7, 8. Ask, and it shall be given you; . . . for every one that asketh receiveth. 1 Tim. 2:1, 2. I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions . . . be made for all men.

BSBPhilippians 4:6 Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 四大构成的编号纲目：赞美—感谢—认罪—恳求（英文首字母常缩写为 ACTS：Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Supplication，此处次序略异）。
语法参：him that / them that = he who / those who → 001 every one that asketh receiveth / Whoso confesseth and forsaketh：凡祈求的／承认离弃的。

中祷告的构成部分。（1）赞美——包括把尊荣、圣洁、威严、权能、称颂与荣耀归给神，以及倾慕、致敬、敬畏与虔诚的表达。这一切在诗篇中、在两约所记的祷告中、在启示录的天上图景中，都既有吩咐也有范例。（2）感谢——旧约的诗人、先知与领袖屡屡要求并表达，新约也加以嘉许：腓立比书 4:6 —— 只要凡事借着祷告、祈求和感谢，将你们所要的告诉神。歌罗西书 3:15 —— 且要存感谢的心。以弗所书 5:20 —— 凡事要奉我们主耶稣基督的名常常感谢父神。诗篇 100:4 —— 当感谢他，称颂他的名。50:14 —— 你们要以感谢为祭献与神。（3）痛悔的认罪：约翰一书 1:9 —— 我们若认自己的罪，神是信实的，是公义的，必要赦免我们的罪。箴言 28:13 —— 承认离弃罪过的，必蒙怜恤。诗篇 32:5 —— 我说：我要向耶和华承认我的过犯，你就赦免我的罪恶。51 篇。以赛亚书 6:5 —— 祸哉！我灭亡了！（4）恳求（祈求、求告），包括代求——这是祷告的主体构成。旧约中神说：你要求告我。诗篇 50:15 —— 并要在患难之日求告我，我必搭救你。105:4 —— 时常寻求他的面。以赛亚书 65:24。耶利米书 33:3 —— 你求告我，我就应允你。马太福音 7:7-8 —— 你们祈求，就给你们……因为凡祈求的，就得着。提摩太前书 2:1-2 —— 我劝你第一要为万人恳求、祷告、代求、祝谢。

ENResponsive to these constituents, we note the attitude and spirit of the accepted suppliant, suggesting the conditions of acceptable prayer. 1. Reverent, praiseful recognition of God's greatness and majesty, His holiness and justice, His love and truth (Ps. 89:7, 8; 95:3, 6; 96:8, 9; 99:5, 9). 2. Thankfulness for providential goodness and pardoning mercy (Ps. 103:1-13; 107; 121:1-4; 145; 1 Chron. 29:11-13). 3. A feeling of dependence and need, with a penitent sense of unworthiness and sin: Ps. 51:17. The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, Thou wilt not despise. Ps. 86:1. Bow down Thine ear, O Lord, hear me: for I am poor and needy. Luke 18:13. God be merciful to me a sinner. 2 Cor. 3:5. Not that we are sufficient of ourselves to think any thing as of ourselves. 4. Humble submission and obedient self-surrender: Ps. 119:94. I am Thine, save me. Ps. 119:176. I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek Thy servant. Job 34:32. If I have done iniquity, I will do no more. (Illustrations: Jacob, Gen. 32:9-12; Job 42:5, 6; Isaiah 6:5-8; the prodigal, Luke 15:17-21.) 5. Heart-trust in Christ, and assured reliance on the promises of God to hear and answer: Matt. 9:29. According to your faith be it unto you. Mark 11:24. What things soever ye desire, when ye pray, believe that ye receive them, and ye shall have them. Heb. 11:6. He that cometh to God must believe that He is, and that He is a rewarder of them that diligently seek Him. James 1:6. But let him ask in faith, nothing wavering. 6. Patient expectation, and hopeful, assured waiting upon God's time and way of answer: Micah 7:7. I will wait for the God of my salvation: my God will hear me. Ps. 123:2. Our eyes wait upon the Lord our God, until that He have mercy upon us. 7. Pure hands, a whole and true heart, an abiding in Christ, and a Christ-serving life: Lam. 3:41. Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God. 1 Tim. 2:8. Lifting up holy hands. Ps. 145:18. The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon Him in truth. John 4:24. They that worship Him must worship Him in spirit and in truth. Heb. 10:22. Let us draw near with a true heart. John 15:7. If ye abide in Me, and My words abide in you, ye shall ask what ye will, and it shall be done unto you. James 5:16. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much. 1 John 3:22. We receive of Him, because we keep His commandments, and do those things that are pleasing in His sight.

BSBJames 5:16 Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous man has great power to prevail.

语法参：纲要式标题句（并列名词短语，无谓语） → 001 七条编号的『蒙悦纳条件』——本词条的实践核心，可视作一份祷告自检表。
语法参：呼格 O + 称呼语 → 003 a broken and a contrite heart, O God, Thou wilt not despise：呼格插入＋宾语前置（诗 51:17）。
语法nothing wavering：一点不疑惑（分词短语作方式状语）；effectual fervent：切实火热的（雅 5:16 钦定本连用两形容词）。

中与这些构成相对应，我们要看看蒙悦纳之祈求者的心态与心志——它们提示了祷告蒙悦纳的条件。1. 以敬畏与赞美之心认取神的伟大威严、他的圣洁公义、他的慈爱信实（诗篇 89:7-8；95:3, 6；96:8-9；99:5, 9）。2. 为护理的良善与赦罪的怜悯而感恩（诗篇 103:1-13；107 篇；121:1-4；145 篇；历代志上 29:11-13）。3. 自觉倚赖与缺乏，并痛悔地自觉不配与有罪：诗篇 51:17 —— 神所要的祭就是忧伤的灵；神啊，忧伤痛悔的心，你必不轻看。86:1 —— 耶和华啊，求你侧耳应允我，因我是困苦穷乏的。路加福音 18:13 —— 神啊，开恩可怜我这个罪人！哥林多后书 3:5 —— 并不是我们凭自己能承担什么事。4. 谦卑降服与顺命的自我交出：诗篇 119:94 —— 我是属你的，求你救我。119:176 —— 我如亡羊走迷了路，求你寻找仆人。约伯记 34:32 —— 我若行了孽，必不再行。（例证：雅各——创世记 32:9-12；约伯记 42:5-6；以赛亚书 6:5-8；浪子——路加福音 15:17-21。）5. 心里信靠基督，笃信神垂听应允的应许：马太福音 9:29 —— 照着你们的信给你们成全了吧。马可福音 11:24 —— 凡你们祷告祈求的，无论是什么，只要信是得着的，就必得着。希伯来书 11:6 —— 因为到神面前来的人必须信有神，且信他赏赐那寻求他的人。雅各书 1:6 —— 只要凭着信心求，一点不疑惑。6. 耐心期待，满怀盼望而笃定地等候神应允的时候与方式：弥迦书 7:7 —— 要等候那救我的神；我的神必应允我。诗篇 123:2 —— 我们的眼睛也照样望耶和华我们的神，直到他怜悯我们。7. 清洁的手、完全真实的心、常在基督里、并过事奉基督的生活：耶利米哀歌 3:41 —— 我们当诚心向天上的神举手祷告。提摩太前书 2:8 —— 举起圣洁的手。诗篇 145:18 —— 凡求告他的，就是诚心求告他的，耶和华便与他们相近。约翰福音 4:24 —— 所以拜他的必须用心灵和诚实拜他。希伯来书 10:22 —— 我们心中天良的亏欠已经洒去……就当存着诚心……来到神面前。约翰福音 15:7 —— 你们若常在我里面，我的话也常在你们里面，凡你们所愿意的，祈求就给你们成就。雅各书 5:16 —— 义人祈祷所发的力量是大有功效的。约翰一书 3:22 —— 并且我们一切所求的，就从他得着，因为我们遵守他的命令，行他所喜悦的事。

祷告受阻的原因；神垂听的应许 / Hindered Prayers; God the Hearer

ENHindered and rejected prayers—causes and effects: Job 15:4. Yea, thou castest off fear, and restrainest prayer before God. Job 21:15. What profit should we have, if we pray unto Him? Job 35:13. Surely God will not hear vanity. Ps. 66:18. If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me. Isa. 59:2. Your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid His face from you, that He will not hear. Matt. 6:7. Use not vain repetitions, as the heathen do. James 4:3. Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts. 1 Pet. 3:7. That your prayers be not hindered. Prov. 15:8. The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord. (Christ's refusals: the ambition of James and John, Mark 10:35; the call for vengeance, Luke 9:54.) Other conditions or limitations to favorable response: 1. Requests must be in harmony with His gracious will, and contributory to His glory: 1 John 5:14. If we ask any thing according to His will, He heareth us. John 14:13. That the Father may be glorified in the Son. 2. They must come from a loyal and obedient, forgiving and loving, unselfish heart and life: Matt. 6:15. But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses. James 4:3. 3. Answers are withheld for lack of asking: James 4:2. Ye have not, because ye ask not. Mal. 3:10. Prove Me now herewith, saith the Lord of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven. Ezek. 36:37. I will yet for this be inquired of by the house of Israel, to do it for them. [I know not which is the greater wonder: that prayer—a duty so easy and facile, so ready and adapted to the powers and opportunities of every man—should have so great effects and be productive of such mighty blessings; or that we should be so unwilling to use so easy an instrument of procuring so much good. Jeremy Taylor.]

BSBPsalm 66:18 If I had cherished iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not have listened.
