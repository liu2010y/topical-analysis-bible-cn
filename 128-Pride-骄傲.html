骄傲的来源与神的憎恶 / Whence It Springs; Hated by God

ENPRIDE. Proud; loftiness; haughtiness; arrogancy. Whence it springs. Not from the Father: 1 John 2:16. For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world. But out of the heart: Mark 7:21, 22. For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts . . . pride, foolishness. Ps. 10:4. The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek after God: God is not in all his thoughts. Jer. 48:29. We have heard the pride of Moab, (he is exceeding proud) his loftiness, and his arrogancy, and his pride, and the haughtiness of his heart. Obad. 3. The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock. Hated by God and by all that fear Him: Prov. 8:13. The fear of the Lord is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate. Prov. 16:5. Every one that is proud in heart is an abomination to the Lord: though hand join in hand, he shall not be unpunished.

BSBProverbs 16:5 Everyone who is proud in heart is detestable to the LORD; be assured that he will not go unpunished.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate：宾语整串前置＋do 强调倒装（这些都为我所恨恶）——箴 8:13 的重锤式句法。
语法参：呼格 O + 称呼语 → 003 thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock：呼格＋that 关系式＋-est 词尾（住在山穴中的啊）。
语法whence：从何处（古疑问副词）；froward：乖谬的（古义，与 forward 无关）；though hand join in hand：即使彼此连手（互相勾结），也必不免受罚。

中骄傲。自高；高傲；狂傲；自大。它从何而来。不是从父来的：约翰一书 2:16 —— 因为凡世界上的事，就像肉体的情欲、眼目的情欲，并今生的骄傲，都不是从父来的，乃是从世界来的。乃是『从心里出来』：马可福音 7:21-22 —— 因为从里面，就是从人心里，发出恶念……骄傲、狂妄。诗篇 10:4 —— 恶人面带骄傲，说：耶和华必不追究。他一切所想的都以为没有神。耶利米书 48:29 —— 我们听说摩押人骄傲，是极其骄傲；听说他自高自傲，并且狂妄，居心自大。俄巴底亚书 3 —— 住在山穴中、居所在高处的啊，你因狂傲自欺。为神并一切敬畏神的人所憎恶：箴言 8:13 —— 敬畏耶和华在乎恨恶邪恶；那骄傲、狂妄，并恶道，以及乖谬的口，都为我所恨恶。16:5 —— 凡心里骄傲的，为耶和华所憎恶；虽然连手，他必不免受罚。

ENPresent returns of pride: Prov. 11:2. When pride cometh, then cometh shame: but with the lowly is wisdom. Prov. 13:10. Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom. Prov. 16:18. Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall. Prov. 18:12. Before destruction the heart of man is haughty, and before honor is humility. Prov. 29:23. A man's pride shall bring him low: but honor shall uphold the humble in spirit. Hardening of heart: Dan. 5:20. But when his heart was lifted up, and his mind hardened in pride, he was deposed from his kingly throne, and they took his glory from him. Ps. 119:21. Thou hast rebuked the proud that are cursed, which do err from Thy commandments. How God deals with the proud: James 4:6; 1 Pet. 5:5. God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble. Isa. 2:11, 12, 17. The lofty looks of man shall be humbled, and the haughtiness of men shall be bowed down, and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day. For the day of the Lord of hosts shall be upon every one that is proud and lofty. Isa. 10:33. And the haughty shall be humbled. Therefore: Jer. 13:15-17. Hear ye, and give ear; be not proud: for the Lord hath spoken. Give glory to the Lord your God, before He cause darkness . . . But if ye will not hear it, my soul shall weep in secret places for your pride.

BSBProverbs 16:18 Pride goes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 When pride cometh, then cometh shame / Only by pride cometh contention / with the lowly is wisdom：连续三处主谓倒装——箴言体常以倒装制造对偶节奏。
语法参：the + 形容词表一类人 → 001 the proud … the humble … the haughty：the + 形容词表一类人（骄傲的人／谦卑的人／高傲的人）。
语法goeth before：行在……之前（16:18『骄傲在败坏以先』）；deposed：被革去（王位）；resisteth：抵挡、阻挡（-eth 古词尾）。

中骄傲当下的报应：箴言 11:2 —— 骄傲来，羞耻也来；谦逊人却有智慧。13:10 —— 骄傲只启争竞；听劝言的，却有智慧。16:18 —— 骄傲在败坏以先，狂心在跌倒之前。18:12 —— 败坏之先，人心骄傲；尊荣以前，必有谦卑。29:23 —— 人的高傲必使他卑下；心里谦逊的，必得尊荣。心的刚硬：但以理书 5:20 —— 但他心高气傲，灵也刚愎，甚至行事狂傲，就被革去王位，夺去荣耀。诗篇 119:21 —— 受咒诅、偏离你命令的骄傲人，你已经责备他们。神如何对待骄傲的人：雅各书 4:6；彼得前书 5:5 —— 神阻挡骄傲的人，赐恩给谦卑的人。以赛亚书 2:11-12, 17 —— 到那日，眼目高傲的必降为卑，性情狂傲的都必屈膝，惟独耶和华被尊崇。必有万军耶和华降罚的一个日子，要临到骄傲狂妄的。10:33 —— 高傲的必被砍下。所以：耶利米书 13:15-17 —— 你们当听，当侧耳而听，不要骄傲，因为耶和华已经说了。耶和华你们的神未使黑暗来到……你们当将荣耀归给他。你们若不听这话，我必因你们的骄傲在暗地哭泣。
