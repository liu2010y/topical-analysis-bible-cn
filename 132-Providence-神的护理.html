护理的定义与三重区分 / Definition and Three Distinctions

ENPROVIDENCE. One of the two elements included in the comprehensive term Grace (see Grace). The Scriptural word for Providence is Goodness, which expresses the kindly and beneficent treatment of God toward all His creatures, rational and irrational, in upholding and disposing of them, and controlling all events which affect their condition and destiny. Scripture texts and passages intimate three distinctions in the method of God's providential dealing: 1, with reference to the irrational animal creation; 2, with reference to all men, irrespective of moral character; 3, with reference to individual believers, and to the believing body, the Church. I. The providence of God toward irrational creatures is limited to provision adapted to each class or species, and bestowed in accordance with the instinct imparted to each: Job 38:41. Who provideth for the raven his food? Ps. 36:6. O Lord, Thou preservest man and beast. Ps. 104:14. He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man. Ps. 145:15. The eyes of all wait upon Thee; and Thou givest them their meat in due season. Ps. 147:9. He giveth to the beast his food, and to the young ravens which cry. II. Providence toward men, the evil and the good: Ps. 21:3. For Thou preventest him with the blessings of goodness. Ps. 65:9-11. Thou visitest the earth, and waterest it: Thou greatly enrichest it. Ps. 107:4-31 (a series of deliverances from peril, followed by instances of God's goodness, and concluded with the call to observe His hand in all events, ver. 43). Matt. 5:45. For He maketh His sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust. All this confirms the original pledges to Noah of the perpetuity of day and night, of the seasons and a fruitful earth, and the charters of food given to Adam and to Noah. Paul intimates God's purpose in His providential dealings: Rom. 2:4. Not knowing that the goodness of God leadeth thee to repentance.

中护理（Providence）。这是『恩典』这一总称所含两大要素之一（参主题词条：恩典 Grace）。圣经中与『护理』相当的词是『良善／恩慈』（Goodness），它表达神对一切受造物——无论有理性的还是无理性的——所施的慈爱与厚待：托住他们、安排他们，并掌管一切影响其境况与结局的事。圣经的经文与段落提示：神施行护理的方式有三重区分：一、对无理性的动物界；二、对一切世人，不分其道德品格；三、对个别信徒，以及信徒的团体——教会。一、神对无理性受造之物的护理，只限于按各类各族所需而供给，并照赐给各类的本能而施与：约伯记 38:41 —— 乌鸦之雏因无食物飞来飞去哀鸣的时候，谁为它预备食物呢？诗篇 36:6 —— 耶和华啊，人民、牲畜，你都救护。104:14 —— 他使草生长，给六畜吃，使菜蔬发长，供给人用。145:15 —— 万民都举目仰望你，你随时给他们食物。147:9 —— 他赐食给走兽和啼叫的小乌鸦。二、对世人的护理，无论恶人善人：诗篇 21:3 —— 你以美福迎接他。65:9-11 —— 你眷顾地，降下透雨，使地大得肥美。107:4-31（一连串脱险的记述，随后是神良善的实例，最后以第 43 节呼吁人在万事中留意他的手作结）。马太福音 5:45 —— 因为他叫日头照好人，也照歹人；降雨给义人，也给不义的人。这一切都印证了神起初向挪亚所立的凭据：昼夜、四季与丰产之地永不止息；也印证了赐给亚当与挪亚的食物之约。保罗提示了神在护理作为中的用意：罗马书 2:4 —— 岂不晓得他的恩慈是领你悔改呢？

神在护理中自设的两重界限 / Divinely Established Limitations

ENDivinely established limitations to divine working in providence. God's working in providence must always be in harmony with His methods of working in nature—that is, in accordance with His established natural laws. God will not interfere to protect or deliver any one from the hurtful effect of the infraction of natural law, though it be done ignorantly and without wrong purpose. Such interposition could only be through the temporary setting aside of the infringed law—that is, by miracle. Yet it is just at this point that so many sufferers, or their friends, question or deny the goodness of God in reference to particular providential events. The simplest consideration shows the baselessness of this unjust implication and charge. The taking of disease by exposure to its germs, or by heedless disregard of the laws of health, or the careless and unwitting encounter with any of a thousand sources of bodily peril, must lead to sickness, hurt, and even death. But in the prevention of these and kindred results it is plain that God cannot intervene save by miraculous energy; and this would involve an utter overturning of the established order. [Undoubtedly the Lord can cure disease as readily now as when He was upon the earth; and one may feel aggrieved that He does not interpose on behalf of a suffering friend or near relative. But He is impartial and all-loving. He must consider the welfare of all His children and of the whole race; and that welfare requires that the laws of organic nature shall prevail. He cannot reverse or repeal those laws without bringing upon the race disaster compared with which present ills are as nothing. We have no right to ask Him to make us exceptions to the common lot—no right to ask for special exemptions which it would be impossible to grant to all. It is ours to bear the discipline of the present order of our existence, and to be strengthened in our immortal being by it. ENGod's providence finds further limitation in the divinely ordered independence of man's own will. By God's directly appointed and authorized free-will, the responsibility is placed upon man for all forms and results of his acting, alike in his dealings with outward affairs and with his moral obligations. He cannot, therefore, rightly charge the effects of his own improvidence to the providence of God; nor can he attribute the consequences of his wilful wrongdoing either to the providence or to the original purpose of God. Apart from these divinely established limitations—through God's own creation and appointment of second causes in nature and in man's independent will—His providence absolutely directs and controls all particular events to the production of all actual issues, of smaller or greater moment, alike affecting individuals and nations. Thus, and thus alone, has history, personal and national, been wrought upon the earth. Providence and prayer: the considerations above apply equally to providential answers to prayer.
