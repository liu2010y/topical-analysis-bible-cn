为属灵生命更新所辑的经文 / Texts for Renewal of Spiritual Life

ENREVIVAL TEXTS—for the renewal of spiritual life, of the individual or of the Church. 2 Chron. 7:14. If My people, which are called by My name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek My face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land. Ps. 80:3, 19. Turn us again, O God, and cause Thy face to shine; and we shall be saved. Ps. 80:18. Quicken us, and we will call upon Thy name. Ps. 85:6. Wilt Thou not revive us again: that Thy people may rejoice in Thee? Ps. 119:25. My soul cleaveth unto the dust: quicken Thou me according to Thy word. Ps. 119:37. Turn away mine eyes from beholding vanity; and quicken Thou me in Thy way. Jer. 4:3. Break up your fallow ground, and sow not among thorns. Hos. 10:12. Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in mercy; break up your fallow ground: for it is time to seek the Lord, till He come and rain righteousness upon you. Ezek. 34:26. And I will cause the shower to come down in his season; there shall be showers of blessing. Ezek. 36:37. Thus saith the Lord God; I will yet for this be inquired of by the house of Israel, to do it for them; I will increase them with men like a flock. Hab. 3:2. O Lord, revive Thy work in the midst of the years, in the midst of the years make known; in wrath remember mercy. Zech. 10:1. Ask ye of the Lord rain in the time of the latter rain. Mal. 3:10. Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse . . . and prove Me now herewith, saith the Lord of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there shall not be room enough to receive it. Rev. 2:4, 5. Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love. Remember therefore from whence thou art fallen, and repent, and do the first works. Rev. 3:2. Be watchful, and strengthen the things which remain, that are ready to die.

BSB2 Chronicles 7:14 and if My people who are called by My name humble themselves and pray and seek My face and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven, forgive their sin, and heal their land.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive …：then 引导的主句倒装（代下 7:14——条件在前、应许在后的经典句式）。
语法参：呼格 O + 称呼语 → 003 Turn us again, O God / O Lord, revive Thy work：呼格 O 两例，皆为祈求复兴的呼求。
语法quicken：使活过来、救活（古义）；fallow ground：荒地、休耕已久板结的地（耶 4:3、何 10:12 皆以『开垦荒地』喻心田的翻松）；the latter rain：春雨（巴勒斯坦收成前的末次雨）。

中复兴经文——为个人或教会属灵生命的更新而辑。历代志下 7:14 —— 这称为我名下的子民，若是自卑、祷告，寻求我的面，转离他们的恶行，我必从天上垂听，赦免他们的罪，医治他们的地。诗篇 80:3, 19 —— 神啊，求你使我们回转，使你的脸发光，我们便要得救。80:18 —— 求你救活我们，我们就要求告你的名。85:6 —— 你不再将我们救活，使你的百姓靠你欢喜吗？119:25 —— 我的性命几乎归于尘土；求你照你的话将我救活。119:37 —— 求你叫我转眼不看虚假，又叫我在你的道中生活。耶利米书 4:3 —— 要开垦你们的荒地，不要撒种在荆棘中。何西阿书 10:12 —— 你们要为自己栽种公义，就能收割慈爱；现今正是寻求耶和华的时候，你们要开垦荒地，等他临到，使公义如雨降在你们身上。以西结书 34:26 —— 我必按时降雨，必有福如甘霖而降。36:37 —— 主耶和华如此说：我要加增以色列家的人数多如羊群；他们必为这事向我求问，我要给他们成就。哈巴谷书 3:2 —— 耶和华啊，求你在这些年间复兴你的作为，在这些年间显明出来；在发怒的时候以怜悯为念。撒迦利亚书 10:1 —— 当春雨的时候，你们要向发闪电的耶和华求雨。玛拉基书 3:10 —— 万军之耶和华说：你们要将当纳的十分之一全然送入仓库……以此试试我，是否为你们敞开天上的窗户，倾福与你们，甚至无处可容。启示录 2:4-5 —— 然而有一件事我要责备你，就是你把起初的爱心离弃了。所以应当回想你是从哪里坠落的，并要悔改，行起初所行的事。3:2 —— 你要警醒，坚固那剩下将要衰微的。

EN[If the right kind of praying is a prelude to a genuine revival, the right kind of preaching is of vast moment also. The men who led in seasons of great spiritual quickening were not afraid to preach the exceeding sinfulness of sin and its just retribution, as well as the wondrous love of God in redemption. The thunders of Sinai and the loving invitations of Calvary were both made audible in their trenchant sermons. Such preaching made thorough work. The surfaces of men's hearts and consciences were not merely scratched over with cultured essays about Christianity; the Gospel plough was thrust down deep into the lower strata of human hearts, and into their nethermost convictions of divine truth; and when souls were converted, their eternal hopes were bottomed on the base-rock. Sinners were not only invited to come to Jesus, but were told why they should come and how they should come, and that unless they left their darling sins behind them the Saviour would not accept them. Bear in mind that it was this style of heart-piercing presentation of the Gospel by the apostle Peter which produced the glorious harvest of converts in Jerusalem. That was a typical revival: earnest praying and earnest preaching were attended by a powerful outpouring of the Holy Spirit. Why not now? God's word is our supreme authority; God's glorious Gospel is our weapon; God's promises are our guarantee. Up yonder hangs the waiting cloud of heavenly blessings. Shall we have them now? T. L. Cuyler.]

BSBActs 2:41 Those who embraced his message were baptized, and about three thousand were added to the believers that day.

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 Up yonder hangs the waiting cloud of heavenly blessings：地点状语前置引起全句倒装（主语在末，形象地把『云』推到句尾）。
语法参：省略结构（省略主语、系动词 be 等） → 002 unless they left their darling sins behind them：unless 条件从句（若不撇下所宠爱的罪）——虚拟过去式用于间接引语中。
语法prelude：前奏；of vast moment：关系重大（moment 古义『重要性』）；trenchant：锋利犀利的；strata：地层（stratum 复数）；nethermost：最底下的；bottomed on the base-rock：坐落在磐石根基上。

中〔正当的祷告若是真复兴的前奏，那么正当的讲道也同样关系重大。历次属灵大苏醒中的带领者，既不怕传讲神在救赎中奇妙的爱，也不怕传讲罪的极其可恶及其应得的报应。西奈山的雷声与各各他慈爱的邀请，在他们锋利的讲章中都被人听见。这样的讲道作的是彻底的工。人心与良心的表层，不只是被几篇有文化气息的基督教随笔轻轻刮擦一遍；福音的犁被深深推入人心底下的土层，推入他们对神圣真理最深处的确信；而当灵魂归正时，他们永恒的盼望是坐落在磐石根基之上的。罪人不只是被邀请到耶稣这里来，而且被告知为什么要来、当怎样来，并且被告知：他们若不把自己所宠爱的罪撇在身后，救主就不会接纳他们。要记得：正是使徒彼得这种刺透人心的福音陈述，在耶路撒冷带来了归信者的荣耀收成。那是一场典范式的复兴：恳切的祷告与恳切的讲道，伴随着圣灵大能的浇灌。今天为什么不能如此呢？神的话是我们至高的权柄；神荣耀的福音是我们的兵器；神的应许是我们的保证。天上福分的云彩正悬在那上头等候着。我们今天就要得着它们吗？柯以勒（T. L. Cuyler）〕
