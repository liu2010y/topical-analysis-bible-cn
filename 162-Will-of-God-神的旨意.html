神在护理与救赎中的旨意 / The Will of God in Providence and Redemption

ENWILL OF GOD. Scripture references. There is no definite reference to creation as such: "God said, Let there be light"; "God created"—these are the forms in which His will is revealed in creation. The will of God in providence: Dan. 4:35. And He doeth according to His will in the army of heaven, and among the inhabitants of the earth. Rom. 9:19. For who hath resisted His will? Rom. 15:32. That I may come unto you with joy by the will of God. James 4:15. For that ye ought to say, If the Lord will, we shall live, and do this, or that. 1 Pet. 3:17. For it is better, if the will of God be so, that ye suffer for well doing, than for evil doing. 1 Pet. 4:19. Wherefore let them that suffer according to the will of God commit the keeping of their souls to Him in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator. Lam. 3:33. For He doth not afflict willingly, nor grieve the children of men. 1 Cor. 12:11. Dividing to every man severally as He will. The will of God in man's redemption—in the sacrifice of Christ: Gal. 1:4. Who gave Himself for our sins, that He might deliver us from this present evil world, according to the will of God and our Father. Heb. 10:7. Then said I, Lo, I come (in the volume of the book it is written of Me,) to do Thy will, O God. In regeneration: John 1:13. Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God. In sanctification: 1 Thes. 4:3. For this is the will of God, even your sanctification. Heb. 10:10. By the which will we are sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.

BSB1 Thessalonians 4:3 For it is God’s will that you should be holy: You must abstain from sexual immorality;

语法参：Let + 第三人称祈使句 → 003 let them that suffer according to the will of God commit the keeping of their souls to Him：let ＋ them that 关系式的第三人称祈使（彼前 4:19）。
语法resisted His will：抗拒他的旨意；severally：各别地、分别地；willingly 在哀 3:33 中义为『甘心、出于本心』（神并非乐意使人受苦）。

中神的旨意。圣经的相关经文。关于创造，并没有直接用『旨意』一词：『神说：要有光』『神创造』——这些就是他的旨意在创造中显明出来的形式。神在护理中的旨意：但以理书 4:35 —— 他在天上的万军和世上的居民中，都凭自己的意旨行事。罗马书 9:19 —— 有谁抗拒他的旨意呢？15:32 —— 叫我顺着神的旨意，欢欢喜喜地到你们那里。雅各书 4:15 —— 你们只当说：主若愿意，我们就可以活着，也可以做这事或做那事。彼得前书 3:17 —— 神的旨意若是叫你们因行善受苦，总强如因行恶受苦。4:19 —— 所以那照神旨意受苦的人要一心为善，将自己灵魂交与那信实的造化之主。耶利米哀歌 3:33 —— 因他并不甘心使人受苦，使人忧愁。哥林多前书 12:11 —— 这一切都是这位圣灵所运行，随己意分给各人的。神在人之救赎中的旨意——在基督的献祭上：加拉太书 1:4 —— 基督照我们父神的旨意，为我们的罪舍己，要救我们脱离这罪恶的世代。希伯来书 10:7 —— 那时我说：神啊，我来了，为要照你的旨意行；我的事在经卷上已经记载了。在重生上：约翰福音 1:13 —— 这等人不是从血气生的，不是从情欲生的，也不是从人意生的，乃是从神生的。在成圣上：帖撒罗尼迦前书 4:3 —— 神的旨意就是要你们成为圣洁。希伯来书 10:10 —— 我们凭这旨意，靠耶稣基督只一次献上他的身体，就得以成圣。

ENIn adoption: Eph. 1:5. Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will, to the praise of the glory of His grace. In perfecting and assurance: Col. 4:12. That ye may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God. In resurrection to eternal life: John 6:39. And this is the Father's will which hath sent Me, that of all which He hath given Me I should lose nothing, but should raise it up again at the last day. All is summed up in salvation: 1 Tim. 2:4. Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth. John 6:40. And this is the will of Him that sent Me, that every one which seeth the Son, and believeth on Him, may have everlasting life. Eph. 1:9-11. Having made known unto us the mystery of His will . . . that in the dispensation of the fulness of times He might gather together in one all things in Christ; in whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of Him who worketh all things after the counsel of His own will. The will of God for man's redemption is withheld only by man's refusing and rejecting that will: John 5:40. And ye will not come to Me, that ye might have life. Matt. 23:37. How often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not! The will of God touching Christian living: 1 Thes. 5:14-18. Warn them that are unruly, comfort the feebleminded, support the weak, be patient toward all men. See that none render evil for evil unto any man; but ever follow that which is good . . . Rejoice evermore. Pray without ceasing. In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you. 1 Pet. 2:15. For so is the will of God, that with well doing ye may put to silence the ignorance of foolish men. 1 Pet. 4:2. That he no longer should live the rest of his time in the flesh to the lusts of men, but to the will of God.

BSBEphesians 1:11 In Him we were also chosen as God’s own, having been predestined according to the plan of Him who works out everything by the counsel of His will,

语法参：倒装（宾语/状语前置引起的主谓倒装） → 001 How often would I have gathered thy children together …, and ye would not!：感叹式的 how often 前置＋两个 would 对峙（我多次愿意／你们却不愿意）——太 23:37 全句的重量就在这一组对比上。
语法predestinated：预定（钦定本拼法，今作 predestined）；good pleasure of His will：他意旨所喜悦的；dispensation of the fulness of times：日期满足时的安排；feebleminded 在帖前 5:14 中古义为『灰心丧胆的』，非今日『智力低下』之义。

中在儿子的名分上：以弗所书 1:5 —— 又因爱我们，就按着自己意旨所喜悦的，预定我们藉着耶稣基督得儿子的名分，使他荣耀的恩典得着称赞。在得以坚立与笃定上：歌罗西书 4:12 —— 叫你们在神一切的旨意上得以完全，信心充足，能站立得稳。在复活得永生上：约翰福音 6:39 —— 差我来者的意思就是：他所赐给我的，叫我一个也不失落，在末日却叫他复活。这一切总归于救恩：提摩太前书 2:4 —— 他愿意万人得救，明白真道。约翰福音 6:40 —— 因为我父的意思是叫一切见子而信的人得永生。以弗所书 1:9-11 —— 都是照他自己所预定的美意，叫我们知道他旨意的奥秘……要照所安排的，在日期满足的时候，使天上、地上、一切所有的都在基督里面同归于一。我们也在他里面得了基业；这原是那位随己意行作万事的，照着他旨意所预定的。神救赎的旨意，只因人拒绝、弃绝这旨意而被拦阻：约翰福音 5:40 —— 然而你们不肯到我这里来得生命。马太福音 23:37 —— 我多次愿意聚集你的儿女，好像母鸡把小鸡聚集在翅膀底下，只是你们不愿意！关乎基督徒生活的神的旨意：帖撒罗尼迦前书 5:14-18 —— 要警戒不守规矩的人，勉励灰心的人，扶助软弱的人，也要向众人忍耐。你们要谨慎，无论是谁都不可以恶报恶；或是彼此相待，或是待众人，常要追求良善……要常常喜乐，不住地祷告，凡事谢恩；因为这是神在基督耶稣里向你们所定的旨意。彼得前书 2:15 —— 因为神的旨意原是要你们行善，可以堵住那糊涂无知人的口。4:2 —— 你们存这样的心，从此以后就可以不从人的情欲，只从神的旨意在世度余下的光阴。
