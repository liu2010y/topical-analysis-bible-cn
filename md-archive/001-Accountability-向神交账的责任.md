# ACCOUNTABILITY TO GOD ｜ 向神交账的责任

> 📖 出处：J. Glentworth Butler, *Topical Analysis of the Bible*（《圣经主题分析》），1897 年出版，公有领域。
>
> **体例说明**：正文中的罗马数字引用（如 XI. 268）指向作者另一部著作 *The Bible Work*（《圣经工作》）的卷号与页码，保留原样。原书为 OCR 扫描文本，明显的识别错误已修正。经文中文采用和合本（CUV）通行译法；原书引用经文时常为节选或意译，中文以和合本对应字句为准。

## 标题 / Title

**EN** — ACCOUNTABILITY TO GOD; Responsibility, Individual and Personal; Judgment according to Life-Work.

**中** — 向神交账的责任；个人性、切身的责任；照一生行为受审判。

---

## 正文对照 / Text

**EN** — The sense of accountability, the inward conscious conviction of personal responsibility, is an inherent product of the conscience or moral judgment, that exists and spontaneously acts in every man by nature.

**中** — 交账的意识，即内心对个人责任的自觉确信，是良心（道德判断力）与生俱来的产物；这种良心天然存在于每个人里面，并自发地运行。

**EN** — The recognition by the moral judgment of right and wrong, good and evil, is necessarily accompanied by the moral feeling consequent upon the willing or doing the right or the wrong.

**中** — 道德判断力对是非、善恶的辨识，必然伴随着一种道德感受——这感受随着人意愿或行出正事或恶事而产生。

**EN** — And, like the other self-recognized fact of dependence, which is produced and set in every man's conviction by known and felt helplessness, this conviction of responsibility implies of necessity a Being to whom the account must be rendered, or a rightful and supreme Moral Ruler and Judge.

**中** — 正如人因深知并切身感到自己的无助，而在心中确认"依赖"这一自明的事实一样，这种责任感也必然指向一位受账的存在者——一位名正言顺、至高无上的道德主宰与审判者。

**EN** — To this character the fact of dependence intimates the further relation of Creator, Upholder, and Provider. So much of God is assuredly disclosed by conscience and by actual living.

**中** — 在此之上，"依赖"这一事实进一步暗示出祂作为创造者、扶持者、供应者的关系。关于神的这些方面，确实已借着良心和实际的生活显明出来。

## 经文见证 / Scripture Testimony

- **EN** — Rom. 14:10, 12. We shall all stand before the judgment-seat of God. Each one of us shall give account of himself to God. XI. 268.
  **中** — 罗马书 14:10, 12 —— 我们都要站在神的台前……我们各人必要将自己的事在神面前说明。

- **EN** — 1 Pet. 4:5. Shall give account to Him that is ready to judge the quick and dead.
  **中** — 彼得前书 4:5 —— 他们必在那将要审判活人死人的主面前交账。

- **EN** — Matt. 12:36. Every idle word … shall give account in the day of judgment.
  **中** — 马太福音 12:36 —— 凡人所说的闲话，当审判的日子，必要句句供出来。

- **EN** — Heb. 13:17. As they that shall give account.
  **中** — 希伯来书 13:17 —— 好像那将来交账的人。

- **EN** — Eccles. 3:15. God requireth (seeketh again) the past. VI. 462.
  **中** — 传道书 3:15 —— 并且神使已过的事重新再来（原文直译：神必再寻回已过的事）。

- **EN** — Eccles. 11:9. VI. 511. Eccles. 12:14. VI. 528.
  **中** — 另参：传道书 11:9；12:14。

- **EN** — Matt. 25:15. To every man according to his ability.
  **中** — 马太福音 25:15 —— 按着各人的才干给他们银子。

- **EN** — Prov. 9:12. Wise for thyself; if thou scornest thou alone shalt bear it. VI. 293.
  **中** — 箴言 9:12 —— 你若有智慧，是与自己有益；你若亵慢，就必独自担当。

- **EN** — Job 34:11. The work of a man shall He render to him, and cause every man to find according to his way. VI. 179.
  **中** — 约伯记 34:11 —— 他必按人所做的报应人，使各人照所行的得报。

- **EN** — Ps. 62:12.
  **中** — 诗篇 62:12 —— 主啊，慈爱也是属乎你，因为你照着各人所行的报应他。

- **EN** — Rom. 2:5. Who will render to every man according to his deeds.
  **中** — 罗马书 2:5（-6）—— 他必照各人的行为报应各人。

- **EN** — 2 Cor. 5:10. We must be made manifest before the judgment-seat of Christ, that every one may receive according to that he hath done. XI. 362.
  **中** — 哥林多后书 5:10 —— 因为我们众人必要在基督台前显露出来，叫各人按着本身所行的，或善或恶受报。

- **EN** — Rev. 2:23. I will give unto each one of you according to your works.
  **中** — 启示录 2:23 —— 我要照你们的行为报应你们各人。

- **EN** — Rev. 22:12.
  **中** — 启示录 22:12 —— 看哪，我必快来！赏罚在我，要照各人所行的报应他。

- **EN** — Ezek. 18:20. The soul that sinneth, it shall die; the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him. IX. 81-86.
  **中** — 以西结书 18:20 —— 惟有犯罪的，他必死亡。义人的善果必归自己，恶人的恶报也必归自己。

**EN** — See Condemnation; Day of the Lord; Conscience.

**中** — 另参主题词条：定罪（Condemnation）；主的日子（Day of the Lord）；良心（Conscience）。
